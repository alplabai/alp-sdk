*v0.18.0-rc1 is a release candidate. v0.18.0 adds the Alif SE STOP backend on the E1M-AEN401 M55-HP, STOP wake from the RV-3028 EVI pin, and microSD with FatFs on the E1M-EVK. Pre-1.0 minor: two public macros added, nothing removed or changed.*

## Highlights

- **AEN low-power** — Alif SE STOP backend on the E4 M55-HP (E1M-AEN401), with a CMSDK watchdog node on the E4/E8 boards (#2876, #2877), and STOP wake from the carrier/CC3501E through the RV-3028 EVI pin, `ALP_POWER_WAKE_EXT_EVENT` (#2811).
- **microSD + FatFs** — E1M-EVK microSD on the E1M-AEN SoMs (E4 devicetree, `e1m_evk_microsd` shield, SDHC TCM bounce buffer), `<alp/storage.h>` `ALP_STORAGE_KIND_SD_MMC` over Zephyr `disk_access`, and `libraries: [fatfs]` in `board.yaml` selects ELM FatFs (#2856-#2859, #2860).
- **`alp.lock` platform key** — `alp.lock` records the pinned Alp Platform release (#3053).
- **Yocto** — sibling recipes track the SDK release with a per-machine package arch, the dev-kit opkg feed is edge-only with an allowlist packager, and a build without the feed fragment no longer aborts at parse (#2850, #2854, #2853).
- **Camera** — IMX296 gain now takes effect and the sensor runs at its full frame rate, and CSI-2 errors are counted per cause (#2881, #2880).
- **Provisioning** — checks are pinned to the bundle being provisioned, the OPTIGA Trust M probe tolerates its sleep NACK, and `BOOT_CONFIG` is documented at protocol 0.14.1 (#2893, #2904).
- **Build and metadata** — plain-CMake builds get the SoM's SoC capability profile (#2912), the CMake floor is 3.28 (#2995), and AEN OPTIGA, V2N GD32-owned pads and preset I2C addresses are corrected (#2958, #2959, #2990).

## Known issues

- **Body-control is not yet bench-tested on the Riverdi build.**

## Breaking

- None. Public ABI against v0.17.0: `ALP_POWER_WAKE_EXT_EVENT` (`<alp/power.h>`) and `RV3028C7_WAKE_EVF` (`<alp/chips/rv3028c7.h>`) added, plus the version macros; snapshot `docs/abi/v0.18-snapshot.json`.
