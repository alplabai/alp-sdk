Apply a power-management policy hint to the CC3501E (POWER_POLICY, 0x62).

Hints how aggressively the CC3501E firmware idles between events. The coarse preset maps to the CC35xx Power manager: PERFORMANCE keeps the device awake (WFI only), BALANCED lets it opportunistically sleep between events, and LOW_POWER / DEEP_SLEEP let it reach its deepest sleep state. Takes effect on the device's next idle-detection cycle.

Parameters
ctxInitialised bridge handle.
policyPower-policy payload: policy is one of alp_cc3501e_pp_preset_t; wake_events is a bitmap of ALP_CC3501E_WAKE_* (all-zero is rejected with a low-power preset – keep at least ALP_CC3501E_WAKE_HOST_SPI); idle_ms_before_sleep is the minimum idle before sleep (0 = firmware default). reserved is sent as zero.
timeout_msCaller budget.
Returns
ALP_OK on success; ALP_ERR_INVAL if policy is NULL, the preset is unknown, or a low-power preset carries no wake source; otherwise the mapped error.
