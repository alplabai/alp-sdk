Detailed Description

ADI (Maxim) MAX9295A + MAX9296A GMSL2 camera SerDes.

6 Gbps Gigabit Multimedia Serial Link 2 over coax. Same overall shape as TI's FPD-Link III pair (ti_ds90ub953_954) but at 6 Gbps with native automotive-grade qualification. Dominant SerDes in ADAS production today.

ABI status: [ABI-EXPERIMENTAL]
Verification status: [UNTESTED] — driver compiles + passes NULL-arg smokes;
no HiL silicon bring-up yet. Treat all numbers + lifecycle sequencing as paper-correct only until the v1.0 verification sweep lands.
Driver status: [stub-impl] — chip-ID + soft reset only.

Datasheets: MAX9295A (Rev 5), MAX9296A (Rev 5).
