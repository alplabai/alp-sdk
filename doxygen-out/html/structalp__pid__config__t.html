◆ deriv_on_measurement

bool alp_pid_config_t::deriv_on_measurement

When true, the derivative acts on -measurement (avoids the derivative kick from a setpoint step); when false, on the error.

Steady-state behaviour is identical.

◆ integ_limit

float alp_pid_config_t::integ_limit

Anti-windup limit on the integrator accumulator (in error*time units).

|integrator| is clamped to this; pass <= 0 to auto-select half the output span ((out_max - out_min) / 2).
