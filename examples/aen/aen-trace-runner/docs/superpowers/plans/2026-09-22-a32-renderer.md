> **Status (2026-10-08): Landed** — the A32 renderer shipped in `examples/aen/aen-trace-runner/a32/renderer/` (#2452).

Status: plan; supersedes sections 3–5 of `2026-09-22-real-3d-renderer.md`. Sections 1–2 of that plan (game design, DL pipeline, 28.4 fixed raster, host-golden discipline) stand. (M) = measured in `docs/2026-09-22-measurements.md`; (E) = estimate to be replaced at the named checkpoint; (?) = unknown, resolved by T-A0.

Maintainer decision: the renderer targets the two Cortex-A32 cores directly. HP-first is dropped.

## 3. Core roles (decided)

| Core | Runtime | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| M55-HE @160 MHz | Zephyr (today's image, `alp-sdk-lcd`) | DSI/D-PHY/HX8394 panel init (never `BBh`/`C3h`), CDC200 flip via `cdc200_swap_fb` + line-1296 ISR (M), IMU, attract mode, `tr_game_step`, mailbox publish/consume, watchdog on the A32; later camera capture + `tr_detect_frame`, Ethos-U85 dispatch |
| A32_0 @ (?) MHz | bare metal, NS SVC under TF-A sp_min | stub (loader/park), mailbox consume, `tr_scene_step/build` -> DL + band bins, band raster, HUD/banner blits, publish done |
| A32_1 | bare metal, PSCI CPU_ON from the stub | band raster only (shares DL/textures through L2) |
| M55-HP | Alif `m55_stub_hp.bin` (parked) | nothing now; NPU/vision later |

Why HE, not HP, for the M55 role: HE is the only M55 that RAM-runs over the debugger on 2026W36-0009 (HP `examination failed`, M), and the residual M55 work (game step ~0.2 ms, 1 ms mailbox poll, IMU I2C, later detect on a 64x40 grid) fits 160 MHz.

Why the A32s never touch the CDC200: (i) NS access to `0x49031134`/`0x49031024` from the A32 is unknown; (ii) CDC200 line IRQ routing to the A32 GIC (`GICD 0x1C010000`) is unknown and the bare-metal image has no GIC bring-up; (iii) the M55 driver's ISR calls `cdc200_shadow_reload_control()` every line IRQ and keeps `curr_fb/next_fb` bookkeeping — a second master on the same shadow registers is a race. The M55 path is the one measured alternating `0x02000000`/`0x02200000` at 40 Hz (M).

Coherency — stated once:
- **Framebuffers (SRAM0):** A32 maps them Normal **non-cacheable** (NC NEON fill 1859.49 vs WB 1966.65 + 0.41 ms clean, M). M55 dcache off (`CONFIG_DCACHE=n`, pinned). **M55 writes no pixels** (HUD moves to the A32). CDC200 reads memory. So no clean/invalidate ever on a framebuffer; a `dsb sy` before publishing `out_seq` is the only ordering step.
- **Mailbox page (SRAM1 `0x02401000`):** A32 NC; M55 uncached. Publish = payload, `DSB`, seq. Consume = seq, `DMB`, payload, re-read seq.
- **DL, band bins, z bands, textures, atlas, stacks (SRAM1 WB):** A32-only. Section descriptors MUST set **S=1** (Inner Shareable) on every WB section — the probe's `ATTR_WB 0x1C1E` has S=0 (fine for one core, wrong for two). TF-A sets `CPUECTLR.SMPEN`, so S=1 is hardware-coherent across the pair. `dmb ish` around the per-frame go word.

Failure isolation: A32 fault -> vectors record `fault_*` in the mailbox, `stub_state=FAULT`, park in the stub loop. No `out_seq` for 100 ms -> M55 freezes the last frame and logs once. **As built (2026-09-24, `src/ipc/tr_wd.h`, release flow `TR_M55_AUTOLAUNCH`):** nothing is commanded until the stub is alive (mailbox magic); M55 boot waits for it (bounded), adopts a RUNNING renderer whose `ctrl_entry/len/crc` are the HE's image (else HALT then LAUNCH), LAUNCHes one still PARKED after a 500 ms settle. A first frame after boot/LAUNCH gets 2 s (doubling per failed LAUNCH, capped at 32 s); after frames landed a 100 ms miss is a stall -> HALT; a HALT is never repeated while the stub parks, and a HALT-parked stub is LAUNCHed at the next poll; no command budget (backoff resets after 60 s of frames); every command logged with its reason. Wedged A32 (HALT never consumed, or stub core 0 double fault): the HE logs the pending time every second (`tr_a32_pending_ms`); `SERVICE_BOOT_RESET_CPU` with `HOST_CPU_0 = 0` (`hal_alif-ref/se_services/include/services_lib_api.h:248`) from the M55 is the candidate — unproven (T-A0 item 8); until then, power cycle.

MRAM: `0x80002000` bl32 (BOOTLOAD, A32_0, boot); `0x80020000` A32_APP = stub (+ embedded renderer payload in release); `HP_APP`/`HE_APP` as `loadAddress` entries only (#1981). Two ATOC configs derived from `e1m-aen-evk-a32-probe.json`: **dev** = stub + `m55_stub_he.bin`/`m55_stub_hp.bin`; **release** = stub-with-payload + trace-runner HE `.bin` at `loadAddress 0x58000000`, `["load","boot"]` (<= 256 KiB ITCM).

Z and colour bands live in SRAM0 MiB 2 (above), not the renderer `.bss`: the 512 KiB image budget is code + atlas (~92 KiB) + up to 8 textures (256 KiB), which leaves no room for 180 KiB of bands. SRAM0 MiB 2 total: 630 KiB setup + 180 KiB bands + 160 KiB zone ground (P15 + the far road) = 970 KiB of 1 MiB. The P15 zone textures ship in the image as 4 bpp indices (10 x 8 KiB; every zone texture is <= 16 colours, `tools/genzone.py` asserts it) and are unpacked into `TR_MEM_A32_ZIDX` when a slot's zone changes, which is what keeps P15 + P16 inside the 512 KiB image budget. Both A32 cores run on the renderer table (core 1 switches after core 0's gate): the stub table maps `0x022` NC and `0x026` WB, the opposite of what the renderer needs.

## 5. A32 platform: stub, dual core, dev loop

**Stub (`a32/stub/`, replaces `a32/probe/` as A32_APP; reuses probe `start.S` for vectors, DCCISW loop, table build, NEON enable).**
1. Reset at `0x80020000`: VBAR, SP, then **copy itself to `0x0240C000` and jump** — the core never executes from MRAM once parked, so a Flow D write cannot tear under it.
2. Stub table (SRAM1 MiB 0 NC-X, SRAM0 NC, MRAM WB read-only, else Device), MMU+caches+NEON on.
3. Init mailbox: `magic`, `version`, `stub_state=PARKED`; do NOT clear `ctrl_entry`.
4. PSCI CPU_ON: `r0=0x84000003, r1=1 (MPIDR Aff0=1), r2=stub_core1_entry, r3=0` (TF-A `ensemble_pwr_domain_on()` sets `HOST_CPU_BOOT_MSK|=1<<1`, `HOST_CPU_WAKEUP|=1<<1` at `0x1A010000`). Core 1: SP, VBAR, same TTBR0, MMU+NEON on, `stub_core1_state=PARKED`, spin on its go word. Park loops poll (no GIC, so no WFI wake) with heartbeats.
5. Core-0 park loop polls `ctrl_cmd`. **LAUNCH**: CRC32 over `[ctrl_entry, +ctrl_len)` == `ctrl_crc` else `FAULT/BAD_CRC`; then DCCISW all levels, `ICIALLU`, `BPIALL`, `DSB`, `ISB`; `stub_state=RUNNING`; core-1 go (it does the same maintenance and jumps with `r0=1`); jump `ctrl_entry(r0=0, r1=&mbox)`.
6. Release build: stub header `{payload_len, payload_crc}`; if non-zero, copy payload MRAM -> `0x02500000` and self-LAUNCH.

**Renderer image (`a32/renderer/`, flat .bin at `0x02500000`).** Own vectors (fault -> mailbox, FAULT, back to stub park), own table at `0x02408000` (FBs NC, MiB 1–2 WB S=1, MiB 0 NC, MiB 3 unmapped), `.data` copied and `.bss` zeroed at every entry, per-core stacks, newlib-nano libm (`lroundf` only -- sin/cos are the in-repo `tr_sincosf`). **Every A32 (and M55) build of the renderer sources passes `-ffp-contract=off`** (host `runner.sh` does): a fused multiply-add changes front-end bits vs the host goldens. **HALT** (both cores poll `ctrl_cmd` once per frame): core 1 finishes its band, DCCISW, to stub core-1 park; core 0 waits `PARKED`, DCCISW, `ICIALLU`, TTBR0 -> stub table, `stub_state=PARKED`, to stub park.

**Dev loop per iteration (no MRAM write):**
1. `make` in `a32/renderer/` -> `renderer.bin` + CRC.
2. J-Link on the AP that read the probe results (HP AP `0x00200000` worked; AXI-AP alternative): write `renderer.bin` to `0x02500000`, then `ctrl_entry=0x02500000`, `ctrl_len`, `ctrl_crc`, `ctrl_cmd=1`. No core halt.
3. Read `stub_state`, `out_seq`, `out_ticks*`, heartbeats over the same AP; M55 RAM console as today.
4. Iterate: `ctrl_cmd=2`, wait `stub_state==0`, back to 2.

M55 side: Flow C RAM-run the HE image as today. Order per session: cold cycle -> A32 chain parks -> Flow C HE -> HE writes mailbox identity -> LAUNCH. T-A0 item 6 checks whether the HE Flow-C reset leaves the A32 stub running; if not, Flow C first then LAUNCH.

One-time MRAM writes (authorised Flow D) only when the stub changes or for release, and only while the resident A32 code is parked in SRAM. **Maintainer decision (2026-09-22):** the dev ATOC stays resident on 2026W36-0009 for the project's duration — no per-session slot0 restore. Canonical person_detect (md5 5839e003d5d069f6fd912eb22774d037) is restored when the project ends. Every MRAM write still needs a held reservation, the A32 parked in SRAM, sector padding and a cold-cycle read-back.

Alternative dev loop to try once (zero firmware): J-Link `AE822FA0E5597LS0_A32_0` halt / `loadbin` / `SetPC` / `go`. If it works it is the fallback for a wedged renderer.

## 6. Inter-core protocol

Shared header `src/ipc/tr_mbox.h`, compiled into the M55 image, the A32 renderer, the stub and host tests. Fixed-width integers only (no `enum`, no `bool` — `tr_game_t` is copied field-by-field), `_Static_assert` on every size/offset, 64-byte separation between writer-owned blocks.

```c
#define TR_MBOX_ADDR    0x02401000u
#define TR_MBOX_MAGIC   0x54524D42u   /* 'TRMB' */
#define TR_MBOX_VERSION 1u
#define TR_FB_A         0x02000000u
#define TR_FB_B         0x02600000u   /* as built: SRAM1, see sec 4 (was lcd_fb 0x02200000) */

typedef struct { uint8_t kind, lane, low, pad; int16_t y, pad2; } tr_pkt_ent_t;   /* 8 B; kind 0 free 1 obstacle 2 pickup */

typedef struct {                         /* 152 B (16 x 8 B entities + 24 B header) */
	uint32_t     tick, score, rng;
	uint32_t     flags;                  /* bit0 alive, bit1 airborne, bit2 ducking, bit3 attract_active, bit4 paused */
	uint8_t      lane, air_ticks, duck_ticks, banner;   /* banner 0 none 1 stand 2 step_back 3 attract 4 game_over 5 check_camera */
	int16_t      track_h, pad;
	tr_pkt_ent_t ents[16];
} tr_frame_in_t;

typedef struct {
	/* +0x000 identity, M55 writes once per boot */
	uint32_t magic, version, m55_boot_count, pad0[13];
	/* +0x040 M55 -> A32 */
	uint32_t in_seq;                     /* incremented AFTER in_* + in_fb are written and DSB'd */
	uint32_t in_fb;                      /* TR_FB_A or TR_FB_B: draw here */
	uint32_t pad1[14];
	tr_frame_in_t in;                    /* +0x080 */
	uint8_t  pad2[0x100 - 0x80 - sizeof(tr_frame_in_t)];
	/* +0x100 A32 -> M55 */
	uint32_t out_seq;                    /* == in_seq of the frame now complete in out_fb */
	uint32_t out_fb;
	uint32_t out_ticks0, out_ticks1;     /* CNTVCT ticks each core spent on this frame */
	uint32_t out_tris, out_dropped, out_frames, out_heartbeat, pad3[8];
	/* +0x140 stub control */
	uint32_t ctrl_cmd;                   /* 0 none 1 LAUNCH 2 HALT (consumer clears) */
	uint32_t ctrl_entry, ctrl_len, ctrl_crc;
	uint32_t stub_state;                 /* 0 PARKED 1 RUNNING 2 FAULT */
	uint32_t stub_core1_state;           /* 0 never started 1 PARKED 2 RUNNING */
	uint32_t stub_heartbeat0, stub_heartbeat1, pad4[8];
	/* +0x180 fault record */
	uint32_t fault_core, fault_code, dfsr, dfar, ifsr, ifar, lr, pad5[9];
} tr_mbox_t;
_Static_assert(sizeof(tr_mbox_t) == 0x200, "");
/* As implemented (src/ipc/tr_mbox.h, T-A1): the 152 B `in` block needs 192 B, so the
 * blocks sit at +0x040 +0x080 +0x140 (out) +0x180 (ctrl) +0x1C0 (fault); total 0x200.
 * The layout above is the original sketch -- the header is authoritative. */
```

A32-internal words (renderer `.bss`, WB S=1): `frame_go`, `band_next` (LDREX/STREX), `core1_done`. Exclusives only on Shareable Normal memory — never in the NC page.

**Frame pipeline** (double buffer):
M55 tick (paced by the flip's vblank; `TICK_MS` deleted): (1) input + `tr_game_step`; (2) wait `out_seq == in_seq` (1 ms poll; 100 ms timeout -> watchdog); (3) `cdc200_swap_fb(out_fb)`, wait landing, `tr_flip_count++`; (4) fill `in`, `in_fb = free buffer`, `DSB`, `in_seq++`, `DSB`.
A32 core 0: spin on `in_seq`; copy `in` with seq re-read; scene step/build -> DL + band bins; `dmb ish`; `band_next=0`; `frame_go=seq`. Both cores: `while ((b = atomic_inc(&band_next)) < 40) raster_band(b)`. Core 1: `core1_done=seq`. Core 0: wait `core1_done`; `dsb sy`; `out_fb=in_fb`; `out_ticks*`; `dmb`; `out_seq=seq`.
At most one frame in flight; the A32 is never handed the scanned-out buffer. Over 25.0 ms the flip slips to 20 Hz and game logic follows the flip rate. `tr_frame_overrun_count` is the bench signal.

## 7. What the A32 budget affords

Assumption: A32 clock 800 MHz (`e8.json`) — **not measured on this unit**; T-A0 measures PMCCNTR over exactly 10,000,000 CNTVCT ticks (100.0 ms), cross-checked with a dependent-add loop. Every (E) scales 1/f.

Design: **banded z-buffer, 1/z interpolated**, Gouraud per vertex, RGB565 textures; painter's order no longer required.
- z band + colour band per core: 2 x 720 x 32 x uint16 = 92,160 B, SRAM1 WB S=1 (sec 4), rewritten per band; all z-tested read-modify-write hits these, the NC framebuffer is only written (one full-row NEON copy per finished band -- uncached SRAM0 reads are the measured bus limit). `w16 = 65535 * TR_CAM_Z_NEAR / z`, nearer = larger. `ponytail:` 16-bit first; uint32 if far z-fighting shows on glass.
- Bins: 40 bands x <=1024 tri indices (core 0 from each tri's y range); overflow -> `out_dropped`.
- Textures: RGB565 128x128 (32 KiB), <=8 in the image, L2-hot; affine per 8-px sub-span, one divide per sub-span.
- HUD/banners: existing 4bpp atlas compiled into the A32 image; blit NC into the FB after the bands.
- Budget: `TR_DL_MAX_TRIS 4096`, `TR_DL_MAX_VERTS 3072`.

Per frame (E), two cores @800 MHz:

| stage | basis | ms |
|---|---|---|
| snapshot + scene + xform/light/clip 4k tris + bins (core 0) | ~150 cyc/tri | 0.8 |
| background rows into the cached bands | 1.84 MB cache-resident | 0.3 |
| z clears | 1.84 MB cache-resident | 0.3 |
| z-tested flat raster, 921,600 px + 25% overdraw | ~4 cyc/px NEON | 2.9 |
| Gouraud | +1.5 cyc/px | 1.1 |
| textures on ~50% px | +6 cyc/px | 1.7 |
| band -> FB copy (write-only NC, full rows) | 1,843,200 B / 1859.49 MB/s (M) | 1.0 |
| HUD + publish | | 0.2 |
| **total** | | **~8 typ, <=12 worst** (single core ~15; dual at 400 MHz ~16) |

Hard cap: `out_ticks` <= 18 ms mean at the target budget.

## 8. Implementation plan

Carries over unchanged from feat/r3d-core: `r3d_math.c` (camera, projection, near clip, guard band, Lambert+fog, emit) as the A32 front-end; `r3d_raster.c` edge DDA + top-left rule (extended); `r3d.h` conventions; all host tests. Changes: `tr_tri_t` gains per-vertex `w/u/v/rgb`; `tr_raster_tri` gains `(y_lo, y_hi, zband)`; `span.h` gains a NEON branch; `tr_r3d_draw` becomes `raster_band`.

**T-A0 — probe session (one authorised Flow D of an extended `a32_probe.bin`, slot restored after).** (1) PMCCNTR-vs-CNTVCT clock; (2) NS read of CDC200 `0x49031134` + line-position register twice; (3) PSCI CPU_ON core 1 to an SRAM1 heartbeat loop — SMC return, core-1 MPIDR/heartbeat; (4) LDREX/STREX counter 1,000,000 per core on a WB **S=1** word — expect exactly 2,000,000; (5) core 0 writes 64 KiB WB S=1, core 1 checksums it; (6) after park, Flow C the HE game — does the A32 heartbeat keep advancing? can HE read `0x02401000`? can the debug AP read/write `0x02500000`?; (7) J-Link A32 profile halt/resume; (8) `se_service_boot_reset_cpu(HOST_CPU_0=0, 0x80002000)` from HE. **Pass:** 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 green; 2, 7, 8 informative.

**T-A1 — `src/ipc/tr_mbox.h` + host test.** Struct, offsets, `tr_mbox_publish()`/`tr_mbox_take()`/`tr_frame_in_from_game()`; round-trip, tearing re-read, static asserts with and without `-fshort-enums`.

**T-A2 — stub.** Relocation, tables, mailbox init, CPU_ON, park loops, LAUNCH (CRC, DCCISW/ICIALLU), release payload mode, fault record; `mkpayload.py`, `decode.py`. The stub writes **FPSCR = 0** at entry on both cores (round-to-nearest, FZ=0, DN=0 -- IEEE defaults, what the host tests assume) before any float code runs. **CP-A2:** both heartbeats advance; LAUNCH a tiny payload that NC-fills FB A with a colour bar; HALT/LAUNCH repeatable; HE display-only image showing it on glass. First A32 pixels.

**T-A3 — renderer core rework (host).** Attributes, `raster_band()` with z band, 1/z, Gouraud, textured spans, bins, NEON `span.h` branch (8-px `vst1q_u16`, overlapping tail store for n>=8, scalar n<8), HUD blit. Tests: z-order independent of emission order; band seams (banded CRC == unbanded); Gouraud endpoints exact; textured quad golden; NEON span == scalar (boot self-check on A32); full-frame golden PPM.

**T-A4 — M55 image.** `display.c` full-repaint contract without pixel writes; `tr_display_flip(fb)`; watchdog. `main.c` mailbox loop, `TICK_MS` deleted, identity + HALT/LAUNCH at boot. Remove sprite render paths and atlas from the M55 image. `CONFIG_DCACHE=n`. **CP-A4:** stub payload echoes `out_seq=in_seq` after filling `in_fb` with a tick-keyed colour; expect 40.0 flips/s, overrun 0.

## 9. Tradeoffs considered

- A32 owns the flip: three unknowns + a two-master shadow-register race to save a 1 ms poll. Loses.
- DL built on the M55, A32 raster only: 57–256 KiB per frame across the boundary vs a 152-byte snapshot. Loses.
- MHU (`apss-mhu`) instead of polling: never driven, 40 Hz handshake. Deferred (worth it when the A32s should sleep).
- WB framebuffer + clean vs NC: NC is as fast for a write stream and removes the cross-architecture cache hazard. NC wins.
- Full 1.8 MB z-buffer in SRAM1: crowds TF-A's MiB; band z is cache-resident. Band wins.
- Static halves vs atomic band counter: counter balances for one LDREX/STREX per band.
- HP as the M55: faster, but not RAM-runnable on 2026W36-0009 and not needed.
- Per-iteration MRAM image: unsafe while an A32 runs from MRAM, and slow. Rejected.

## 10. Confidence and gaps

Confident: framebuffer coherency (M); shared-memory handshake shape; TF-A boot contract and PSCI mechanism (source-read); #1981 ATOC shape; SRAM1 powered in this chain (M); MHU0 window init-only use.

Not confident until T-A0: A32 clock; PSCI CPU_ON on this TF-A build; exclusives and cross-L1 coherency across the pair; SRAM1 reachable from HE and the debug AP with the chain resident; HE Flow-C reset scope; SE-mediated A32 reset; whether the SE `load`s a ~225 KB HE image at `0x58000000` cleanly (check before T-A9).

Out of scope: landscape mount, camera `ALP_ERR_IO`, NPU detection.
