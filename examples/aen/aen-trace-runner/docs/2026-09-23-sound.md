## Synth

- **16 kHz mono**, output as identical L/R so U27 (left, J14) and U28 (right, J21) both play.
  16 kHz is the rate the I2S3 -> TAS2563 path was heard at on the reworked EVK
  (aen-i2s-tas2563-probe PROBE_LISTEN/PROBE_MELODY, 2026-09-15). `TR_AUDIO_RATE` can be
  changed at compile time. It must be a multiple of 20; the one-pole filter coefficients are
  per sample, so a higher rate gives a darker sound.
- 18 voices: 6 music voices (bass, arp, lead, kick, snare, hat), plus a pool of 12 for SFX that
  steals the oldest voice when full. Each voice has a square (variable duty), saw, triangle,
  parabolic sine or noise source, a linear pitch slide, a one-pole LP/HP filter with a
  coefficient slide, a Q24 ADSR and a start delay, so one event can schedule a whole phrase.
- Music: synthwave at 100 BPM, Am-F-C-G. 16 bars = **38.4 s** loop. Bars 1-4 are bass,
  arpeggio and hats. Kick and snare come in at bar 5, the lead at bar 9, hat sixteenths and the
  arpeggio octave-up at bar 13, and bar 16 ends on a snare fill. Levels are `TR_MUSIC_PLAY`
  full, `TR_MUSIC_ATTRACT` 55%, and `TR_MUSIC_OFF` fades out and stops the sequencer. A crash
  ducks the music to 35% for 1.2 s, and game over ducks it for 2.0 s.
- SFX, measured by `test_audio` (length until the last voice ends, peak in S16):

| event | sound | length | peak |
|---|---|---|---|
| FOOTSTEP | short HP noise tick + 95 Hz thud; subtle | 48 ms | 2858 |
| PICKUP (param = combo) | two-note sine chime (E6, +5th). Rises a semitone per combo step (up to 12); combo >= 2 adds the octave | 304-368 ms | 6811-8101 |
| JUMP | noise with an opening LP sweep + 260->620 Hz blip | 288 ms | 4056 |
| DUCK | noise with a closing LP sweep + 520->180 Hz triangle | 256 ms | 4931 |
| CRASH (1 low / 2 high / 3 wire) | 110->34 Hz sine thump, 70 Hz square body, falling noise burst, 6 debris ticks. Wire adds a 1400->90 Hz zap + crackle | 736 ms | 19137 / 15770 |
| WIRE (param = intensity) | 100 Hz saw hum + 150 Hz square + random HP crackle bursts; P4b re-sends it while a wire is near | 448 ms | 4218 |
| ATTRACT | C5-E5-G5-C6-E6 square arpeggio + triangle pad | 1168 ms | 9783 |
| GAME_OVER | falling A4-G#4-G4-F#4 saw + A3 triangle | 2000 ms | 6229 |

- Headroom: the music loop alone peaks at 21732 (-3.6 dBFS, the test's bound is -3 dBFS).
  With every effect fired at once, twice, over play-level music, the peak is 12348 and nothing
  clips.
- Determinism: a scripted run (attract, every event) renders to CRC32 `0xDBF70C58` on the
  host **and** on the A32 under qemu (`runner.sh` cross stage), and the M55 compile smoke covers
  the same sources.
- Previews: `cc -std=c11 -O2 -o /tmp/audio_preview tools/audio_preview.c src/audio/tr_audio.c
  && /tmp/audio_preview OUT && python3 tools/audio_spectro.py OUT/*.wav`. This writes
  `music.wav`, one `sfx_*.wav` per effect, and `game.wav` (a 22.5 s run: attract, steps, jumps,
  pickup streak, wire, crash, game over), plus a spectrogram PNG for each.

## Event ring (`src/ipc/tr_aring.h`)

- Address **SRAM0 `0x0237F000`**, 0x1C0 B. It sits in the top 4 KiB of the renderer's documented
  SRAM0 spare (`0x02335000..0x0237FFFF`, [a32-renderer plan § 4](superpowers/plans/2026-09-22-a32-renderer.md)),
  just below the TF-A MHU0 window at `0x02380000`. It is clear of FB A, the renderer
  setup/bands/DL, SRAM1 (mailbox, stub, renderer, FB B) and the camera pool, and a
  `_Static_assert` pins it inside the spare.
- SRAM0 is powered from reset, unlike SRAM1, so the HP can run before the A32 chain.
- The A32 build only. The `TR_RENDER=M55` build scans the shield's `lcd_fb`
  (`0x02200000..0x023C1FFF`), which covers this address.
- Protocol: one producer and one consumer, with free-running 32-bit indices. The HE writes only
  `head`, the HP writes only `tail` and the status words, so no cross-master atomics are needed.
  A slot is written, then a barrier, then `head` is bumped. 64 events of 4 B each; a push into a
  full ring counts in `dropped`. If `head - tail > 64`, the HP resyncs.
- The HE's `tr_aring_init()` keeps a live ring's indices (HP running, HE rebooted) and drops
  unread events. Anything else is zeroed and the magic `'TRAU'` is written last.
- The HP status block, readable over SWD from any AP:

| offset | field | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| +0x00 | magic | `0x54524155` once the HE is up |
| +0x40 / +0x44 | head / dropped | HE side |
| +0x80 | tail | HP side |
| +0x84 | hp_state | 1 running, 2 fault |
| +0x88 | hp_heartbeat | +1 per 16 ms block |
| +0x8C | hp_underruns | I2S writes that failed or timed out |
| +0x90 | hp_fault_step | which bring-up step failed (see below) |
| +0x94 | hp_events | events consumed |

- The HE side (`src/main.c`, `sfx_frame()` after every presented frame) sends:
  - MUSIC and the attract jingle on every attract on/off edge;
  - JUMP and DUCK on their rising edges;
  - PICKUP when the score rises by 10, with a combo if the pickup lands within 90 ticks of the
    last one;
  - FOOTSTEP every 8 ticks on the ground;
  - CRASH on the fatal tick (LOW/HIGH from the obstacle hit);
  - GAME_OVER once the crash sequence has played out (not in attract).

  WIRE is P4b's to send. With no HP running the ring fills and pushes just count as dropped;
  the HE never waits.

## `sound/` firmware

The same source serves two roles:

- **GAME** (default): the M55-HP game image, which drains the ring.
- **TEST**: the standalone bench test (music, scripted SFX, a 1 kHz reference tone, and a
  PDM loopback capture with an in-band verdict).

**Build gate:** CMake refuses to configure without `-DTR_SND_REWORKED_U46=ON`. Enabling
`i2s3` drives P9_3/4/5, and on a stock 74LVC157 U46 that contends with U46's outputs
(alp-sdk `examples/aen/aen-i2s-amp-alif/README.md`, SAFETY). Only the 2026W36-0002 carrier
is known reworked. Its U46 is a 3257-type switch with VCC moved to +3V3.

Bring-up steps (the step number is `hp_fault_step`, and the `[snd] N` console prefix):
1. CC3501E bridge
2. I2S_SELECT (E1M IO13) = 0. **Never 1**: on the reworked carrier, S=1 routes the M.2 card's
   3.3 V I2S onto a 1.8 V pad.
3. I2S_EN (IO8) = 0
4. SD_N (P5_2) held low for 24 ms, then released high
5. carrier I2C bus 0
6. `tas2563_init` on 0x4D and 0x4E
7. AMP_LEVEL MIN + `configure_i2s` (U27 LEFT, U28 RIGHT)
8. PDM mics (TEST only, 48 kHz stereo)
9. I2S3 open, volume `TR_SND_VOLUME` (default and cap: 128, the level heard "louder and clean"
   on this unit), start
10. 8 silent blocks, which must all be consumed in 60..250 ms. This proves the bit clock
    runs: a queued write proves nothing (see "First bench run" below).
11. `tas2563_resume` on both amps

After that the stream never stops (#2146: an idle bit clock shuts the amps down). On exit, the
amps are shut down first. SD_N is released as an input, not driven low (#2164), and /E is set
high; S stays 0.

The DesignWare I2S driver is FIFO/interrupt-driven. There is no DMA path, so the double buffer
is the audio_out slab: one block clocks out while the next renders.

### First bench run (2026-09-23): nothing clocked, and the verdict lied

- **Result.** Image md5 `67888d95...`, built against `$ZEPHYR_BASE`.
  - Steps 1-11 all returned 0.
  - Then 1455/1455 I2S writes and 205/205 mic reads failed.
  - The run took 187722 ms instead of 23280, and the capture was all zeros.
  - The image still printed `RESULT PASS`, because `0 >= 100 * 0`.
- **Verdict fix.** `sound/src/snd_verdict.h` is shared with `tools/audio_spectro.py` and
  host-tested by `tests/host/test_snd_verdict.c`. It fails on any of:
  - more than 2 write or mic-read failures;
  - an incomplete capture;
  - playback more than 10% slower than real time;
  - no mic with nonzero silence noise;
  - a 1 kHz bin under 25 (amplitude 10 LSB);
  - a music RMS under 5 LSB.
- **Root cause (measured from the images, not yet on silicon).** `$ZEPHYR_BASE`
  does not carry alp-sdk's clockctrl patch
  (`zephyr/patches/zephyr/0001-clock_control_alif-master-source-expmst-i2s-setrate.patch`).
  - That patch is what enables the CGU 76.8 MHz audio source I2S3 and the PDM both run from.
  - `i2s_dw.c` tolerates the missing `set_rate` silently: the first write queues, and nothing
    ever clocks out.
  - The failed image has no `alif_clock_control_set_rate` symbol.
  - The build that was heard on 2026-09-15 used `ZEPHYR_BASE=$ZEPHYR_BASE`, which is
    patched (the probe's `CMakeCache.txt`; `aen-i2s-tas2563-probe` is not in this tree).
  - `sound/CMakeLists.txt` now refuses an unpatched `ZEPHYR_BASE`.
  - The game HE image does not need that clock, so it keeps building against zephyrproject.

If C1 is PASS and T still fails at step 10, the clock is not the whole story. Go through the
ranked list in the P10 report. PDM and I2S3 run together in C3 (proven 2026-09-15), so T keeps
them in one image.

**What proves playback.** The capture has three windows. W0 is 0.5 s of silence (the stream
running, zeros). W1 is 1.0 s of a 1 kHz sine at -12 dBFS. W2 is 1.5 s of music and SFX. The
image and `tools/audio_spectro.py` apply `sound/src/snd_verdict.h`'s rule:

- **Integrity:** at most 2 write and 2 mic-read failures, a complete capture, and real-time
  playback within 10%.
- **Live mic:** a mic counts only if its W0 AC RMS is at least 0.5 LSB (never exactly 0).
- **Tone heard:** the W1 1 kHz bin is at least 25 (amplitude 10 LSB), at least 20 dB over W0's,
  and holds at least 10% of W1's AC power.
- **Music heard:** the W2 RMS is at least 5 LSB and at least 2x W0's.

For scale, the 2026-09-15 loopback on this unit put the 1 kHz bin 34-48 dB above silence at
volume 16/48, with gain 0x200 (alp-sdk `examples/aen/aen-pdm-mic-alif/README.md`, round 4f).

Expected console, in order:
1. `[snd] 1 ... -> 0` through `[snd] 9`
2. `[snd] 10 8 silent blocks -> 0 in ~80-100 ms (expect 60..250)`
3. `[snd] 11 tas2563_resume(0x4d/0x4e) -> 0`
4. `capture done: 142848 frames`
5. three window lines, all with nonzero rms
6. `played ~23300 ms, 0 write failures, 0 mic read failures, synth clipped 0`
7. synth cost (budget 2,560,000 cycles per 16 ms block at 160 MHz)
8. `RESULT PASS: the mics heard the 1 kHz tone and the music`

If step 2 fails, the IO8 revision guard found no CRC-valid manifest confirming `2626-r2`. If
step 10 fails, the bit clock is not running.

## Second bench round (2026-09-23): per-effect loopback, synth V2, amp A/B

The patched clock fixed playback: P played 23277 ms of 23280 with 0 write failures. The
maintainer heard it and reported "it is playing but some of the sounds is broken". The broken
sounds are "harsh / distorted / buzzy", "only some effects", and the music is fine.

Two further results from this round:
- **T's mics** failed every read and captured all zeros, while control C2 (`aen-pdm-mic-alif`)
  captured live noise on this board.
- **C3** (the probe built for the AEN801 target) could not open the mux GPIOs on this AEN803
  board.

### Why T's mics read nothing

T **started** the PDM in bring-up step 8. Its first read came after step 10's clock check
(111 ms) and the amps' I2C resume. The alp audio_in slab holds 4 blocks (64 ms), and an
overrun is sticky: `alp_audio_in_read()` returns `ALP_ERR_IO` until the stream is stopped and
restarted (alp-sdk `include/alp/audio.h`, `alp_audio_in_read` @return, #2133 round 4c). C2
reads straight after its start.

The fix:
- Step 8 now only opens the PDM, and it is started right before the first read.
- 4 pre-roll blocks are discarded.
- An `ALP_ERR_IO` stops and restarts the stream. It is counted, and the first 4 return codes
  are printed.

### TEST image v3: every effect in isolation, compared against the PC render

The capture is mono mic U19 at 48 kHz. Windows (`sound/src/snd_script.h`, shared with the PC
renderer):
- silence;
- 1 kHz at -12 dBFS and at -3 dBFS, and 200 Hz at -6 dBFS (chain THD against level);
- 10 s of music from bar 9 (lead and drums);
- every effect alone: footstep, pickup, pickup combo 6, jump, duck, crash, crash wire, wire,
  attract jingle, game over.

The capture header is 512 bytes, 'TRC2', documented in `sound/src/main.c`. After each window
**both amps' latched faults are read and cleared** (limiter active / max attenuation / mute,
VBAT below inflection, brown-out, TDM clock, boost clamp). A window that trips the amp's
protection therefore names itself.

`tools/audio_spectro.py --capture` then does the following:
1. It rebuilds the PC renderer with the image's synth build (V1/V2, rate) and renders the same
   script.
2. It estimates the speaker+mic response from the music window.
3. Per window it reports:
   - `excess`: captured energy the render x response does not explain (distortion, aliasing,
     buzz);
   - log-spectral distance;
   - excess above 6 kHz;
   - mic clipping and flat-tops;
   - the render's own peak;
   - THD of the tones;
   - the amp faults.
4. It ranks the worst effects.

`--selftest` checks two things: the verdict rejects the all-zero and write-storm captures, and
a deliberately hard-clipped effect ranks worst.

### Synth V2 (`TR_AUDIO_V2`, default on; `-DTR_SND_V2=OFF` renders V1 bit for bit)

- **polyBLEP square and saw.** Measured on the host renders: energy above 5 kHz drops by
  5.1 dB (attract jingle), 4.1 dB (crash wire) and 6.2 dB (game over). Those are aliases folded
  down from the naive oscillators at 16 kHz: inharmonic, and they sweep with pitch.
- **Soft master knee above -2.5 dBFS** (`y = K + dR/(d+R)`) instead of a hard clamp. It is
  counted as `limited`.
- **Level trims.** The crash thump, body and noise went from 7000/2500/4500 to 5000/1800/3200.
  The crash now peaks at 13674 (-7.6 dBFS); it was 19137 (-4.6 dBFS). The debris squares are
  low-passed and 30% quieter, the wire zap goes from 3500 to 2500, and the jump whoosh opens to
  250‰ instead of 450‰.
- **48 kHz** (`-DTR_SND_RATE=48000`, V2 only). 76.8 MHz / (64 x 48 kHz) = 25 is exact, and so
  is 16 kHz (75). 32 kHz gives 37.5, so it is not offered. Filter coefficients are converted
  (integer cube root) and noise keeps its 16 kHz colour, so both rates render the same levels.
  The image reports the worst render cycles per window; the budget is 2,560,000 per 16 ms at
  160 MHz.
- **Goldens:**
  - V1 `0xDBF70C58` (unchanged);
  - V2 16 kHz `0x3BD638D8`;
  - V2 48 kHz `0x411B757B`.

  `runner.sh` checks all three on the host and on the A32 under qemu.
- **Previews:** V2 WAV/PNG files, and the loopback script render
  (`loopback_script_ref.wav`), are in the local work directory `p10v2/`.

### TAS2563 configuration audit (alp-sdk `chips/tas2563/tas2563.c` as linked from alp-sdk-lcd)

SLASET3D itself is not on the build host. The citations below are the driver's own, and each
one is cross-checked against the Linux tas2562 codec driver, which also binds `ti,tas2563`
(`<Alif Linux>/sound/soc/codecs/tas2562.{c,h}`).

| register | what we program | check |
|---|---|---|
| TDM_CFG2 0x08 | RX_WLEN=00b (16-bit word), RX_SLEN=10b (32-bit slot) | Matches the DW I2S3 frame, which is 64 SBCLK/FSYNC (i2s_dw `wss_len` 32). SLASET3D §7.4.2 p.39 allows only 32-bit slots in a 2-slot frame. Linux uses the same encodings. OK. |
| TDM_CFG1 0x07 | RX_OFFSET=1 (I2S), RX_EDGE=0 (rising), RX_JUSTIFY=0 | §7.4.2 p.41: "1 for an I2S format". Linux uses the same for NB_NF I2S. OK. |
| TDM_CFG3 0x09 | reset: RX_SLOT_R=1, RX_SLOT_L=0; per amp, U27 LEFT / U28 RIGHT via `tas2563_rx_channel_t` | OK. We send the same mono to both slots. |
| TDM_CFG0 0x06 | SAMP_RATE 001b (16k) / 100b (48k); AUTO_RATE, RAMP_RATE, CLASSD_SYNC at reset | Linux changes RAMP_RATE only for the 44.1 kHz family. OK. |
| PB_CFG1 0x03 | AMP_LEVEL 01h = 8.5 dBV (3.76 Vpk), the lowest | Lowest analog gain. It lowers the full-scale output; it does not add distortion. |
| DVC_CFG1..4, page 2 0x0C..0x0F | never written (POR 0x40000000 = 0 dB) | A/B override `sound/amp_regs_dvc_minus6db.h` sets -6.02 dB (map and Q2.30 format from Linux `TAS2562_DVC_CFG1..4`). |
| PWR_CTL 0x02 | MODE only; VSNS/ISNS power-down bits left at reset | The IV-sense state does not affect playback. |
| limiter, brown-out (BOP), VBAT tracking, noise gate / idle-channel detect, class-D edge rate, spread spectrum, boost | **not configured by the driver**: POR state; register pages not citable without SLASET3D | Not asserted as a cause.   steals the oldest allocation, so the event being started keeps its voices. V1/V2 keep 12 voices

  case where age and slot order differ.
- **Ring.**
  - `tr_aring_pop()` refuses a ring with another `TR_ARING_VERSION`.
  - test_aring checks order, not just barrier counts: at the push's first barrier the slot is
    written and head is not yet bumped; the pop has a barrier before the slot read and another
    before the tail bump.

   `loadAddress 0x50000000`, `["load","boot"]`; 163432 B fits the 256 KiB HP ITCM). Only on a
   carrier with the reworked U46.

For sound work on this bench, iterate on the **HE** with the TEST image (Flow C, no MRAM
write). The synth, the ring and the I2S/amp path are the same code on either core. Move to the
HP only for the integration run.

## Open risks

- **The carrier I2C bus is shared across cores.** In the game, the HE reads the BMI323 on
  carrier I2C bus 0 (Alif I2C2) every tick, and the HP's bring-up (steps 5-7) configures the
  same controller and talks to 0x4D/0x4E. Two cores programming one controller at once can
  corrupt either transfer. Mitigations:
  - have the HE start the HP (option 2) after the IMU is up and pause IMU reads during the HP's
    ~100 ms of amp init; or
  - move the amp init to the HE.
  Not a problem on the bench test (HE only).
- **The game board's audio path.** The game board (E1M-AEN803 2026W36-0009) has an unverified
  audio path (amps, speakers, U46 rework). The GAME image must not run there until its U46 is
  confirmed reworked; the CMake gate makes that an explicit choice.
- **The bridge on the HP.** The HP owns the CC3501E bridge in the game. Its LP-GPIO pad enable
  (`sound/src/cc3501e_bridge.c`) was extended from the HE-only `#if` to include the HP. The
  bridge from the HP is not silicon-proven (IRQ 138 for SPI1 is already marked BENCH-TBD in the
  alp-sdk overlays), and the HE game must never start the bridge too.
- The PDM/loopback thresholds are first estimates, tied to the 2026-09-15 loopback numbers.
  Adjust them after the first run rather than trusting them blind.
