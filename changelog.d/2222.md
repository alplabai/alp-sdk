### Fixed — V2N bake-status docs now distinguish base images from DRP-AI-enabled images (#2222)

The V2N integration guide, verification tables, OS support matrix, and
`mera2-drpai-tvm` recipe commentary now use
`docs/bring-up-drpai-v2n.md` as the bake-status authority. They record the
completed 12,118-task, 716 MB `drpai`-OFF `alp-image-edge` bake without
implying that a `drpai`-enabled bake, `alp-image-prod` bake, or on-bench boot
of that image has happened. The recipe text also identifies the wrapper
compile as a hand-run host probe rather than a BitBake `do_compile` run.
