### Fixed — `tests/scripts/` no longer leaves files in the host's `/tmp` (#2625)

Every run of the suite left 27 files in the real `/tmp`: 25 `<tag>-atoc-before.<random>` transcripts from `bench_atoc_replace_guard()` and `bench_flowd_atoc_guard()`, one `reread.*` and one `bench-jlink-run.jlink.*`. The bench scripts keep those under `${TMPDIR:-/tmp}` on purpose, as the record of what was resident before a destructive write, and the tests that source `bench-env.sh` never exported a `TMPDIR` of their own. One board-farm host had collected about 20,000 of them since 2026-09-12.

`tests/scripts/conftest.py` now has an autouse fixture, `_sandbox_tmpdir`, that points `TMPDIR` at a per-test directory under pytest's basetemp, which pytest prunes to the last three runs. A test that exports `TMPDIR` inside the script it runs still wins, so the unwritable-`TMPDIR` and missing-`TMPDIR` cases are unchanged. The before and after counts were measured on Linux; a host whose bash does not inherit the test process's environment still falls back to its own `/tmp`. The bench scripts themselves are not touched: retention on a real bench stays as documented in `scripts/bench/aen/README.md`.
