### Fixed — retract the corrupted-tree V2N supervisor / DEEPX rail claims from #2021 / a4ff0e3a (#2021)

#2021 claimed the `CONFIG_ALP_SDK_V2N*` Kconfig symbols were undefined; its
own author retracted it once the cause surfaced -- the claim came from a
worktree holding a binary-corrupted copy of
`zephyr/kconfigs/v2n-supervisor.kconfig`. Before the retraction, `a4ff0e3a`
(#2019) had already rewritten `docs/v1.0-readiness.md`'s
`src/zephyr/v2n_power_mgmt.c` bullet from that corrupted tree, adding text
that the two symbols "do not exist" / "are not defined anywhere" and that
the `#else` stub "wins on every build". None of that holds against a clean
`20fec7a7` checkout: both symbols resolve `=y` on `alp_e1m_v2m101_m33_sm`
and `alp_e1m_v2n101_m33_sm` via the generated `alp.conf`.

`docs/v1.0-readiness.md`, `docs/console.md`,
`docs/cc3501e-companion-commands.md`, the `v2n-m1-deepx-inference` example
(README, `board.yaml` comment, `main.c` comment),
`src/zephyr/v2n_power_mgmt.c`'s DT-alias comment, and
`zephyr/kconfigs/v2n-supervisor.kconfig`'s help text now describe the real
mechanism: the module's DT aliases are `v2n-deepx-pwr-en-req` /
`v2n-deepx-core-0p75-en` (not a `-gpios` property naming convention), and no
in-tree board defines them, so DEEPX rail bring-up compiles to its
`ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` stub on `alp_e1m_v2m101_m33_sm` -- tracked as #2045.
Separately, the V2N supervisor's `CONFIG_ALP_SDK_V2N_SUPERVISOR_SPI_BUS_ID`
/ `_I2C_BUS_ID` both default `-1` and nothing in-tree overrides them, so the
supervisor singleton never opens a bus and `alp companion` commands fail
`ALP_ERR_NOT_READY` on a stock build -- tracked as #2044.

`zephyr/kconfigs/chips.kconfig`'s DA9292 help text is also corrected: the
PMIC's 7-bit I2C address is `0x1E` (`include/alp/chips/da9292.h`), not the
stale `0x1C` it carried.
