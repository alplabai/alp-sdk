### Fixed — chip drivers busy-waited up to 1.5 s in a primitive reserved for sub-millisecond timing (#1621)

`alp_delay_us()` is documented in `include/alp/peripheral.h` as a **non-yielding**
spin for sub-millisecond hardware timing, with `alp_delay_ms()` named as the
scheduler-friendly alternative. 34 call sites across `chips/` used it for waits
of 1 ms and longer — up to **1500000 us**, a 1.5 s spin that starves every other
thread on the system, including the ones a driver's own timeout depends on.

The long waits move to `alp_delay_ms()`. Sites left as busy-waits are the ones
where yielding would be a worse bug than the spin — a wait inside an ISR, a
critical section, or a lock — and each is called out where it stands rather than
silently kept.

This is a class of defect rather than one bug, and nothing yet stops it coming
back: the issue's suggested `check_*.py` gate rejecting a fixed-literal
`alp_delay_us(>= 1000)` under `chips/` is not part of this change (a new gate
needs the four-site lockstep registration, which is its own slice).
