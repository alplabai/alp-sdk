### Documented — backfilled the verification ledger's v0.11.0-v0.15.0 gap (#1893)

`docs/test-plan.md`'s newest per-release section had stalled at **v0.9.0**
while `metadata/sdk_version.yaml` had moved on to **0.16.0** — seven tagged
releases plus the in-flight v0.17.0 candidate with no per-release ledger
entry, even though `docs/abi/` carries a snapshot for every one of them.

Added sections for **v0.11.0/v0.11.1**, **v0.12.0**, **v0.13.0**, **v0.14.0**,
and **v0.15.0**, narrowing the gap to two releases (**v0.10.0** and
**v0.16.0**, tracked as remaining scope on #1893). This is a headline-feature
backfill, not a full re-audit of each `CHANGELOG.md` section — most entries
across those five releases are bug fixes to already-ledgered rows or
CI/build/`tan`-CLI infrastructure outside this ledger's scope. Every new row
defaults to its legend's `⏳ untested` state and says "no bench evidence
in-tree" unless a real bench result exists, to avoid manufacturing silicon
evidence nobody produced:

- Real bench evidence found and cited: the `<alp/jpeg.h>` Hantro VC9000E
  hardware encoder (silicon-proven on E1M-AEN801, `JPEG_HW_ID 0x90001000`)
  and a CC3501E BLE GATT dynamic-registration bench example (PING/register/
  advertise proven PASS on physical E1M-AEN801, persistent across a cold
  POR, server-only).
- Real bug-reproduction evidence: an OSPI0/OSPI1 clock-enable bus fault
  (`BFAR 0x83000018`) reproduced identically on two E8 bench runs, then
  fixed.
- No bench evidence in-tree, marked accordingly: the AEN401 (E4) USB-host
  xHCI skeleton, and four new Yocto backends (`<alp/display.h>`,
  `<alp/i3c.h>`, `<alp/storage.h>`, `<alp/usb.h>`) that are code-complete
  and link-clean but unexercised on real hardware.
- Folded into the existing v0.3.0 DRP-AI row rather than duplicated: the
  v0.15.0 DRP-AI3 opt-in `PACKAGECONFIG`/devicetree switches.

Also rewrote the "Ledger scope" note to say plainly that this document is
not a per-release ledger for every tagged version, and regenerated
