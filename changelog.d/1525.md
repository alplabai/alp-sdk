### Fixed — `check_changelog_citations.py`'s foreign-repo skip list was incomplete, hard-failing correct cross-repo citations (#1525)

Same defect and same fix as #1522: `_FOREIGN_PREFIXES` widened from three
hand-picked subpaths (`python/tan/`, `crates/`, `contract/`) to their whole
top-level directories, so any citation into tan-cli's `python/` tree —
`python/tan/...`, `python/tests/...`, `python/scripts/...` — is SKIPPED as
foreign instead of hard-failing as "no such file in this tree". A gate that
fails correct input trains people to strip the citation rather than trust
the gate; see `changelog.d/1522.md` for the full evidence and the
mutation-proof.
