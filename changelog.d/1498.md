### Fixed — a stale zcbor citation shipped in `CHANGELOG.md` because the #1416 citation gate stops at `changelog.d/` (#1498)

`CHANGELOG.md:4006` cited
`meta-alp-sdk/recipes-devtools/zcbor/zcbor_0.9.1.bb:90` / `:92` for the
double `SRC_URI`/`SRCREV` pin that broke every A55 image build; the actual
lines are `:102` (`SRC_URI`) and `:104` (`SRCREV`). The citation was correct
against the fragment at authoring time and went stale when a later edit to
the recipe shifted both lines by 12 — `scripts/check_changelog_citations.py`
only ever reads `changelog.d/`, so once `scripts/assemble_changelog.py`
folded the fragment into `CHANGELOG.md` and deleted it, the citation left
gate scope permanently and nothing re-checked it. Fixed in place.

Extending the gate to also scan `CHANGELOG.md` is the real fix, but running
it against the shipped file first (`_check_one` against `CHANGELOG.md`)
surfaces 12 pre-existing broken citations unrelated to this issue — 3 stale
anchors and 9 paths that no longer resolve (mostly short filenames missing
their directory prefix, plus a couple of moved/renamed files). Landing the
extension now would redden on arrival, so it is left as follow-up; this
citation-only fix is the smaller step.
