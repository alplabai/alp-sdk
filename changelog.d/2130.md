### Fixed — bench-env's fatal `swd` check had no test, and a `changelog.d/2076.md` citation was stale (#2130)

Two small gaps left behind by #2033 (#2032, #2064), found in review of its
final commit `0f97f6c1a`. Neither is a regression from that PR, and neither
blocked it.

**No test proved an empty `swd` resource still refuses.** `0f97f6c1a` split
`bench_labgrid_resolve()`'s two resource checks: a place exporting no
`seuart` became legal (`e1m-aen-evk-02`/`-03` have no SE-UART at all), while a
place exporting no `swd` stays fatal
(`scripts/bench/aen/bench-env.sh:247-250` ("exports no 'swd' resource path")).
Only the relaxed half was tested
(`tests/scripts/test_bench_labgrid_resolver.py::test_place_with_swd_but_no_seuart_resolves_successfully`)
-- nothing constructed a place whose `acquired resources:` block omitted
`swd` and asserted the refusal, so a future edit that loosened the `swd`
check the same way `seuart` was loosened would have passed the whole suite.
Added `test_place_with_seuart_but_no_swd_still_refuses`
(`tests/scripts/test_bench_labgrid_resolver.py:386-411` ("The mirror image")),
mirroring the
`seuart` test's fixture-construction technique (an executable stub written
into the test's own `tmp_path`, host-independent) and asserting exit status 1
and the `exports no 'swd' resource path` stderr message. Confirmed red --
fails when the `swd` check is temporarily relaxed the same way `seuart` was
relaxed -- and green again with the check restored.

**Stale bare citation in `changelog.d/2076.md`.** Line 13 cited
`` `scripts/bench/aen/bench-env.sh:329-401` `` as `bench_jlink_assert_connected`;
on current `bench-env.sh` that range is the `SETOOLS_DIR`/J-Link
device-profile section, and `bench_jlink_assert_connected()` actually spans
`scripts/bench/aen/bench-env.sh:826-908` ("bench_jlink_assert_connected() {").
`check_changelog_citations.py` passed it anyway: only the parenthesised
`` (`path:NNN` ("anchor")) `` form is text-verified, a bare `path:NNN` is only
range-checked. Re-pointed the citation at the real range and converted it to
the anchored form so the gate catches the next drift. No other bare
`bench-env.sh:NNN` citation in that fragment had drifted (the file's other
two `bench-env.sh` mentions carry no line number at all).
