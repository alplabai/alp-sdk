### Fixed — `check_atoc_reservation.py`'s preset check refused an OSPI0 row for merely being the highest address in the file, instead of scoping "the top" to the SoC's declared MRAM aperture (#2069)

`_check_preset()` (`scripts/check_atoc_reservation.py:565` ("def _check_preset(path: Path)"))
built its span list from every `memory_map:` row with a resolved integer base, with no aperture
filter, and took `window_top = max(hi)` across the whole file. An OSPI0 HyperRAM row at XIP base
`0xA0000000` — well above the App MRAM window — became the file-wide maximum and was refused as
"the boot table in disguise" purely for being highest, even though the real `atoc` band correctly
owned the top of the actual MRAM window underneath it.

The aperture is now resolved before the top-of-window decision, not after it
(`scripts/check_atoc_reservation.py:614` ("aperture = _resolve_aperture(doc)")) — the 4b/4c
aperture checks already resolved it, just too late to inform this one. Where an aperture resolves,
"the top" is the SoC variant's aperture end (`full_hi`), not the authored maximum, and only
non-zero-size rows ending exactly at `full_hi` are eligible to own it — a zero-size row placed at
the right address reserves nothing and must not be able to satisfy the rule by merely existing
there. A layout where nothing ends at `full_hi` now fails explicitly too
(`scripts/check_atoc_reservation.py:627-634` ("no region in the declared memory_map ends at")),
naming the expected top address, instead of silently picking whatever row happened to be highest.
Where no aperture resolves (a non-Alif SoC, or a fixture with no `silicon:`) the original
file-wide-max behaviour is unchanged — deliberately fail-closed, so losing aperture resolution
still refuses an out-of-window row loudly rather than passing it.

This gate fix stands on its own; adding an OSPI0 row to a SoM preset's `memory_map:` is explicitly
out of scope here — that population still lives in `on_module.hyperram` /
`on_module.ospi_memories.ospi0` per the #2069 decision.

Also corrected the check's own stale docstring, which named the shared OSPI0 HyperRAM at
`chip_select: 1` and by a single part number: every populated SKU now declares `chip_select: 0`,
and the fitted part is per-SKU (`on_module.hyperram`), not a single name true of every AEN board —
naming neither here avoids the docstring going stale again the next time a SKU's BOM changes.
