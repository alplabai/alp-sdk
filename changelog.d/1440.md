### Fixed — `docs/glossary.md` stated the generic Cortex-M33 IDCODE as a GD32G553 measurement (#1440)

The **IDCODE** glossary entry read:

> Documented as `0x6BA02477` for the GD32G553 (Cortex-M33 r0p1 SW-DPv2);
> used by `gd32_swd_connect` to confirm the link reaches the right silicon.

Both halves are contradicted by the header the entry documents.
`include/alp/chips/gd32_swd.h` carries `@warning UNVERIFIED on a GD32G553`
on `GD32_SWD_EXPECTED_IDCODE` and states that "a correctly-wired GD32
therefore fails a comparison against this macro, so do NOT make a mismatch
fatal (`gd32_swd_connect()` deliberately does not)".

So `0x6BA02477` is the **generic** Cortex-M33 r0p1 SW-DPv2 expectation, not
a GD32 reading — and `gd32_swd_connect()` reports the value rather than
confirming the link reached the right silicon. A reader trusting the
glossary would expect a wrong-board refusal that does not exist, and would
read a mismatch on a correctly-wired GD32 as a fault.

The entry now matches the header, names both candidate values with the
places they were read (`0x0BE12477` off the GD32 bridge and `0x6BA02477`
off the V2N CM33 DAP, both on `e1mx-v2n-m1-01`), and repeats the header's
guidance that a production test wanting to refuse on a mismatch must match
a value measured on its own board.

No hardware fact is settled here: **neither candidate has been measured on
a GD32 with a probe attached**, which is what #1440 and #1369 are open for.
`metadata/chips/gd32_swd.yaml`'s `target_expected_idcode` gains the same
hedge as a comment. Its **value is deliberately unchanged** — picking
between the two candidates would be guessing a hardware fact, and the
field is not consumed as a fatal guard today. The comment records that it
is the generic value, that a correct GD32 is expected to fail against it,
and that it must not be read as a wrong-board guard until one candidate is
measured.

`scripts/bench/aen/bench-env.sh` and `docs/test-plan.md:176` already
carried this hedge correctly; the glossary and the manifest were the sites
left uncorrected.
