### Fixed — GD32 bridge protocol doc no longer claims non-CRC fields are big-endian (#2307)

`docs/gd32-bridge-protocol.md` §4 said the CRC was the only little-endian
field and every other multi-byte field was big-endian, contradicting §2 and
the firmware. Every multi-byte field on the wire is little-endian. A host
that followed §4 and packed `OTA_BEGIN`'s `size`/`crc32` big-endian got
`STATUS_OUT_OF_RANGE` (`0x08`) back. §4 now matches §2 and names the
MSB-first hex text in `protocol_vectors.txt` as the actual trap.
