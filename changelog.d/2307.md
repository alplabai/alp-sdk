### Fixed — GD32 bridge protocol doc no longer claims payload integers are big-endian (#2307)

`docs/gd32-bridge-protocol.md` §4 said the CRC was "the one field in the
envelope that is little-endian; every other multi-byte field in this protocol
is big-endian". That contradicted §2 and the firmware, which decodes every
multi-byte payload field little-endian. A host that followed §4 got
`STATUS 0x08` (OUT_OF_RANGE) on `OTA_BEGIN` (hit on E1M-V2M103). §4 now points
back to §2's little-endian rule.
