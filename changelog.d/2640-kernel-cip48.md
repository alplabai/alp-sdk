### Changed — Linux kernel pin moves to 6.1.157-cip48 (#2640)

**`meta-alp-sdk` now builds `linux-renesas` from `rz-6.1-cip48` (`1cac8c7e4cdb2d8194f5249e309493dadd9015c3`, `6.1.157-cip48`) instead of `rz-6.1-cip43` (`6717c06c72df`).** `linux-renesas_%.bbappend` overrides `LINUX_VERSION` / `KERNEL_BRANCH` / `KERNEL_REV`, and the 29 ALP kernel patches were ported to it, none dropped: 0001, 0011, 0017, 0020, 0021 and 0033 needed hand refreshes (0011's ICU error masks are rewritten against the new `rzv2h_icu_setup_irqs()`), the rest apply as before. Not yet verified: the board has not booted cip48 (console on the new `rsci` driver, GbE, PCIe + DX-M1, CSI/CRU, audio, Wi-Fi/BT, GD32 bridge, CM33 survival past `clk_disable_unused`, `irq 14: nobody cared`, eMMC/SD, USB over-current, DSI), the vendor DRP-AI / codec / OpenCV-accelerator patches and out-of-tree modules are unchecked against cip48, and the 0018/0034/0035 DRP patches are untouched.
