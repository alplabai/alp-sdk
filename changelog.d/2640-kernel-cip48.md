### Changed — Linux kernel pin moves to 6.1.157-cip48 (#2640)

**`meta-alp-sdk` now builds `linux-renesas` from `rz-6.1-cip48` (`1cac8c7e4cdb2d8194f5249e309493dadd9015c3`, `6.1.157-cip48`) instead of `rz-6.1-cip43` (`6717c06c72df`).** `linux-renesas_6.1.bbappend` (the 6.1 recipe only) overrides `LINUX_VERSION` / `KERNEL_BRANCH` / `KERNEL_REV`, and the 29 ALP kernel patches were ported to it, none dropped: 0001, 0011, 0017, 0020, 0021 and 0033 needed hand refreshes (0011's ICU error masks are rewritten against the new `rzv2h_icu_setup_irqs()`), the rest apply as before. Not yet verified: the board has not booted cip48 (console on the new `rsci` driver, GbE, PCIe + DX-M1, CSI/CRU, audio, Wi-Fi/BT, GD32 bridge, CM33 survival past `clk_disable_unused`, `irq 14: nobody cared`, eMMC/SD, USB over-current, DSI), the vendor DRP-AI / codec / OpenCV-accelerator patches and out-of-tree modules are unchecked against cip48, and the 0018/0034/0035 DRP patches are untouched.

**This cannot ship until the vendor feature layers are updated for cip48.** `meta-rz-drpai` `0003-set-cru-amnaxiattr-axilen.patch` and `0004-config-macro-eMMC_SD_SEL-to-support-eMMC-boot.patch`, and the `0001-add-drp-property-to-devicetree.patch` from the `meta-rz-codecs` / `meta-rz-opencva` layers, fail `do_patch` against cip48; they must be updated upstream or in those layers. The host build check (`bitbake linux-renesas -c compile`, dtbs included, `e1m-v2m103-a55` and `e1m-v2n101-a55`) passes only with those three patches removed and the DRP1 overlay (`ALP_DRP1_DT_ENABLE`) stubbed, in a scratch `local.conf`. The board dts/dtsi are ported to the cip48 labels (`gpt1_2` to `&gpt1` pwm index 5, `sci4` to `rsci4`, `usb3_phy0`/`xhci0` to `usb3_phy`/`xhci`), pin assignments unchanged; the GPT/PWM binding and RSCI4 Bluetooth UART are not bench-validated.
