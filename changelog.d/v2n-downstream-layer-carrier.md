### Added — Downstream layer template and custom-carrier guide for V2N / V2N-M1

**`meta-alp-sdk/templates/meta-example-product` is a starting layer for a product on V2N / V2N-M1, and `docs/v2n-custom-carrier.md` documents the carrier bring-up.** The template has `layer.conf` with `LAYERDEPENDS` on `alp-sdk`, an image on `alp-image-base`, and an example carrier dts that includes the stock SoM dtsi and keeps the ownership dtsi last. The guide covers keeping the stock MACHINE, bblayers and local.conf, the pad rules (PA6/P80/P95, port-9 claims, the CM33 pads, the EEPROM and DA9292 buses, `check_pad_voltage.py`), the `core-image-%` bbappend trap, the brcmfmac compatible string, the Vulkan and ffmpeg caveats, and why U-Boot only loads the stock dtb basename.
