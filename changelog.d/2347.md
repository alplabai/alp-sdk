### Added — a gate that pins the V2N power-chip driver register tables to their manifests (#2347)

`scripts/check_chip_reg_parity.py` compares the hand-written C register tables with `metadata/chips/<part>.yaml`, field by field. Before this, only the existence of a driver and its manifest was checked:

- **ACT88760:** every `rail_table[]` entry, expanded through the driver's own `BUCK_R`/`BUCK_R1`/`BUCK_F`/`LDO` macros. It checks the slave, status/VSET0/ON registers, range selector, VSET mask and per-range base/step. It also checks `raw_write_allow[]` against the `write: allow` rows.
- **DA9292:** the `DA9292_REG_*` addresses, the `DA9292_CTRL01_*` channel bits and VOUT registers, and the raw-write window.
- **TPS628640:** the `TPS628640_REG_*` and `TPS628640_VOUT_*` defines.

A one-digit slip on either side, or a manifest row flipped to `write: allow` behind the driver's back, now fails CI rather than programming the wrong register on a live PMIC. On its first run the gate found `PMC_CFG_00` (`0x0E`), which the DA9292 driver reads but the manifest did not list; the manifest now lists it as `write: deny`.

`power-tree-v1.schema.json` now types the `chips` block: ACT88760 ADD1/ADD2 and the DA9292 address and `identity` (`dev_id`/`rev_id`/`cfg_rev`, 0-255) are required. A typo there is now a validation error instead of a `KeyError`. `gen_power_tree.py`'s cross-check also pins `chips.act8760.addr_add1/addr_add2` to the manifest's `addr_7bit` values.
