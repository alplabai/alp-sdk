### Documented — `docs/soms/aen.md` claimed every non-`E1M-AEN801` preset still carried the pre-#1848 LPI2C0 assumption; none do (#2118)

The paragraph above the `## On-module housekeeping I2C (BRD_I2C)` heading in
[`docs/soms/aen.md`](../docs/soms/aen.md) said "the other AEN SKUs still carry
the pre-#1848 LPI2C0 assumption in their own preset files pending the same
netlist evidence." False on `dev`: `metadata/e1m_modules/E1M-AEN301.yaml`,
`E1M-AEN401.yaml`, `E1M-AEN501.yaml`, `E1M-AEN601.yaml`, and `E1M-AEN701.yaml`
each explicitly reject the LPI2C0 reading ("HELD, NOT the disproven LPI2C0
claim (#1848, ADR 0017 amended ...)"), and `E1M-AEN803.yaml` carries the
corrected `I2C0_SDA_C = P7_0` / `I2C0_SCL_C = P7_1` mapping outright. Reworded
to say what is true: #1848 disproved the LPI2C0 reading for the whole family,
not just `E1M-AEN801`, and no shipped preset carries it -- the other SKUs
still await their own per-part datasheet confirmation of the corrected
mapping, which the presets' own comments already say (`bus_master` held
pending that check).
