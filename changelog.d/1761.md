### Fixed — a repo-wide documentation sweep corrected 27 claims that contradicted the tree (#1757–#1783)

Twenty-six docs stated something the current tree disagrees with, and one
example directory promised a `.gitignore` that did not exist. Every claim was
checked against its source of truth before editing.

Two mattered more than the rest.

`docs/tutorials/02-i2c-scan.md` told the reader to expect OPTIGA Trust M
(`0x30`), TMP112 (`0x48`) and RV-3028-C7 (`0x52`) to ACK on the bus the
scanner opens. `BOARD_I2C_SENSORS` resolves to `ALP_E1M_I2C0` (SoC I2C2), and
`metadata/e1m_modules/E1M-AEN801.yaml` puts only the 24C128 EEPROM at `0x50`
on `e1m_i2c0:`; the other three sit on a separate bus, `brd_i2c:`, that this
scan never opens. Three of the four documented ACKs
could never appear, so a reader chasing them was debugging a bus that was
never there. The page now agrees with `docs/bring-up-aen.md`, including that
`0x48` on I2C2 is U32 INA236B (+V_CAM0 rail) on pre-respin carriers, not the
TMP112.

`examples/connectivity/iot-connected-camera/certs/README.md` told the reader
that `client.key` was covered by a `.gitignore` in that directory. There was
no `.gitignore` there at all — `git check-ignore` on the path exited 1, and
the directory tracked only `README.md`. A customer generating a key beside it
and running `git add -A` would have committed a TLS private key. The missing
`.gitignore` is now present and the sentence describes what it actually does.

The remainder were stale identifiers and versions: the GD32 bridge protocol
documented as `0.2.0` where `gd32-bridge-firmware:src/protocol.h` defines
`0.9.0`; `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` printed as `-5` in two expected-output blocks
where `include/alp/peripheral.h` defines `-6`; `v0.15-snapshot.json` named as
the current ABI snapshot while `metadata/sdk_version.yaml` declares `0.16.0`;
AEN and NX9101 described as E1M-X when they are E1M (35x35 mm); console
samples showing `EVK_PIN_*` names that `gpio-button-led` never prints; and a
`west build` line missing the `/r9a09g056n48gbg/cm33` board qualifiers.

Three ADRs keep their decisions intact and gained corrections instead:
ADR-0009 records in a dated amendment that the AEN sysbuild overlay ships
`SB_CONFIG_MCUBOOT_MODE_SINGLE_APP=y` rather than swap-using-scratch,
ADR-0022's status block now says the Renode models it called "still present"
were deleted, and ADR-0015's prebuilt-blob claim points at a path that exists.
