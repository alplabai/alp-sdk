### Added — public GD32 bridge recovery images and their boundary (#2215)

`docs/gd32-bridge.md` now says where the prebuilt recovery images live (tagged
releases of the public `gd32-bridge-firmware` repository, with checksums and,
when keyed, detached signatures), how to verify them, and where the public
recovery path ends and internal manufacturing begins. `som-release-signing.md`
documents how those releases are signed.
