### Fixed — the SN65DSI83 recovery no longer re-inits the bridge every 5 s on a latched PLL_UNLOCK (display blink)

`CHA_PLL_UNLOCK` (CSR `0xE5` bit 0) is a latched flag set while the PLL (re)acquires lock, including during our own re-init. The first poll after a replay saw it, `after_recovery` turned it into another re-init, and the cycle repeated every `SN65_REINIT_MIN_GAP_MS` (5 s). With PLL_EN, `PLL_EN_STAT` and `HS_CLK_SRC` all intact, a lone `0xE5` = `0x01` is now cleared and counted (`pll_unlock_events`, printed with the recovery line) and never escalates; any other `0xE5` bit keeps the existing escalation (3 polls in a row, or the first poll after a re-init), and lost config (`0x0D` PLL_EN clear, `PLL_EN_STAT` clear, `HS_CLK_SRC` mismatch) still re-inits. Upstream Linux had the same regression in its error recovery (Toradex, LKML 2025-11, "[REGRESSION] TI SN65DSI83 is being reset making display to blink On/Off"). Bench observation: `0xE5` = `0x01` after HE warm resets, previously logged as "link errors cleared".
