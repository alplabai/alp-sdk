### Fixed — `test-all.sh`'s serial-mode notice no longer lands in `--list-required-gate-scripts` output (#2647)

`scripts/test-all.sh` decides at load whether its stages may overlap, and
when they may not — `MemAvailable` below `ALP_GATE_MIN_MEM_KB` (default
12 GiB), or `ALP_TWISTER_JOBS` set — it says so. It said so on stdout, which
`--list-required-gate-scripts` uses as a machine-readable list, so on a host
short of free memory the notice became a list entry and
`tests/scripts/test_test_all_gate_coverage.py` failed with
`would run 73/72 declared gate scripts`.

Both notices now go to stderr. A full gate run's log is unchanged, since it
captures both streams. Reproduced before the change, and confirmed fixed
after it, by running the test with `ALP_GATE_MIN_MEM_KB=999999999` and again
with `ALP_TWISTER_JOBS=1`.
