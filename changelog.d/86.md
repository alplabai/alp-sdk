### Fixed — `gd32_ota_host` completes an I2C OTA past a stalling chunk (#86)

On E1M-V2M103, a slave at 0x55 on BRD_I2C holds SDA low after certain byte
sequences (gd32-bridge-firmware#315). The OTA_WRITE_CHUNK frame carrying such a
sequence times out on every retry, so the tool used to fail at a fixed offset.
The same bytes framed at a different length go through. After two failed tries,
the tool now re-sends the chunk at half the length, 8-aligned (56, then 32,
16, 8), and returns to full-size chunks afterwards.

Verified on E1M-V2M103 with the released bridge image `v0.3.0`
(`gd32-bridge-v0.3.0-slot-a.bin`, SHA256SUMS OK) over `/dev/i2c-8` with
`--unbind`:
- 54 stalled chunks were re-sent shorter, 12 of them down to 8 bytes.
- VERIFY crc32 matched `0xaebf52d9`.
- COMMIT booted `build_id=0.3.0+bad7456c702eed` in slot 0: `RESULT: PASS`.
