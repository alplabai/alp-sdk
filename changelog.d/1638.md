### Fixed — 27 Zephyr backends hand-rolled the same errno switch and drifted; a timed-out transfer surfaced as `ALP_ERR_IO` at 11 of them (#1638)

`src/common/alp_errno.h` has documented since #630 that its baseline takes a
**positive** POSIX errno and that a Zephyr mapper for the **negative** values the
Zephyr driver APIs return was "future work". In the absence of one, every Zephyr
backend wrote its own `static alp_status_t _errno_to_alp(int err)` switch — 27 of
them — and they drifted exactly as the 31 POSIX copies #630 consolidated had:

| errno | what the 27 did |
|---|---|
| `-EBUSY` | all 27 agree — the only consistent arm |
| `-EAGAIN` | 16 → `ALP_ERR_TIMEOUT`, 11 had no arm → `ALP_ERR_IO` |
| `-ETIMEDOUT` | 19 mapped it, 8 had no arm → `ALP_ERR_IO` |
| `-ENXIO`, `-EPERM`, `-EACCES`, `-EOVERFLOW` | appeared in no Zephyr switch at all |

`alp_status_from_zephyr_errno()` and `alp_status_from_zephyr_errno_ex()` are the
negative-domain twins. They negate and delegate to the POSIX baseline rather than
restating the table, so the two cannot drift apart arm by arm. A **positive**
value handed to the Zephyr mapper is the wrong domain and yields `ALP_ERR_IO`
rather than accidentally matching a case arm.

Three arms are answered locally rather than delegated:

- **`-EAGAIN` → `ALP_ERR_TIMEOUT`, a deliberate divergence** from the POSIX
  baseline's `ALP_ERR_BUSY`. In Zephyr driver APIs `-EAGAIN` signals an expired
  deadline (`i2s_read`, `spi_transceive`, `k_sem_take`-backed paths); in POSIX
  socket/file APIs it means "would block, retry now". Sixteen of the 27 already
  answered TIMEOUT, and `src/backends/can/yocto_drv.c` overrides the POSIX
  baseline the same way for the same reason. Delegating naively would have
  silently rewritten 16 drivers.
- **`-ENOSPC` → `ALP_ERR_NOMEM`** and **`-ERANGE` → `ALP_ERR_OUT_OF_RANGE`**,
  neither of which the POSIX baseline maps at all. Both are live arms in
  `src/backends/storage/zephyr_littlefs.c`, `src/backends/storage/zephyr_flash.c`
  and `src/backends/jpeg/alif_hantro.c`, so delegating them would have demoted a
  full filesystem and an out-of-range offset to a generic I/O error.

**This is not a pure refactor.** All 26 delegating sites widen what a caller can
receive — every arm they lacked previously fell to `ALP_ERR_IO`. Eleven widen on
the axis that matters most: `src/backends/{counter,dac,gpio,pwm,qenc,rtc,spi,uart,wdt}/zephyr_drv.c`
and both storage backends had no `-EAGAIN` and/or no `-ETIMEDOUT` arm, so a
driver-reported deadline was indistinguishable from an I/O error. No site's
existing arm *conflicted* with the twin — verified arm-by-arm across all 27
before deleting a switch — so nothing narrows and nothing changes answer.

`src/backends/storage/zephyr_littlefs.c` closes a real documented-contract
violation: `include/alp/storage.h` promises `alp_storage_open()` can return
`ALP_ERR_TIMEOUT` "translated from the underlying flash-area / littlefs error",
and that mapper had no arm that could produce one. Its `zephyr_flash.c` sibling
could. One header, one of two backends, contradicting itself.

`src/backends/jpeg/alif_hantro.c` keeps `-ENOBUFS → ALP_ERR_NOMEM` through an
override table rather than losing the arm: from the Hantro encoder it means the
caller's **output** buffer was too small, which is not a general allocation
failure and has no business in the shared baseline. Note its key is **negative**,
unlike the CAN table's positive POSIX keys — each `_ex` form matches in its own
domain, so a positively-keyed table there would compile and silently never match.

No ABI impact: `src/common/alp_errno.h` is internal-only. `CMakeLists.txt`
installs only `include/alp`, no public header includes it, and `docs/abi/` carries
no `alp_errno` reference.

Regression test: `tests/unit/errno_mapping/`, covering every baseline arm, the two
locally-answered arms the POSIX baseline lacks, the `-EAGAIN` divergence
(asserting both that the twin says TIMEOUT and that the POSIX baseline still says
BUSY), wrong-domain rejection of positive values, and the override form.

Out of scope, noted so the next reader does not think they were missed:
`se_rc_to_alp` exists as four byte-identical copies
(`src/backends/soc_info/alif_se.c`, `src/backends/security/se_cryptocell.c`,
`src/backends/mproc/alif_se_boot.c`, `src/backends/power/alif_se_profile.c`),
which map `-EAGAIN` to a **third** answer, `ALP_ERR_NOT_READY`. That is the same
disease in a mixed negative-errno / positive-SE-firmware-code domain and wants its
own shared helper. `src/yocto/inference_drpai.cpp`'s `_drpai_errno_to_status` is
positive-POSIX and belongs to that baseline's migration;
`src/yocto/inference_ort.cpp`'s `_ort_errorcode_to_status` maps `OrtErrorCode`,
not errno.
