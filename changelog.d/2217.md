### Documented — J5 pin 11 has no pull-up on the E1M-EVK, and the OV5647 bring-up is blocked at D-PHY Stop-state, not working (#2217)

Bench-proven 2026-09-22 on an E1M-AEN803 (serial 2026W36-0001) on an
E1M-EVK (hw_rev 2626-r2). `docs/boards/e1m-evk.md`'s `CAM_EN` row and RPi
camera connector (J5) section previously said only "keep `CAM_EN = 0` so
the module self-enables" — true for a module with its own pull-up, silent
about one that has none. The `CAM_EN_#` net has exactly three nodes: J5
pin 11, `Q1` (`BSS138PW,115` N-channel MOSFET, gate on `CAM_EN`, source on
`0V`) pin 3, and `D45` (`D5V0L1B2S9-7`, a bidirectional 5V TVS — an ESD
clamp, not a pull-up) pin 2; `R2` is a 10k gate pull-down. Nothing on the
EVK can source current into pin 11, so firmware can only leave it floating
or pull it to ground — never drive it high. A new "J5 pin 11 pull-up
rework" section documents the fix for a module without its own pull-up
(the InnoMaker CAM-OV5647): a resistor from J5 pin 11 to J5 pin 15
(`+3V3`), sized to roughly a quarter of the module's own measured
pull-down (a 10k-vs-10k pair divides to 1.65 V, below VIH), and a
resistor, never a bare wire — with pin 11 hard-tied, any firmware that
asserts `CAM_EN` turns `Q1` on and shorts `+3V3` to ground through it.
Measured effect: before the rework a full `0x08`..`0x77` I2C sweep found
zero devices and `alp_camera_open` returned `ALP_ERR_IO`; after, the
sensor answers at `0x36` with chip ID `0x5647` and board draw rises from
0.065-0.067 A to 0.074-0.080 A at 16.0 V. `CAM_EN` must stay `0` after the
rework too — asserting it still powers the module down.

`docs/camera-shields.md` previously listed the OV5647 module as
"Build-only / not run on hardware", which is now stale: with the pull-up
fitted the sensor answers on I2C, and camera bring-up was bench-attempted
and found BLOCKED, not working. `alp_camera_open` fails at
`ALP_ERR_TIMEOUT` with `E: D-PHY not locked to Stop-state. PHY status -
0x00010000 DPHY ID: 0`. `CSI_PHY_STOPSTATE` (`0x4903304c`) reaches only
bit0 (`STOPSTATEDATA_0`); bit1 (`DATA_1`), bit16 (`CLK`) and
`CSI_PHY_RX` bit17 (`RXCLKACTIVEHS`) never assert, unlike the OV9281 path
on the same receiver, which reaches `0x00010003`. Ruled out by
control-validated bench runs: module power, the sensor's own MIPI PHY
being disabled (register `0x3018` = `0x44`), the mainline "coax lanes
into LP-11" park sequence, sensor-before-receiver ordering, and the
receiver's D-PHY frequency bin (identical at `hsfreqrange 0x16` and
`0x09`, the OV9281's own bin). Open candidates: the physical clock/data-1
pairs through the P/N-crossing adapter, a module-level fault, and `0x4837
PCLK_PERIOD`, which the driver never programs and sits at its reset value
`0x15`. The OV9281 rows and their bench-verified claims are unchanged.
