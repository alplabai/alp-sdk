### Fixed — RZ/V2N pad metadata corrected against the Renesas manual, and A55 ownership recorded (audit IO-06, IO-10, IO-13, IO-M1, IO-M2, MM-X1, NPU-15, OWN-1, XSPI-2, PCIE-4, CM33-04) (#2660)

`renesas-peripheral-map.tsv` now names the E1M SPI1/SPI2 pads after RSCI2/RSCI3 (`RSCI2_MOSI2`, `RSCI3_SCK3`, ...) instead of the RSPI1/RSPI2 names that do not exist on those pads, with P14/P60/P61 recorded as GPIO chip selects. The file also records function 5 for CANFD2/CANFD3 and the non-3.3 V-tolerant pads (P2x, P90-P92, PBx). `core-ownership.yaml` gains 70 cited A55 rows marked BENCH-PENDING (on-module pads only; carrier-only claims are left out) and an `a55_only_resources` list (DRP-AI3 with GIC SPI 914-917, P80/P95/PA6 on V2M, USB, PCIe0, CRU/CSI/ISP); the P95 port-9 read-modify-write hazard stays visible. P50/P51, RSPI0 and CAN-FD get no row because their owner is still an open maintainer decision. `riic1` and `i2s0` keep `driver_status: none` (no CM33 driver) with the A55 use in `notes`, and the carrier `mclk` comment no longer equates the E1M AUDIO_CLK pad with Audio_CLKB. Nothing here is bench-verified.
