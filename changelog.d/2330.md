### Added — the on-module TMP112 temperature sensor is exposed as a Linux hwmon (#2330)

The TI TMP112 on BRD_I2C (`i2c-8`, 0x40) on every E1M-V2N/V2M SoM now binds the upstream `lm75` hwmon driver:
- `temperature-sensor@40` (`ti,tmp112`) in `e1m-v2n-som.dtsi`
- `CONFIG_SENSORS_LM75=y` via the new `tmp112-hwmon.cfg` fragment

`/sys/class/hwmon/hwmonN/name` reads `tmp112`, and `temp1_input` reports the module temperature in millidegrees Celsius.
