### Fixed — AEN I2C pinctrl comments contradicted each other on pull direction, and an E8-only bench citation leaked into every AEN board tree (#2046)

`scripts/gen_zephyr_board.py`'s two on-module I2C pinctrl groups explained
the same DSC pull-direction encoding two different ways. The BRD_I2C group
said `bias-pull-up` is DSC=1, a real pull-up; the `e1m_i2c0` group said the
opposite — that upstream `pinctrl_soc.h`'s encoding "reads inverted vs the
Alif pad hardware" and that `bias-pull-up` gives a dead bus, a claim
`examples/aen/aen-eeprom-manifest`'s overlay repeated verbatim. Reading
Zephyr's actual `soc/alif/ensemble/pinctrl_soc.h` settles it: its
`ALIF_PINCTRL_BIAS_CFG()` macro (lines 44-48) maps `bias-pull-up` -> DSC=1
and `bias-pull-down` -> DSC=2 unconditionally, exactly matching the field's
own comment at line 24 — there is no inversion anywhere in that file. The
BRD_I2C group's reading was right; the `e1m_i2c0` group's "inverted
encoding" explanation was not, and is retracted. The dead-bus report that
explanation was invented to justify is left UNCONFIRMED, not disproven —
nobody has re-run `bias-pull-up` on that bus in a controlled trial since —
because an honest open question is the right outcome here, not a second
invented explanation. Both groups (`scripts/gen_zephyr_board.py:1101` and
`scripts/gen_zephyr_board.py:1172`), the checked-in `E1M-AEN801` board
trees, the three verbatim-copied example overlays (`aen-eeprom-manifest`,
`aen-i2c2-eeprom-regcheck`, `aen-eeprom-provision`), and
`docs/aen-bench-bringup.md`'s matching bullet now carry the corrected
explanation. No emitted pad VALUE changed: `bias-pull-up` on P7_0/P7_1 and
`bias-pull-down` on P5_6/P5_7 are unchanged and still bench-proven correct.

Separately, the `e1m_i2c0` group cited "bench-validated 2026-06-15 on the
E8" unconditionally, so an `E1M-AEN301` (E3) board tree would carry an
E8-only bench result as if it were family-wide the moment E3 gains a
`zephyr_peripherals_dtsi` — the same class of leak #1988 fixed for
`rtc_alarm.risk` with a per-part map.
`metadata/e1m_modules/aen/on-module-links.yaml:183-186`'s `e1m_i2c0` entry
now carries a `bench_validation:` map keyed by the SoC's own `part`
designator, same pattern as `rtc_alarm.risk`. Unlike that fix, a part with
no entry does NOT get silence: the E8 citation still appears, qualified
with an explicit "NOT been independently repeated on the `<part>`"
statement, because the emitted pad value is unchanged and correct for
every part — only the claim of which silicon it was measured on was wrong.
Found while hand-porting alp-sdk#2036 into tan-cli
(alplabai/tan-cli#1251), where tan's own regression guard against E8 facts
in non-E8 trees tripped on the citation.
