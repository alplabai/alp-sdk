### Fixed — `aen-evk-demo` no longer leaves both TAS2563 amps in hardware shutdown (#2164)

Phase 11's idle restore drove `AMP_ENABLE` / `SD_N` (`P5_2`, `gpio5`) LOW on the
way out. A pin driven low keeps `SD_N` asserted after the app exits, so both
TAS2563s stayed in **hardware** shutdown — and a part in hardware shutdown does
not ACK on I2C. Every later image that scanned the carrier bus therefore
reported the two amps as missing hardware, with no indication why.

Measured on `e1m-aen-evk-03`, same resident diagnostics image both times, the
only variable being the state of `P5_2`: `alp i2c scan 0` returned 12
responders after a run of this phase (`0x40 0x41 0x42 0x47 0x49 0x4a 0x4b 0x50
0x58 0x68 0x69 0x73`, with `E: NACK on i2c@49012000` / `E: IO Fail on
i2c@49012000`), and 15 after a reset released the pin — `0x48`, `0x4d` and
`0x4e` all returning together and the NACK lines gone. The parts were never
faulty: a run that pulses `SD_N` low-then-high reported
`tas2563_init(0x4d) -> 0`, `tas2563_init(0x4e) -> 0`, fault words
`0x00000000` on both, and `2/2 amp(s) up, no fault`.

`0x48` is the TAS2563 *broadcast* address, not a third device — all three
addresses belong to the same two chips, which is what distinguished this from
a bus-wide fault.

The idle restore now RELEASES the pin instead
(`examples/aen/aen-evk-demo/src/main.c:4621`
("RELEASE AMP_ENABLE; do not drive it low")): configuring it as an input stops
driving it, so `R138`'s 10 kOhm pull-up to `+VIO` defines the resting level
exactly as it does at power-on. An actively driven low overrides that pull-up,
which is why "the pull-up will release them" did not hold before. The amps are
deinitialised just above, and nothing this phase leaves behind needs them held
in hardware shutdown.

The `ok_amps == 0` failure path did the same thing and is fixed the same way
(`examples/aen/aen-evk-demo/src/main.c:4342`
("gpio_pin_configure(gpio5, AMP_ENABLE_PIN, GPIO_INPUT)")) — leaving `SD_N`
asserted on the way out of a failure would make the next run's scan see absent
hardware and mask whatever actually went wrong.

Phase 11's step-13 header, which promised "AMP_ENABLE driven LOW", is corrected
to say what the pin is actually left as and why.

**Cost of the old behaviour, for the record:** a bench verification session
aborted reporting a suspected hardware failure on `e1m-aen-evk-03`, and a
second bench session was needed to establish the board was fine.
