### Fixed — the on-module TMP112 answers at `0x40`, not the declared `0x48` (#1978)

An I2C census on `E1M-AEN803` serial `2026W36-0002` found the on-module
temperature sensor ACKing at `0x40` while every metadata declaration in the tree
said `0x48`. It reproduced on two of two modules, so this is the part's actual
address on this SoM, not a one-board anomaly.

`0x48` was not an arbitrary guess — it is the TMP112's address when `ADD0` is
tied to `GND`, and it is the address most reference designs use. On the E1M-AEN
modules `ADD0` lands in the `0x40..0x43` row of the datasheet's address table
(TI SBOS473L Table 7-4) instead, which nothing in the tree had captured.

* `metadata/e1m_modules/E1M-AEN{301,401,501,601,701,801,803}.yaml` and
  `metadata/e1m_modules/aen/on-module-links.yaml` now declare
  `address_7bit: 0x40`, with the node named `tmp112@40`.
* `chips/tmp112/tmp112.c` and `include/alp/chips/tmp112.h` accept the
  `0x40..0x43` row (`TMP112_I2C_ADDR_ADDRVAR_*`) alongside the `0x48..0x4B` row
  the driver already knew, and the three-row Table 7-4 is documented at the
  enum rather than left implicit.

The address is the whole bug: a driver pointed at `0x48` on these modules gets
no ACK and reports the part missing, which is indistinguishable from a
depopulated or dead sensor.
