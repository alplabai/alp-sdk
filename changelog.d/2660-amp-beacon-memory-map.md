### Changed — one definition of the AMP liveness beacon, and the OpenAMP window moves into SoC metadata (#2660)

The beacon layout (magic, version, heartbeat, attach epoch; offsets from the end of the window) is defined once in `include/alp/protocol/amp_beacon.h` and used by the RPC backend, the `rpmsg-v2n` CM33 example and `firmware/alp-stock-shim`; the three copies are gone. The window itself (A55 `0x4F700000`, `0x900000` bytes, CM33-NS `0x9F700000`, rsctbl page `0x1000`) is the SoC `openamp_carveout` block in `metadata/socs/renesas/rzv2n/n44.json`: `gen_zephyr_board.py` emits the CM33 board `.dts` nodes from it, `scripts/gen_amp_window.py` generates the backend's `alp_amp_window.h`, and the new `check_amp_window.py` gate pins the Linux reserved-memory/UIO nodes, the board `.dts` and the header to it. No address changes; **not bench-verified**, ordered steps in `docs/HIL/amp-window-from-metadata.md`.
