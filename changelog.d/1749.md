### Fixed — cold-chain-monitor README sent every DEEPX customer to the wrong SKU (#1749)

`examples/ai/cold-chain-monitor/README.md` told a reader to "Flip `som.sku` in
`board.yaml` to `E1M-V2M101` for the DEEPX DX-M1 path" -- correct advice for an
`E1M-V2N101`/`E1M-V2N102` customer, but misleading for an `E1M-V2M102`
customer, whose SKU already carries the DEEPX DX-M1 NPU
(`metadata/socs/deepx/dx/m1.json`'s `variants[0].alp_module_skus` lists both
`E1M-V2M101` and `E1M-V2M102`; `metadata/e1m_modules/E1M-V2M102.yaml` sets
`on_module.npu: deepx_dxm1`, `inference.preferred_backend: deepx_dxm1`, and
`capabilities.deepx_dxm1: true`).

The sentence now states the SKU-neutral fact -- the DEEPX DX-M1 NPU is
populated on `E1M-V2M101`/`E1M-V2M102` and absent on `E1M-V2N101`/
`E1M-V2N102`, the same PCB without it -- and leaves the `som.sku` pick to the
reader either way.
