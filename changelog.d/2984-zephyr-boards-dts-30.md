### Fixed — zephyr: bound the E8 MCUboot MRAM write window to this core, drop orphan V2N/V2M overlays (#2984)

the Alif E8 MPU table (mpu_regions_e8.c) no longer maps the other core's slot0 as writable under CONFIG_BOOTLOADER_MCUBOOT. The M55-HE image previously got a privileged read-write MRAM window that included the HP slot0. The window is now split into the slot0 trailer sector plus reserved_partition..storage, and the neighbour's slot is read-only and non-executable. Removed six orphan zephyr/boards/alp_e1m_v2{n,m}10x_m33_sm.overlay files that referenced an undefined &mhu label and were never applied. The alp,i2c-handover binding now documents that the flag page must be non-cacheable. ensemble_e8_peripherals.dtsi marks the LP peripheral bank and dma2 as M55-HE only. (Refs #2984)
