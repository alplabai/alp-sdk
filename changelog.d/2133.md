### Fixed — `alif_pdm.c` ignored the Zephyr `dmic` contract; `<alp/audio.h>` PDM capture returned `ALP_OK` then read nothing (#2133)

`alp_audio_in_open()` + `alp_audio_in_start()` returned `ALP_OK` on the E1M-AEN801's HP PDM mics, then every `alp_audio_in_read()` timed out with an empty FIFO. `dmic_alif_pdm_configure()` (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c`) broke the standard Zephyr `dmic` API contract two ways: it read `req_chan_map_lo`'s low byte VERBATIM as the hardware channel-enable mask instead of decoding the standard `dmic_build_channel_map()` nibble encoding, and it never left the PDM clock-mode field `MICROPHONE_SLEEP` nor programmed per-channel FIR/gain. The portable `src/backends/audio/zephyr_drv.c` backend used the standard encoding correctly, so it was the one broken by the driver's divergence: for its 2-channel request the verbatim read produced `0x10` (hardware channel 4 only) instead of `0x03` (hardware channels 0 and 1, where the EVK's mics actually are).

Fixed by making the driver honour the contract itself: a dependency-free translation header, grounded in the register layout and HWRM 15.7.4.3.1's even/rising-odd/falling edge rule (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm_chanmap.h:21` ("hw_channel = (pdm_controller * 2) + lr")), replaces (not ORs) the channel-enable field and rejects a duplicate/out-of-order map the ISR cannot honour; per-channel FIR/gain defaults are primed in `configure()`, with the actual `PDM_MODE` write deferred to `DMIC_TRIGGER_START` so `configure()` alone cannot start the block sampling. `configure()` validates the requested `pcm_rate` against a HWRM/DFP-grounded clock-mode table (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:452` ("{ 8000U, PDM_MODE_STANDARD_VOICE_512_CLK_FRQ, 512000U },"), `zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:465` ("{ 16000U, PDM_MODE_HIGH_QUALITY_1024_CLK_FRQ, 1024000U },"), `zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:474` ("{ 32000U, PDM_MODE_WIDE_BANDWIDTH_AUDIO_1536_CLK_FRQ, 1536000U },"), and `zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:518` ("{ 48000U, PDM_MODE_FULL_BANDWIDTH_AUDIO_3071_CLK_FRQ, 3072000U },")) AND the board's own mic clock range, a board-level DT fact (`clk-frequency-min`/`clk-frequency-max` on `alif,alif-pdm.yaml`) rather than something the driver or an app invents -- the portable backend's own generic clock window is unchanged (`src/backends/audio/zephyr_drv.c:291` (".min_pdm_clk_freq = 1000000,")). HWRM 15.7.5.3.1's back-to-back `PDM_CTL0` write-spacing requirement is honoured by `pdm_ctl0_write_spacer()` (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:541` ("static void pdm_ctl0_write_spacer(const struct device *dev)")) before every `PDM_CONFIG_REGISTER` write; `pdm_force_sleep()` (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:563` ("static void pdm_force_sleep(const struct device *dev)")) forces the block's channel-enable and clock-mode fields inert on a genuine cold boot only, so a fresh image no longer silently inherits whatever mode/channels the PREVIOUS image left running.

**Drop reporting.** On `dev`, `alif_pdm.c` had no overflow detection at all: a slab-alloc miss, a delivery-queue overflow (oldest block evicted), or a genuine hardware FIFO overflow (`PDM_ERROR_IRQ`'s status-register bit 0, `zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm_reg.h:85` ("#define PDM_ERROR_IRQ_FIFO_OVERFLOW_STAT (0x1U << 0U)")) all passed silently. Fixed: each site now sets a new sticky `overrun` flag (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:377` ("bool overrun;")) -- the slab-alloc miss (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1279`), the hardware FIFO overflow (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1219`), an unplannable burst (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1442`), and the delivery-queue overflow (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1474`) -- checked by `dmic_alif_pdm_read()` before every `k_msgq_get()` so a caller gets `-EIO` instead of silently splicing the next block onto the gap (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1168` ("if (pdata->overrun) {")); the flag clears only in `DMIC_TRIGGER_STOP`, after it frees the in-progress block and drains the queue, so a restart without a prior `STOP` cannot erase the report. `DMIC_TRIGGER_START`/`dmic_alif_pdm_configure()`'s refuse/no-op/proceed decisions are pure functions, `pdm_decide_start()`/`pdm_configure_allowed()` (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm_trigger_state.h:56` and `zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm_trigger_state.h:93`), mirroring `dmic_mcux.c`'s `DMIC_STATE_ACTIVE`/`DMIC_STATE_ERROR` handling (ZEPHYR_BASE `zephyr/drivers/audio/dmic_mcux.c`): START refuses `-EIO` when unconfigured or still flagged `overrun`; `configure()` refuses `-EBUSY` while a capture is active, so a second `configure()` call can no longer force-sleep the hardware or swap `mem_slab` out from under an in-progress capture.

**Resume/suspend hygiene.** On `dev`, `PM_DEVICE_ACTION_SUSPEND` left `record_data`/`overrun`/the in-progress buffer/the queue exactly as an active session left them -- SUSPEND left capture running, so a post-resume `DMIC_TRIGGER_START` could silently NOOP over a block nothing was sampling into. Fixed: `pdm_hw_bringup(dev, cold_init)` (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1514` ("static int pdm_hw_bringup(const struct device *dev, bool cold_init)")) only force-sleeps on a genuine cold boot, so `PM_DEVICE_ACTION_RESUME` no longer wipes a prior session's hardware channel-enable field; `PM_DEVICE_ACTION_SUSPEND` (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1617` ("case PM_DEVICE_ACTION_SUSPEND: {")) now runs the same free/drain/record_data=0/overrun=false cleanup `DMIC_TRIGGER_STOP` does, and resets `clk_mode` to `PDM_MODE_MICROPHONE_SLEEP` (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1684`) as a fail-safe against the hardware channel-enable/FIR/GAIN registers NOT surviving the low-power transition -- a post-resume `DMIC_TRIGGER_START` now always refuses as "not configured" rather than risk proceeding over dead register state. Latent today (`CONFIG_PM_DEVICE` is off on every board this driver ships on).

**Errno mapping.** `alp_audio_in_read(..., timeout_ms=0)` reaches `k_msgq_get(K_NO_WAIT)`, which returns `-ENOMSG` for "nothing queued yet"; the shared `alp_status_from_zephyr_errno()` baseline has no arm for it and falls through to `ALP_ERR_IO` ("dropped data, sticky") instead of `ALP_ERR_TIMEOUT`. Fixed with a local override in `src/backends/audio/zephyr_drv.c`'s `errno_to_alp()` (`alp_status_from_zephyr_errno_ex()`, `src/backends/audio/zephyr_drv.c:140`), kept local rather than touching the shared baseline header to stay minimal against `fix/2132-audio-out-start-defers-until-first-block`'s concurrently in-flight edits to the same file's `out` paths.

**Gain clamp.** `pdm_set_ch_gain()`'s `ch_gain` is only meaningful in `PDM_CH_GAIN`'s 12-bit field; a caller-supplied value above `PDM_CH_GAIN_MAX` (`0xFFF`) used to be written unclamped, truncating to bits [11:0] -- `0x1000` exactly truncates to 0 (mutes the channel), the opposite of what a caller raising gain would expect. Since the function returns `void` (existing public API), it now clamps through `pdm_ch_gain_clamp()` (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm_reg.h:59` ("static inline uint32_t pdm_ch_gain_clamp(uint32_t ch_gain)")), called from `pdm_set_ch_gain()` (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:884` ("ch_gain = pdm_ch_gain_clamp(ch_gain);")) and from `pdm_apply_channel_defaults()`'s `cfg->channel_gain` application (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:608` ("pdm_set_ch_gain(dev, hw_ch, cfg->channel_gain);")), with a `LOG_WRN` instead of a silent truncation. `PDM_INIT`'s `BUILD_ASSERT` independently bounds the DT `channel-gain` property to `0x001..PDM_CH_GAIN_MAX` at build time (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1736` ("BUILD_ASSERT(DT_INST_PROP_OR(n, channel_gain, 0x200) >= 0x001 && \")); an inverted `clk-frequency-min`/`clk-frequency-max` DT range is independently caught the same way (`zephyr/drivers/audio/alif_pdm.c:1718` ("BUILD_ASSERT(!(DT_INST_NODE_HAS_PROP(n, clk_frequency_min) && \")).

`examples/aen/aen-pdm-mic-alif` moves onto the standard contract: it measures the ACHIEVED sample rate (frames delivered / elapsed wall-clock time, excluding the first read's startup latency) rather than only checking "PCM varies", requires it within ±5% of the requested rate, treats a driver-reported drop (`dmic_read()` returning `-EIO`) as an outright `FAIL` regardless of what the surviving blocks measured (`examples/aen/aen-pdm-mic-alif/src/main.c:414` ("verdict = "), `examples/aen/aen-pdm-mic-alif/src/main.c:423` ("verdict = "), and `examples/aen/aen-pdm-mic-alif/src/main.c:432` ("verdict = ")), and gates `PASS` on at least one channel's AC RMS clearing a documented floor above idle noise, derived directly from silicon (see below) rather than assumed (`examples/aen/aen-pdm-mic-alif/src/main.c:141` ("((4U * 15U * DT_PROP(PDM_NODE, channel_gain)) / PDM_GAIN_BASELINE_0X200)")) -- peak-to-peak is still measured and printed per channel but is no longer gated on, since a single transient spike can clear a peak-to-peak floor without sustained signal. `PASS` (`examples/aen/aen-pdm-mic-alif/src/main.c:440` ("verdict = ")) is worded as "above the idle-noise AC-RMS floor", not "live audio": this example has no controlled stimulus of its own, so the actual acoustic verification is the loopback below, scoped to mic ch0/ch1 only. `examples/aen/aen-evk-demo`'s sound-baseline loop reads `SOUND_BASELINE_BLOCKS + 1` blocks and skips the first (settling transient), preserving the full `SOUND_BASELINE_BLOCKS` in the baseline (`examples/aen/aen-evk-demo/src/main.c:4181` ("for (unsigned b = 0; b < SOUND_BASELINE_BLOCKS + 1u; b++) {")). That path needs a mic sample rate whose PDM clock is >= 1.2 MHz on this EVK: at the example's `SOUND_SAMPLE_RATE_HZ` (16000, mode 4's 1024 kHz clock) the board overlay's `clk-frequency-min = <1200000>` rejects it with `-EINVAL`, so the baseline/correlation path is exercised only once a mic-capable rate is available independent of the speaker rate (issue #2134, software resample). `<alp/audio.h>` documents the settling requirement generically -- discard at least the first block, and more if the backend documents a longer settling duration (`include/alp/audio.h:118` ("-- discard at least the first block, and more if the backend")) -- with the measured figure (see below) kept in the Alif binding, not the portable header.

New host-testable regression: `tests/unit/alif_pdm_chanmap` (12 cases) locks the channel-map translation against the portable backend's and the example's exact requests, plus `pdm_ch_gain_clamp()`'s boundary (`0xFFF` passes through unchanged; `0x1000` and `0xFFFFFFFF` both clamp to `PDM_CH_GAIN_MAX`) -- mutation-checked: removing the clamp (`return ch_gain;` unconditionally) fails exactly the two out-of-range cases, 10/12; restored, 12/12. `tests/unit/alif_pdm_trigger_state` (6 cases) locks `pdm_decide_start()`'s refuse/no-op/proceed decision and `pdm_configure_allowed()`'s active-capture refusal (`tests/unit/alif_pdm_trigger_state/src/test_alif_pdm_trigger_state.c:79` ("ZTEST(alif_pdm_trigger_state, test_configure_refused_while_active)") and `tests/unit/alif_pdm_trigger_state/src/test_alif_pdm_trigger_state.c:84` ("ZTEST(alif_pdm_trigger_state, test_configure_allowed_while_idle)")); mutation-checked per case. `tests/unit/errno_mapping` (`test_zephyr_enomsg_baseline_is_io_not_timeout`, `tests/unit/errno_mapping/src/test_errno_mapping.c:86`) pins the shared baseline's `-ENOMSG` answer; `tests/unit/audio_in_enomsg_timeout` (`test_nonblocking_empty_read_is_timeout_not_io`, `tests/unit/audio_in_enomsg_timeout/src/test_audio_in_enomsg_timeout.c:30`) exercises `zephyr_drv.c`'s override end to end against a fake DMIC backend -- deleting the override fails this test; restoring it passes.

**Silicon evidence, E1M-AEN803 serial 2026W36-0002.** Register-level configuration: `PDM_CONFIG_REGISTER` read back matching the requested channel map and mode, held throughout capture; the 16 kHz build's DT-range guard is confirmed (`dmic_configure -> -EINVAL`, register never written). 48 kHz mode 7: `measured_rate_hz=48000` exactly, `slab_missed=0`, `overrun=0`, no `-EIO` for the full run (commit `68a169977`). Acoustic capture at 48 kHz on mic ch0/ch1 (PDM controller 0) ONLY is separately verified by a speaker-to-mic loopback -- the `PROBE_LOOPBACK` mode of `examples/aen/aen-i2s-tas2563-probe` on branch `test/u46-i2s-tas2563-on-reworked-mux` (commit `56631094d`, issue #2143), NOT this driver's own example, which has no controlled stimulus: TAS2563 speakers (independently verified audible) played known tones at gain `0x200` (readback-confirmed); Goertzel-bin analysis (2 s windows, first 200 ms discarded) found the 1 kHz bin at 9.3/0.1 dB (ch0/ch1) in silence rising to 43.4/48.8 dB at volume 16 and 57.8/57.6 dB at volume 48, a 500 Hz bin at 54.7/56.6 dB that only lit up during the 500 Hz stimulus window (staying within -9.8..+5.3 dB everywhere else), and peak-to-peak (the probe's own `loop_chan_result_t.p2p`; per channel, 960 ms window, after `zephyr_drv.c`'s `dc_block_s16()` high-pass, hence not a multiple of 32) rising from 128/128 in silence to 266/292 at 1 kHz/volume 16 and 651/652 at 1 kHz/volume 48. **The D2 pair (HW 4/5) is register-level verified only, never acoustically tested.** An early raw hex dump of the capture-start transient (from the same unattended 30 s bench build) showed roughly 7 zero samples followed by ~25 samples of ring-down at 48 kHz -- under 1 ms total; documented in `zephyr/dts/bindings/audio/alif,alif-pdm.yaml`'s decimator-settling description (its comma-bearing filename isn't a checkable `path:NNN` citation).

The example's signal-level gate was re-derived from AC RMS instead of peak-to-peak: a probe loopback silence window measured peak-to-peak 545 on one channel with the room silent, above the prior 512 floor, from a transient spike rather than sustained signal. The new floor (`examples/aen/aen-pdm-mic-alif/src/main.c`'s `MIN_SIGNAL_RMS_AC_LSB` = 4 x 15 = 60 at gain `0x200`) is derived directly from this example's OWN idle measurement (E1M-AEN803 serial 2026W36-0002, 2026-09-15 19:51Z, 48 kHz, `channel-gain` `0x200`, raw `dmic_read()`, 3 runs / 12 channel readings over blocks 1-3, n=14400 samples/channel): `rms_ac` read 15 on every channel in every run (one earlier, interrupted attempt read 18-19), and every run read `RESULT INCONCLUSIVE` (idle only; ambient sound during those runs is unknown). **Still unmeasured on this example:** a `PASS` at a real sound level -- a PREDICTION from the probe loopback's AC-RMS estimate (roughly 20 in silence, 68 at volume 16, and 195 at volume 48, all above the 60 floor), not yet confirmed by a run of this example itself.
