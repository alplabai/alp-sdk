### Fixed — the u-boot 0002 patch was malformed (corrupt hunks) and GigaDevice xSPI NOR support was missing (#2264)

`meta-alp-sdk/recipes-bsp/u-boot/u-boot/0002-rzv2n-dev-ALP-E1M-production-boot.patch`
had two hunks with wrong `@@` line counts: the `board/renesas/rzv2n-dev/Kconfig`
hunk header claimed `-30,3 +30,27` while its body actually added 44 lines
after 3 lines of context, and the `include/configs/rzv2n-dev.h` hunk header
claimed `-70,7 +70,76` while its body removed one line and added 94 after 6
lines of context. `git apply` rejected the file outright ("corrupt patch");
GNU `patch` refused it too (`malformed patch at line 203` in dry-run rather
than a silent partial apply). Either way, the `CONFIG_ALP_E1M_FDTFILE`
Kconfig block never landed and `do_compile` failed with `error: expected '}'
before 'CONFIG_ALP_E1M_FDTFILE'`.

Regenerated properly: cloned `renesas-u-boot-cip` at the BSP's pinned SRCREV
(`bcf29d98547d448776573abe643ecbdcbaa44b7d`), applied meta-renesas's
"remove unused PMIC I2C initialization" patch and the ALP `0001` EEPROM-gated
DEEPX bring-up patch in order, hand-applied `0002`'s intended content (the two
malformed hunks byte-verified against the broken patch's own added lines; the
other two hunks' declared post-image blob hashes matched exactly, confirming
the reconstruction), committed with the original author/date/Signed-off-by
and commit message unchanged, and regenerated the file with `git
format-patch -1`. Verified: `git apply --check` and GNU `patch -p1
--dry-run` both succeed for the full series (PMIC removal, `0001`, the
regenerated `0002`) applied in order on a clean `bcf29d98` checkout.

Also enables `CONFIG_SPI_FLASH_GIGADEVICE` via a new `gigadevice-xspi.cfg`
fragment in the same recipe. Some production E1M V2N-family modules carry a
GigaDevice LX-family octal xSPI NOR in the "NOR flash (variant per SKU)"
slot (`docs/soms/v2n.md`) — bench-proven: U-Boot's `sf probe` detects the
part and reports the correct capacity once the symbol is on.
This U-Boot's `drivers/mtd/spi/spi-nor-ids.c` already carries the GigaDevice
LX-family JEDEC IDs upstream; only the vendor-select symbol was off in
`rzv2n-dev_defconfig`. KNOWN LIMIT (bench-proven): reads/writes crossing the
16 MiB boundary fail in this U-Boot's Renesas xSPI driver (`Read: ERROR 1`
at `0xFFFF00+0x200`) — boot content must stay below 16 MiB.
