### Changed — DRP-AI `alp_inference_invoke()` reports a failed job (#2980)

**`alp_inference_invoke()` on the DRP-AI3 backend now returns `ALP_ERR_TIMEOUT` or `ALP_ERR_IO` when the job failed, instead of always returning `ALP_OK`.** The vendor runtime's `Run()` is void, so after it returns the backend reads `DRPAI_GET_STATUS` on one extra open of `/dev/drpai0` (the ioctl takes the driver semaphore and, with shared-memory support, its lock, so it can wait up to 1000 ms more): a job still in RUN maps to `ALP_ERR_TIMEOUT`, a recorded DRP/AIMAC error, reset or suspend to `ALP_ERR_IO`, and a failed query to `ALP_ERR_TIMEOUT`, `ALP_ERR_BUSY` or `ALP_ERR_IO`. A `DRPAI_START` the driver rejects before running is not detected, and if the closed MERA2 runtime does not keep its fd open during `Run()` the driver re-inits the status on our open and the check degrades to `ALP_OK`. The host regression test `alp_test_inference_drpai_regression` covers each mapping.
