### Added — 1280x960 camera streaming (15 fps capture/encode, ~7.5 fps delivered, send-bound) on E1M-AEN801/AEN803 + OV5647, Stage A (#2286)

`examples/connectivity/camera-mjpeg-stream` gains a `CONFIG_CAMERA_MJPEG_STREAM_1280X960`
build variant (`Kconfig`, this directory) that streams the OV5647's existing 1280x960
centre-crop mode at 15 fps through `<alp/camera.h>` — no sensor register-table change; a
full-FOV 2x2-binned 1280x960 mode is Stage B, tracked separately. 1920x1080 and 2592x1944
stay out of scope: the ISP caps table stops at 1920x1080, and two NV12 frames at either size
do not fit the AEN's 4 MiB SRAM0 bank.

Three sizing changes make the larger frame fit SRAM0 alongside the JPEG output buffers:
`src/backends/camera/alif_isp_pico.c`'s raw ISP buffer count is now the
`ALP_SDK_CAMERA_ALIF_ISP_VBUF_COUNT` Kconfig (`zephyr/kconfigs/power-camera-display.kconfig`,
default 3, was a fixed `#define`) so the new
`boards/overlay-1280x960.conf` can drop it to 2 — safe here because this app
requests NV12 (no CPU-side RGB565 conversion holds a buffer); the video buffer pool is
resized to 2 x 1,843,200 B plus slack (`CONFIG_VIDEO_BUFFER_POOL_HEAP_SIZE=3751936`); and
`mjpeg_http.h`'s `MJPEG_HTTP_MAX_JPEG` rises to 160 KiB per buffer under the new Kconfig.
`src/main.c`'s synthetic-frame fallback (used when there's no real camera, e.g. native_sim,
or when the camera stalls) is compiled out entirely at this resolution — a second SRAM0
buffer at 1,843,200 B has no budget left once the ISP and JPEG buffers are paid for; a
stalled/absent camera at 1280x960 just stops publishing instead.

A `camera_mjpeg_stream.aen_1280x960` build-only twister scenario (`testcase.yaml`,
AEN801 and AEN803) keeps this variant compiling in CI. Build result (linker map, not a
bench run): SRAM0 usage 4,131,200 B / 4 MiB (98.50%), ending at 0x023f0980 — inside the
4 MiB bank (0x02000000-0x023FFFF0) and nowhere near the unpowered SRAM1 (0x02400000+); 0
compiler warnings with `-DCONFIG_COMPILER_WARNINGS_AS_ERRORS=y`. Bench run 243
(E1M-AEN803 2026W36-0001, see README.md's Stage A section) since measured 0 CSI/IPI
fatals, 0 JPEG buffer-full, 15 fps capture/encode (encode ~2 ms), JPEG 131-135 KB at
quality 60 against the 163,840 B cap, and ~7.5 fps / 997,544 B/s delivered to the host
(the HTTP send path, not capture/encode, is the bottleneck); the AE envelope for this
resolution is left to #2277.

### Fixed — 1280x960 camera stream: JPEG encoder buffer-full once AE converged, `/stream` dropping clients during the retry burst (#2286)

Bench run 242 (E1M-AEN803 + OV5647, 1280x960 @ 15 fps): capture stayed clean (0 CSI/IPI
fatals) and frames 0-11 encoded fine (~130 KB, dark scene while AE converged), then EVERY
later encode failed with `ALP_ERR_NOMEM` (`alp_jpeg_encode failed (rc=-7 ...)`) as the
scene brightened — quality 80 (the 640x480 default) routinely exceeded
`MJPEG_HTTP_MAX_JPEG` (160 KiB) at this pixel count, and 160 KiB has no SRAM0 headroom
left to grow (98.5% bank usage). Downstream, every `/stream` client got re-served one
stale frame and disconnected after ~2.1 s: `mjpeg_http.c`'s `wait_and_claim()` gave up
and dropped the client the moment its single `IDLE_TIMEOUT_S` (2 s) wait for a new frame
came up empty, even though the capture pipeline was still alive and about to recover.

`src/main.c` now starts 1280x960 encodes at quality 60 (comfortably under the cap for a
typical frame) and, on `ALP_ERR_NOMEM`, retries the same frame once at a lower quality
via the new `jpeg_quality_step_down()` (`src/jpeg_quality_ladder.h`, floor 40,
native_sim-tested in `tests/unit/mjpeg_quality_ladder`) instead of dropping it outright.
`src/mjpeg_http.c`'s `wait_and_claim()` now gives a `/stream` client up to
`STREAM_STALL_TIMEOUT_S` (30 s) total for the next frame, spent across multiple shorter
condvar waits instead of one — `IDLE_TIMEOUT_S` still bounds socket I/O, but no longer
also doubles as the patience for an encode-retry burst. `src/main.c` also gained a
once-a-second stats line (fps, cumulative encoded/failed/retried counts, this-window
JPEG size min/avg/max, encode ms, and `mjpeg_http.c`'s last send ms) so the next bench
run's numbers are readable straight off the console.

Both 640x480 and 1280x960 variants still build with 0 warnings under
`-DCONFIG_COMPILER_WARNINGS_AS_ERRORS=y`. Bench run 243 (E1M-AEN803 2026W36-0001)
re-ran against these fixes: 0 JPEG buffer-full, 0 dropped `/stream` clients, and the
encode-retry ladder never triggered (0 retries — every frame encoded under cap at
quality 60 on the first attempt); see the README's Stage A section.
