### Added — 1280x960@15fps camera streaming on E1M-AEN803 + OV5647, Stage A (#2286)

`examples/connectivity/camera-mjpeg-stream` gains a `CONFIG_CAMERA_MJPEG_STREAM_1280X960`
build variant (`Kconfig`, this directory) that streams the OV5647's existing 1280x960
centre-crop mode at 15 fps through `<alp/camera.h>` — no sensor register-table change; a
full-FOV 2x2-binned 1280x960 mode is Stage B, tracked separately. 1920x1080 and 2592x1944
stay out of scope: the ISP caps table stops at 1920x1080, and two NV12 frames at either size
do not fit the AEN's 4 MiB SRAM0 bank.

Three sizing changes make the larger frame fit SRAM0 alongside the JPEG output buffers:
`src/backends/camera/alif_isp_pico.c`'s raw ISP buffer count is now the
`ALP_SDK_CAMERA_ALIF_ISP_VBUF_COUNT` Kconfig (`zephyr/kconfigs/power-camera-display.kconfig`,
default 3, was a fixed `#define`) so the new
`boards/overlay-1280x960-aen803.conf` can drop it to 2 — safe here because this app
requests NV12 (no CPU-side RGB565 conversion holds a buffer); the video buffer pool is
resized to 2 x 1,843,200 B plus slack (`CONFIG_VIDEO_BUFFER_POOL_HEAP_SIZE=3751936`); and
`mjpeg_http.h`'s `MJPEG_HTTP_MAX_JPEG` rises to 160 KiB per buffer under the new Kconfig.
`src/main.c`'s synthetic-frame fallback (used when there's no real camera, e.g. native_sim,
or when the camera stalls) is compiled out entirely at this resolution — a second SRAM0
buffer at 1,843,200 B has no budget left once the ISP and JPEG buffers are paid for; a
stalled/absent camera at 1280x960 just stops publishing instead.

A `camera_mjpeg_stream.aen_1280x960` build-only twister scenario (`testcase.yaml`,
AEN803 only) keeps this variant compiling in CI. Bench-verified: SRAM0 usage 4,131,200 B /
4 MiB (98.50%), ending at 0x023f0980 — inside the 4 MiB bank (0x02000000-0x023FFFF0) and
nowhere near the unpowered SRAM1 (0x02400000+); 0 compiler warnings with
`-DCONFIG_COMPILER_WARNINGS_AS_ERRORS=y`. Not yet bench-run on real hardware — no
fps/JPEG-size/CSI-fatal numbers exist for this variant yet (see the README's Stage A
section); the AE envelope for this resolution is left to #2277.
