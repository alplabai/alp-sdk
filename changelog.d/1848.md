### Fixed — E1M-AEN801's BRD_I2C is SoC I2C0, not the slave-only LPI2C0; unblocks the on-module `rv3028c7` RTC (#1848, unblocks #1814)

`BRD_I2C` (the on-module housekeeping bus carrying the RV-3028-C7 RTC, the
OPTIGA Trust M secure element, and the TMP112 temperature sensor) was
documented, and wired nowhere, on the assumption that it sat on the Alif
LPI2C0 (`P7_4`/`P7_5`) -- a bus the SoC can only be a *slave* on (TRM
§3.17.4, hardcoded slave address `0x40`), so those three parts were treated
as Tier-3 SE-mediated and unreachable as an ordinary I2C master.

That routing was wrong. `BRD_I2C_SCL`/`BRD_I2C_SDA` land on SoC pins
`P7_1`/`P7_0` (the `E1M-AEN-2626-R2` netlist, balls B3/B8); Alif's
`ADTS0013` v1.2 Table 3-16 gives those pads the alternate function
`I2C0_SCL_C`/`I2C0_SDA_C` -- **SoC I2C0**, one of the four shared-peripheral
I2C modules the HWRM (§15.4.1) documents as master-or-slave capable, not
LPI2C0. The M55 masters BRD_I2C directly, the same as any Tier-1 upstream
`i2c_dw` bus.

**Not done here, and said plainly:** this is bus plumbing, not RTC-source
selection. The AEN calendar backend (`src/backends/rtc/lprtc_calendar_shim.c`,
selected by `silicon_ref` on `alif:ensemble:e8`) still wins dispatch over the
now-reachable `rv3028c7` -- wiring the RTC dispatcher to prefer the external
part when BRD_I2C is present, with the internal LPRTC as documented fallback,
is a separate, larger change and is not part of this fix.

**Verification status: R2-sourced, NOT bench-verified.** Every routing fact
above comes from the `E1M-AEN-2626-R2` netlist + `ADTS0013` v1.2.
`alp-sdk-internal` holds no R1 netlist, and the only AEN801 bench unit on
hand identifies as r1 (`alp board` -> `E1M-AEN801 r1`, serial `2617-0001`).
Probe `P7_0`/`P7_1` on that unit before treating BRD_I2C as confirmed there.
`OPTIGA` also lives on this bus at `0x30` -- do not blind-scan it; probe
known addresses only.
