### Fixed — a model tensor with rank > 4 was silently truncated to rank 4 by the ONNX Runtime and DEEPX DX-M1 backends (#1729)

`alp_inference_tensor_t`'s `shape` field has exactly 4 slots. Both
`src/yocto/inference_ort.cpp` and `src/yocto/inference_deepx.cpp` used to hand
back a tensor descriptor truncated to its first 4 dims whenever a real tensor's
rank exceeded that — sized correctly via `size_bytes`, but with a `shape[]`
that no longer matched the model and no signal to the caller that anything
was wrong. Confidently wrong tensor geometry is worse than a refusal.

Both backends now reject such a model at `alp_inference_open()` with
`ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` instead: ORT's `_gather_tensor_info()` refuses any tensor
whose `GetDimensionsCount()` exceeds 4 before ever reaching the truncating
accessors, and DEEPX's `open()` checks every input/output tensor's
`shape().size()` right after `GetInputs()`/`GetOutputs()`, before staging any
buffers. `include/alp/inference.h`'s `alp_inference_open()` doc and
`docs/tutorials/16-inference-mobilenet.md`'s troubleshooting section both
document the new refusal.
