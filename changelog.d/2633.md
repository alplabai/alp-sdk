### Added — V2N/V2M Linux camera devicetree and kernel config generated from metadata (#2633)

`scripts/gen_camera_dt.py` writes one `e1m-x-evk-<connector>-<module_id>.dtsi` per camera module and carrier camera connector the V2N/V2M SoMs can host, plus `camera-sensors.cfg` and the supported-camera table in `docs/v2n-camera-csi.md`, from `metadata/camera_modules/*.yaml`, the chip `drivers.linux` block, the carrier `camera_connectors`, the SoM `pad_routes` and the SoC `linux_dt`. Adding a camera is now a metadata-only change. A TBD or missing route becomes a `GAP` comment in the fragment and fails `gen_camera_dt.py --check`, naming the missing field. `scripts/check_camera_parity.py` fails CI when a module lacks the Linux compatible, kconfig, link frequencies or BSP kernel driver its hosting SoMs need, when its lanes or I2C address do not fit the chip or carrier, or when a documented `ALP_CAMERA_<connector>` value is not a generated fragment.

The per-sensor `ALP_ENABLE_CAM0_IMX219` / `ALP_ENABLE_CAM0_OV9281` knobs are replaced by one `ALP_CAMERA_CAM0 = "<module_id>"` in `linux-renesas_%.bbappend`; an unknown value aborts the bake and lists the generated modules. `alp` emits it into `local.conf` from a project `cameras:` entry. The hand-written `e1m-x-evk-cam0-ov9281.dtsi`, `e1m-x-evk-cam0-imx219.dtsi` and `camera-csi.cfg` are deleted. The OV9281 fragment is property-for-property the bench-proven hand-written one (the generated file itself has not been built or booted); the IMX219 fragment now also sets the connector's `lane-polarities = <1 1 1>`. Fragments are also generated for the IMX335, OV5647 and IMX296 modules (their chips gain `v2n` / `v2n-m1` in `families`), all bench-unverified; the docs table has a bench-status column driven by the module's `linux_bench_verified_on`. `alp validate` rejects a `cameras:` entry whose module has no fragment for the project's Linux SoM.

### Changed — camera metadata (#2633)

SoC `linux_dt` entries gain optional `pinmux_macro` and `kconfig`. The OV9281 chip's Linux compatible is `ovti,ov9281` (patch 0020 matches it) with a matching `linux-kernel-drivers.yaml` entry. `E1M_X_GPIO_IO18` and `E1M_X_GPIO_IO20` on the V2N/V2M SoMs are `unrouted` instead of `TBD`: neither pad appears in the GD32 or Renesas pin maps, so no Linux GPIO controller reaches CAM0 enable or reset.
