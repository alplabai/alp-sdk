### Fixed -- UART read: RX overrun and ring overflow are reported, not silent (#2934)

**`alp_uart_read()` no longer returns `ALP_OK` with a corrupted frame on Zephyr.** The polled path now checks `uart_err_check()` and returns `ALP_ERR_IO` on an RX overrun, framing or parity error. While an RX ring buffer is attached, `alp_uart_read()` drains the ring and blocks on a semaphore signalled by the RX interrupt instead of polling the controller the ISR already owns. The ring's ISR counts the bytes it cannot store, and the next `alp_uart_rx_ringbuf_pop()` or ring-backed `alp_uart_read()` returns `ALP_ERR_IO` once for that loss. `alp_uart_read()` in `<alp/peripheral.h>` now documents the overrun behaviour and points at `alp_uart_rx_ringbuf_attach()` for bursts longer than the FIFO.
