### Changed

- Documented that `board.yaml` names a project target binding rather than a
  board description, and added a `board` glossary entry distinguishing the
  three things that word means in this repo — a Zephyr board target, a carrier
  preset under `metadata/boards/`, and the project file itself. The filename is
  unchanged: renaming it would touch roughly 1957 occurrences and break the
  `boardYaml` key that `build-plan-v1.schema.json` requires and that tan-cli and
  alp-sdk-vscode both consume (RFC #853).
