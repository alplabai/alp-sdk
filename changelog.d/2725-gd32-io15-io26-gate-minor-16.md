### Fixed

- GD32 bridge: gate E1M IO15/IO26 (bridge bits 21/22) on protocol minor 16 instead of 15 (#2725). A firmware reporting minor 15 may carry a 21-pad map that ignores bits 21/22 yet answers `STATUS_OK`, so the minor-15 gate granted lines the firmware lacks and writes silently "succeeded". `GD32G553_IO15_IO26_MIN_PROTOCOL_MINOR`, the Linux `gpio-gd32-bridge` patch 0005 (`GD32_PROTO_MINOR_IO_EXT`) and `min_bridge_protocol` in the six V2N/V2M SoM presets move together. Firmware must report 0.16 or newer for the pads to be granted; until it does they are refused (`ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` / `-ENODEV`), never silently accepted.
