### Fixed — the ethos_u adapter misread vela's summary CSV as bytes, so a small model's SRAM requirement could round down to zero (#2312)

`scripts/alp_model/adapters/ethos_u.py`'s `_parse_vela_summary()` treated
vela's `sram_memory_used` summary-CSV column as bytes (`sram_bytes // 1024`)
when it is already KiB — a 72 KiB model wrote `req_sram_kib = 0`, which passes
the on-device fit gate at `src/backends/inference/alp_model_select.c:95`
("return e->arena_sram_kib == 0u || t->req_sram_kib <= e->arena_sram_kib;")
against ANY arena. Fixed to read the column as KiB and round UP
(`math.ceil(sram_kib)`) at `scripts/alp_model/adapters/ethos_u.py:72`
("return round(sram_kib * 1024), math.ceil(sram_kib)"), matching the fix
already shipped in tan-cli#789 and tan-cli's `_footprint()`
(`python/tan/model/adapters/ethos_u.py`).

### Changed — `req_sram_kib` is arena-only by design; the const/weight region is never counted (#2312, tan-cli#1011)

Settles the open question from #2312 (follow-up to #1470): `perf.req_sram_kib`
/ the `.alpmodel` manifest's `Target.requires.sram_kib` count the compiled
tensor **arena** alone (vela's `sram_memory_used` column), never the
const/weight region (vela's `on_chip_flash_memory_used` column). The const
region is carried in the model blob itself and sized by the blob's own byte
length (`blob_len`), provisioned by the integrator per the target's
`vela_memory_mode` placement — it is never summed into `req_sram_kib`. No
schema field changed and no `.alpmodel` version bump; the on-device selector
at `src/backends/inference/alp_model_select.c:95`
("return e->arena_sram_kib == 0u || t->req_sram_kib <= e->arena_sram_kib;")
is unchanged, comparing the arena against the arena as it always has. This
mirrors tan-cli's recorded
contract (tan-cli#1011) and the SRAM-port pinning in
`src/backends/inference/ethos_u_aen.cpp`. Documented in
`docs/bench/model-perf-capture.md` and
`metadata/schemas/model-perf-v1.schema.json`'s `perf.req_sram_kib`
description.

### Documented — E1M-NX9101's DRAM capacity and vela system-config stay TBD, explicitly (#2312 item 2)

`vendors/nxp-imx93/README.md` now says outright that
`metadata/e1m_modules/E1M-NX9101.yaml`'s `memory.dram_mbit: TBD` is deliberate
pending the authoritative hardware config, and that until then the vela

`npu_toolchain.vela` sets no `system_config`). No values are set.
