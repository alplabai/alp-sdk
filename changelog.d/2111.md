### Fixed — the `alp companion wifi connect` console command budgeted a CC3501E STA connect against a stale ~40 s bound (#2111)

alp-sdk#2079 re-derived the CC3501E bridge firmware's real STA connect worst
case (10 s `Wlan_RoleUp` + 30 s L2 association + 30 s DHCP-lease poll = 70 s,
`hal/ti/cc3501e_hw_ti_wifi.c`, which documents 75000 ms as the caller budget
that clears it) and moved the bench apps that had it wrong onto 75000u. The
console command was not part of that sweep: `ALP_COMPANION_WIFI_CONN_MS`
stayed at `50000u` (before that, an even older comment claimed "~40s"), so a
genuinely slow-but-healthy association could still time out before the
firmware's own bound elapsed.

`src/zephyr/console/alp_console_companion_wifi.c`'s `ALP_COMPANION_WIFI_CONN_MS`
`50000u` → `75000u`, matching `SOCKTP_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS` /
`CONNTWICE_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS`. The same constant is also passed to
`cc3501e_wifi_ap_start()`; kept shared rather than split — the firmware's own
worst case there is a single 10 s `Wlan_RoleUp` (`CC3501E_WIFI_ROLE_TIMEOUT_MS`),
so 75 s is a harmless (if generous) upper bound on the confirmation poll, not
an incorrect one.

The same under-provisioned budget existed on two more callers wired to the
same CC3501E backend on E1M-AEN801 (`alp_wifi_connect()` routes to
`src/backends/wifi/cc3501e.c` there): `examples/connectivity/mqtt-telemetry`
`WIFI_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS` `10000u` → `75000u`, and
`examples/connectivity/iot-fleet-ota` / `examples/connectivity/iot-dashboard`
moved off an unnamed `10000u`/`5000` literal onto a named
`WIFI_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS` `75000u`. `examples/connectivity/iot-connected-camera`
targets E1M-V2N101 (Murata Wi-Fi, not CC3501E) and is unaffected.

`docs/cc3501e-bridge.md`'s bench table quoted the console's old
`ALP_COMPANION_WIFI_CONN_MS = 50000u` (50 s); updated to `75000u` (75 s).
Stale derivation comments quoting the pre-#2079 DHCP figure (100 tries / 20 s,
totals of 40 s / 60 s, or a `55000u`/`55 s` budget) in
`aen-cc3501e-socket-throughput`, `aen-cc3501e-connect-twice-probe`,
`aen-cc3501e-wedge-postmortem` (source and README), and
`aen-cc3501e-companion-tour` are corrected to the current 150-try / 30 s DHCP
figure and the 70 s / 75000 ms bound, without changing any of those files'
already-correct `75000u` values.
