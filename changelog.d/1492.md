### Fixed — `release.yml`'s Release-body draft is now bounded, and its docs stop claiming it's the final body (#1492)

The CHANGELOG-slice step in `.github/workflows/release.yml` always produced a
*draft* Release body -- a maintainer replaces it by hand with a lean summary
right after the tag lands (`docs/release-policy.md`'s release-cut procedure,
`alp-lab:cutting-a-release` steps 0/9) -- but nothing in the repo said so, and
nothing bounded the draft's size. A full `[Unreleased]` section already
exceeds GitHub's 125,000-char release-body limit routinely (`v0.16.0-rc1`'s
slice was 255,413 chars; `v0.15.0` was 159,174), and the
`softprops/action-gh-release@v3` step that publishes it enforces the limit by
silently `substring()`-ing to 124,999 chars right before the API call
(`truncateReleaseNotes()` in its own source) -- no warning, no log line, and a
cut landing wherever the char count happens to fall, mid-word or
mid-code-fence.

- `release.yml`'s slice step now checks the draft body's length itself: at or
  over the 125,000-char limit, it looks for the last complete CHANGELOG entry
  boundary (a `### ` heading, not a wrapped continuation line, and never one
  sitting inside an open ` ``` ` fence) below the limit. When that boundary
  keeps at least half the budget's worth of real content, it truncates
  there, appends a note pointing at `CHANGELOG.md` and the
  hand-written-summary step, and logs a `::warning::` naming the original
  and truncated lengths. When no such boundary exists -- the first entry
  alone is already oversized, or every candidate boundary is fenced off --
  the step **fails the job** instead of publishing a near-empty or
  fence-broken body.
- `.github/workflows/release.yml`'s header comment and
  `docs/release-policy.md`'s release-cut procedure (steps 7-8) now describe
  the real two-stage flow -- a **published** (not draft-state) auto-generated
  body, replaced by hand with a lean summary once the Release page exists --
  instead of documenting the auto-slice as the shipped state.

Scope is prose + the draft's size guard only: no artefact, checksum, SBOM, or
SLSA provenance is affected, and the existing missing-heading / empty-body
`fail(...)` guards and the `## [v<VERSION>]`-first-match contract
`scripts/bump_version.py` depends on are unchanged.

Deviations from #1492's suggested fix, both deliberate: item 1 (read the
notes from a dedicated per-release file, written and hard-required before
the tag push) is not adopted here -- the CHANGELOG slice stays the source,
per `alp-lab:cutting-a-release` steps 0/9 and `docs/release-policy.md` step
8, which already put the lean hand-written body on the Release page
immediately after it exists rather than before the tag. Item 2 (fail the
job on any oversized body) is adopted **only** when no safe entry boundary
exists below the limit -- an oversized-but-truncatable body (the common
case; every real release to date) still truncates-and-warns rather than
blocking the tag on a purely cosmetic overage of a body nobody is meant to
read in full.
