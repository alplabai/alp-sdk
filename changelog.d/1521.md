The "CI wiring" section, plus two other spots (the seam-1 coverage-table row
and a `seam2` placeholder claim), named `.github/workflows/parity.yml`'s
`seam1-plan-shape` job, its `PINNED_SDK_TAG` pin, and an inbound
`repository_dispatch` — none of which exist in alp-sdk. That file, job id,
pin, and dispatch all belong to **tan-cli's** `.github/workflows/parity.yml`.
alp-sdk's own half of the gate is `.github/workflows/parity-seam1.yml` (job
id `seam1`), path-filtered to `pull_request`/`push` on
`scripts/alp_orchestrate/**`, `metadata/**`, `examples/**/board.yaml`, and
`tests/parity/**` — not "on every pull request" — with no pin and no inbound
dispatch. alp-sdk is the dispatch *sender* (`dispatch-tan-parity.yml`, event
type `alp-sdk-planner-change`), not a receiver. The README, and the matching
stale reference in `tests/parity/seam1_field_diff.py`'s module docstring,
now describe alp-sdk's actual wiring and point at tan-cli by name for the
pin/dispatch/`seam2`-placeholder half.
