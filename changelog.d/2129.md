**`include/alp/boards/alp_e1m_evk.h:96-157`

netlist, the mux's `Y` pins land on the SAME nets the SoC's own
peripheral pins are wired to (U39 `3Y` = `E1M_CLK`, `4Y` = `E1M_CMD`,
`2Y` = `E1M_SDIO_RST`; U38 `1Y`..`4Y` = `E1M_D3`..`E1M_D0`). A 74LVC157
is UNIDIRECTIONAL and always actively drives `Y` -- the selected `A`/`B`
input at `/E` = LOW, forced LOW at `/E` = HIGH -- so it fights the SD
host controller's own CLK/CMD output (and the SD_RST GPIO output
`aen-sdcard-readout` drives on `E1M_SDIO_RST`) in EITHER state, not just
when disabled: SD, and SoC-to-amp I2S through the same-shape U46 mux,
can never work through the stock part at all. A 3257-type FET
bus-switch rework of U38/U39 -- now the standard fit going forward for
THOSE two ICs -- IS a genuine bidirectional switch: `/E` = LOW connects
(does not drive) the selected side, so the SoC's own driver passes
through cleanly, and `/E` = HIGH is real Hi-Z. That fixes SDIO (U38/U39,
both on `+3V3`) -- proven, SD reads 4-bit at 25 MHz, after a U38 solder
fix, on the hand-reworked E1M-AEN803 serial 2026W36-0002, though only with the
unmerged enable path (see the SD row correction below; dev's own SD
path is unverified until #2122 and the `test/2051-sdhc-enable-on-reworked-mux`
ADMA/SRAM0 work land).

**U46's failure mode was itself misdiagnosed for several passes of this
fix.** As-built, U46 is a `74LVC157ABQ,115`
(`E1M-EVK-2626-R2_components.csv`, internal repo) -- its `VCC` range is
1.2-3.6 V, so `+VIO` at 1.8 V is IN SPEC for it. Undervoltage was never
the stock part's problem: like U38/U39, it fails by DIRECTION (a
one-way mux whose `Y` outputs drive the SoC side), so it can NEVER pass
SoC-to-amp I2S at any `VCC` -- moving the stock part's `VCC` alone
fixes nothing. A working U46 needs the part REPLACED with a 3257-type
bus switch, same as U38/U39, though "the standard fit going forward"
does not (yet) apply to U46 the same way -- AND that switch's `VCC` on
a rail within ITS spec. A 3257-type part was fitted at U46 on
E1M-AEN803 serial 2026W36-0002 with `VCC` initially left on `+VIO`. `+VIO` is NOT a
carrier-selected rail -- it is the plugged-in SoM's own `VIO_OUT`
(`E2` pins P1/P2 feed `+VIO_C`, which reaches `+VIO` through U33's
shunt monitor). MEASURED with the E1M-AEN SoM (same unit)
(maintainer, 2026-09-15): `+VIO` = 1.8 V, below a 3257-type bus
switch's 2.3-3.6 V `VCC` spec (unlike the stock 74LVC157, which
tolerates 1.8 V), and with both amps ACTIVE, every I2S/I2C call
returned `ALP_OK` but the TAS2563s produced no audible output. On
that same unit (2026-09-15 ~14:05Z), the fitted 3257-type part's
`VCC` was moved from `+VIO` to `+3V3` between that silent run and an
audible run -- a continuous 1 kHz PROBE_LISTEN tone through I2S3 was
then clearly audible on both TAS2563 amps, confirmed by ear. This is
NOT established as the only difference between the two runs; MEASURED
is the rail move and the audibility change, INFERRED is that the rail
move caused it. SCOPE: only amp PLAYBACK audibility was checked this

VIH (~2.0 V) may not reliably register a 1.8 V HIGH on `/E` or `S` from

worked, but disabling the mux (`/E` HIGH) or selecting M.2 (`S` HIGH)

3257-type swap AND `VCC` on `+3V3` (subject to the `/E`/`S` HIGH
