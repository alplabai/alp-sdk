### Fixed — `EVK_PIN_SDIO_MUX_SEL` doc string and `evk_sdio_select_t` enum had the SDIO mux select polarity inverted (#2129)

`metadata/boards/e1m-evk.yaml:140` (the `EVK_PIN_SDIO_MUX_SEL` `doc:` field) said
"0 = M.2 E-key SDIO, 1 = microSD slot" -- the opposite of the carrier
netlist. `E1M-EVK-2626-R2_pinmap.csv` (internal repo, `grep -a ',U38,'`)
shows `SD_D3` on U38 pin 2 (`1|0`) and `M2E_SDIO_D3` on pin 3 (`1|1`); U39
carries the same shape for CLK/CMD. A 74LVC157/74LV3257's `x|0` inputs are
selected when `S` is LOW, so `S` LOW routes the microSD slot and `S` HIGH
routes the M.2 E-key, not the reverse the doc string claimed. Confirmed on
real hardware, not just the netlist: on `e1m-aen-evk-03`, header P18 open
pulls `S` low through `R27` (100k to `0V`) via `R198` (0 ohm to
`NetP18_2`), and a microSD card in `J7` enumerated and read a real sector 0

this hardware. `metadata/boards/e1m-evk.yaml:140`, the pin's IDE
hover doc string, is corrected and now carries the full r1/r2 split rather than just
r2: on r1 `S` IS firmware-drivable, via CC3501E `GPIO_30`

**`include/alp/boards/alp_e1m_evk.h:96-157`'s hand-written SDIO mux block
carried the same inverted `S` mapping, plus a deeper problem: it presented
`/E = 0` as a working route on the stock 74LVC157, which it never was.**
Per the authoritative 2626-R2 carrier netlist, the mux's `Y` pins land on
the SAME nets the SoC's own peripheral pins are wired to (U39 `3Y` =
`E1M_CLK`, `4Y` = `E1M_CMD`, `2Y` = `E1M_SDIO_RST`; U38 `1Y`..`4Y` =
`E1M_D3`..`E1M_D0`). A 74LVC157 is UNIDIRECTIONAL and always actively
drives `Y` -- the selected `A`/`B` input at `/E` = LOW, forced LOW at
`/E` = HIGH -- so it fights the SD host controller's own CLK/CMD output
(and the SD_RST GPIO output `aen-sdcard-readout` drives on
`E1M_SDIO_RST`) in EITHER state, not just when disabled: SD, and
SoC-to-amp I2S through the same-shape U46 mux, can never work through the
stock part at all. A 3257-type FET bus-switch rework of U38/U39 -- now
the standard fit going forward for THOSE two ICs -- IS a genuine
bidirectional switch: `/E` = LOW connects (does not drive) the selected
side, so the SoC's own driver passes through cleanly, and `/E` = HIGH is
real Hi-Z. That fixes SDIO (U38/U39, both on `+3V3`) -- proven, SD now
works in 4-bit mode.

**U46's failure mode was itself misdiagnosed until now.** As-built, U46
is a `74LVC157ABQ,115` (2626-R2 components list) -- its `VCC` range is
1.2-3.6 V, so `+VIO` at 1.8 V is IN SPEC for it. Undervoltage was never
the stock part's problem: like U38/U39, it fails by DIRECTION (a
one-way mux whose `Y` outputs drive the SoC side), so it can NEVER pass
SoC-to-amp I2S at any `VCC` -- moving the stock part's `VCC` alone
fixes nothing. A working U46 needs the part REPLACED with a 3257-type
bus switch, same as U38/U39, though "the standard fit going forward"
does not (yet) apply to U46 the same way -- AND that switch's `VCC` on
a rail within ITS spec. A 3257-type part was fitted at U46 on
`e1m-aen-evk-03` with `VCC` initially left on `+VIO`. `+VIO` is NOT a
carrier-selected rail -- it is the plugged-in SoM's own `VIO_OUT`
(`E2` pins P1/P2 feed `+VIO_C`, which reaches `+VIO` through U33's
shunt monitor). MEASURED with the E1M-AEN SoM on `e1m-aen-evk-03`
(maintainer, 2026-09-15): `+VIO` = 1.8 V, below a 3257-type bus
switch's 2.3-3.6 V `VCC` spec (unlike the stock 74LVC157, which
tolerates 1.8 V), and with both amps ACTIVE, every I2S/I2C call
returned `ALP_OK` but the TAS2563s produced no audible output.
**FIX VERIFIED ON SILICON** (`e1m-aen-evk-03`, maintainer,
2026-09-15 ~14:05Z): the fitted 3257-type part's `VCC` was re-wired
from `+VIO` to `+3V3`, and a continuous 1 kHz PROBE_LISTEN tone through
I2S3 was then clearly audible on both TAS2563 amps, confirmed by ear --
re-wiring `VCC` to `+3V3` is what made playback audible. NOT verified:
that this was the only difference between the two runs, or that
undervoltage was the sole cause. SCOPE: only amp PLAYBACK audibility
was verified this way -- PDM mic capture and M.2 E-key I2S through
this mux remain unverified. The SAME run also showed an open
`INT_LTCH0` bit 2 (TDM clock error) latch during playback and open
issue #2146 (amps auto-shut down ~1 s after I2S stops, stay off after
restart) -- a working mux does not mean the audio path is otherwise
clean. NEW CAVEAT, untested: at `VCC` = 3.3 V a CBT-type switch's
control-input VIH (~2.0 V) may not reliably register a 1.8 V HIGH on
`/E` or `S` from the CC3501E -- the amp path only needs both LOW,
which is why it worked, but disabling the mux (`/E` HIGH) or selecting
M.2 (`S` HIGH) may not switch reliably. A working U46 therefore needs
BOTH a 3257-type swap AND `VCC` on `+3V3` (VERIFIED above, subject to
the `/E`/`S` HIGH caveat and the open TDM-latch/#2146 findings) --
`VCC` alone is NOT the gate, since a stock 74LVC157 on `+3V3` still
fails by direction. The untested alternative is a switch rated for
1.8 V `VCC` -- e.g. TI TMUX1574 (1.5-5.5 V), which keeps the
SN74CBTLV3257 pin numbering ONLY in its TSSOP-16/SOT-23-THIN-16
packages (its UQFN pinout differs, and none of TMUX1574's packages fit
U46's NXP DHVQFN-16 2.5x3mm land pattern without an adapter or flying
leads) -- UNTESTED in-house. The SDIO block's truth table is now
scoped to "with a 3257-type switch fitted" rather than presented as
universal, and the I2S block (`:159-258`) gets the same per-part
contention wording for `/E` PLUS this `+VIO`/`+3V3` power-rail finding,
