### Fixed — IMX335 camera open no longer fails on the first boot after a power cycle (#2353)

On the first boot after every power cycle, opening the IMX335 through the
DW CSI-2 host failed with `D-PHY not locked to Stop-state. PHY status -
0x00010000`, and kept failing on later boots until one stream start had been
attempted. After a power-up the sensor's data lanes are not in LP-11 until it
has streamed once. The IMX335 driver patch now ends sensor init with a 20 ms
STANDBY 0 -> 1 pulse, which parks the lanes in LP-11 before any host
configures its D-PHY (`docs/camera-shields.md:774`
("5. **First boot after a power cycle fails the D-PHY check")).

Verified on E1M-AEN803 serial 2026W36-0001 with an INNO-MAKER CAM-IMX335-5MP:
`aen-camera-firstlight` RAM-run straight after a power cycle failed 2 of 2
times without the change and captured a clean 1296x972 frame 3 of 3 times
with it.
