### Fixed — the I2C address gate now checks a board's device list and its audio codecs as one bus, and sees broadcast addresses (#2348)

`scripts/check_i2c_address_uniqueness.py` checked a board preset's flat
`i2c_devices:` block and its `audio.codecs:` block separately, even when both
sit on the same bus. It could not see a codec strapped onto a monitor's
address. Board presets now declare `i2c_devices_bus:` (`E1M_I2C0` on the
E1M-EVK, `E1M_X_I2C0` on the E1M-X EVK). When a codec names that same
`i2c_bus`, the gate compares both blocks together.

The gate also reads broadcast addresses from each chip manifest's
`i2c.addresses[]`. A device strapped to a fitted chip's broadcast address is
reported as a collision, for example an INA236 at the TAS2563 global-call
`0x48`, whose CONFIG register the TAS2563 page-select write would hit. Chips
that only share a broadcast address do not collide with each other.

On the E1M-X EVK this flags U32 (INA236B, VCAM2) at `0x48`. That is the known
#2343 conflict: U32 is removed on the current build batch and re-strapped on
the next revision, so it is allowlisted under #2343. The two #1163 allowlist
entries are removed; the TMP112 moved to `0x40` and no longer collides.
