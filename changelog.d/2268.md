### Fixed — Hantro JPEG driver: output-buffer overrun, a leaked import slot, and a stale-completion race (#2268)

**Fixed an output-buffer overrun (SWREG9 was programmed from the raw buffer
capacity instead of relative to the SWREG8 base it actually bounds,
letting the encoder write past the caller's buffer), a permanently-leaked
video-buffer-pool slot on every encode timeout, and a stale-completion race
that could attribute one job's result to another.** A timeout now polls the
hardware's own `SW_ENC_E` bit before stopping the encoder and always
releases its pool slot instead of parking it; the completion semaphore and
latched status are cleared before every new encode and on stream stop --
closed for completions latched before re-arm, but a job the hardware never
finishes remains a known limit. `alp_jpeg_encode()`'s Doxygen now documents
`out_buf` as undefined after `ALP_ERR_TIMEOUT`. Full ownership map, SVD
citations, and bench evidence are in the PR description.
