### Added

- **E1M IO15 and IO26 as V2N/V2M bridge GPIO** — GD32 PB4 (IO15) and PC2 (IO26) are now host-controllable bridge GPIO bits 21/22 (bridge protocol 0.15, firmware 0.3.1). Added `GD32G553_GPIO_LINE_E1M_IO15`/`_IO26` and `GD32G553_IO15_IO26_MIN_PROTOCOL_MINOR`; `gd32g553_gpio_read`/`gd32g553_gpio_write` return `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` for either bit on a bridge below minor 15 (older firmware ignores the bits and reports success). The Linux `gpio-gd32-bridge` driver exports them as lines 22 (`IO15`) and 23 (`IO26`) and gates `.request()` on the same minor; line 21 stays `se-rst`. `PC14` is untouched.

### Fixed

- **SDK text said IO15 is unrouted on V2N** — corrected in the E1M-X EVK docs, board yaml and device-tree comments, and the V2N GD32 IO map README; `E1M_X_GPIO_IO15` and `E1M_X_GPIO_IO26` now carry `gd32_bridge` pad routes on all V2N/V2M SoMs. Not bench-verified: the HIL spec `v2m103-gd32-io15-io26-gpio` is read-only and needs firmware 0.3.1 flashed first.
