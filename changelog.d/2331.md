### Added — E1M-X-EVK carrier audio: TAS2563 ALSA card on V2N-family Linux (#2331)

`meta-alp-sdk/recipes-kernel/linux/linux-renesas/e1m-x-evk.dtsi` gains the
SoC audio side for the carrier's stereo TAS2563 smart-amp pair (U27 @0x4D
left, U28 @0x4E right, on I2C0 + I2S0 through the TMUX1574 path-mux): I2S0
pinctrl (SSIU1/SSI1 SCK/WS/SDATA at PFC function 7), `&audio_clkb {
clock-frequency = <24576000>; };` for the on-module 5L35023B SE3 24.576 MHz
feed, `&rcar_sound` enabled with a `rcar_sound,dai` playback link on `&ssi1`,
and the two `ti,tas2563` codec nodes under a `simple-audio-card` DAI link
(`simple-audio-card,codec@0`/`@1` -- the documented multi-codec pattern,
since the in-tree `audio-graph-card.yaml` at this BSP's kernel SHA (6717c06)
does not cover two codecs sharing one port). `tas2563-audio.cfg`
(`CONFIG_SND_SOC_TAS2562=y`) is re-added to `linux-renesas_%.bbappend` for
`e1m-v2n101`/`e1m-v2m101` -- removed under #1171 when the codec had no DT
consumer; it now does.

The kernel driver/pinctrl facts this needed were previously undocumented
in this repo (metadata/pinmux/v2n.yaml's `e1m_pad`/`e1m_function: "TBD"`
rows); they are now confirmed by reading the actual linux-renesas
6.1.141-cip43 kernel-source tree and the stock RZ/V2N EVK reference dts at
the same SHA (6717c06) -- see the dtsi's header comment for the citations.

Two things remain genuinely open, tracked on #2331: I2C-address ownership
between this kernel codec binding and the SDK's own userspace
`chips/tas2563` driver (no live conflict today -- no `alp-chips`
PACKAGECONFIG enables it on these MACHINEs), and the E1M-X module-pad
silkscreen crosswalk (metadata/pinmux/v2n.yaml's `e1m_pad` column, which
this change does not need but leaves unresolved). Untested on silicon.
