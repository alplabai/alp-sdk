### Fixed — `scripts/bench/aen/reread.sh` no longer shares fixed `/tmp` transcript paths across runs (#2317)

`reread.sh` wrote its J-Link command files and transcripts to fixed paths
(`/tmp/rr.jlink`, `/tmp/rr.out`, `/tmp/reread-preflight.{jlink,out}`). A
concurrent run, including the `tests/scripts` suite's fake-JLinkExe runs,
could overwrite a real read's transcript. It now uses a per-run
`mktemp -d "${TMPDIR:-/tmp}/reread.XXXXXX"` work dir, like `ram-run.sh`. The
dir is removed on success and kept on failure for inspection.

`tests/scripts/test_bench_jlink_connect_guard.py`'s read-back regex also
recognises `"$WORKDIR/..."` capture files. Without that, the moved read-back
would have dropped out of the every-read-back-asserts-the-connection check.
