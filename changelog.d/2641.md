### Fixed — `docs/recommended-libraries.md` no longer states a licence test that the schema rejects (#2641)

The "Evaluation principles" section listed "Apache-2.0, MIT, BSD, Zlib, ISC,
Boost" and allowed LGPL when linked dynamically on Yocto-only targets. The
enforced allowlist for `libraries:` manifests (`license.enum` in
`metadata/schemas/library-v1.schema.json`) admits neither `ISC` nor any
GPL-family licence, and it admits ids the doc did not list.

The doc now points at the "Licence allowlist" section of
`metadata/libraries/README.md` instead of carrying its own list, and says that
a licence outside it needs a maintainer legal review first. The allowlist
itself is unchanged.
