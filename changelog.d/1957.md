### Added — a gate enforcing that a changelog.d/ fragment's leading digits match the issue its own heading cites (#1957)

`changelog.d/README.md` documented a fragment filename's leading digits as the join key back to its GitHub issue, but nothing checked that a fragment's own `### ... (#N)` heading actually agreed with it. Three fragments on `dev` already disagreed: `changelog.d/1909.md`'s heading cited `(#1700)`, `changelog.d/1917.md`'s cited `(#1648)`, and `changelog.d/1932.md`'s cited `(#1909)` — the last of those is exactly what *caused* the collision #1941 exists to fix: the `1909.md` slot was occupied by the misfiled `#1700` fragment, so the real `#1909` fix had nowhere to go and was filed under the PR number instead.

`scripts/check_changelog_fragment_issue.py` now fails a PR the moment a fragment's heading cites exactly one issue number that disagrees with the filename's leading digits. It deliberately under-flags: a heading with no `(#N)` at all (`changelog.d/813.md`, `changelog.d/853.md`, `changelog.d/1652.md`, `changelog.d/1949.md`) or citing more than one distinct issue (a range, e.g. `changelog.d/1761.md`'s `(#1757-#1783)`, or a list) is left to hand review rather than guessed at — only the heading is scoped, never the body, where a fragment legitimately cites other issues in prose.

The three disagreeing fragments are renamed to their true issue numbers: `changelog.d/1909.md` -> `changelog.d/1700-seram-floor-diagnostic.md` (a disambiguating slug, legal since #1941's suffix rule, because `1700.md` is already taken), `changelog.d/1917.md` -> `changelog.d/1648-i2s-negotiated-rate.md` (same reason, `1648.md` is already taken), and `changelog.d/1932.md` -> `changelog.d/1909.md` (no slug needed -- renaming the misfiled `#1700` fragment out of that slot frees it). `scripts/assemble_changelog.py --dry-run`'s output is unchanged except for the reordering these three renames justify. `changelog.d/README.md`'s leading-digit rule, previously documented as "convention, not enforced" pending this gate, now says the join key is enforced.
