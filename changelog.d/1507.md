### Fixed — ADR-0020 index no longer claims the cross-repo dispatch trigger is outstanding (#1507)

`docs/adr/README.md`'s 0020 row said the cross-repo `repository_dispatch`
trigger "stays outstanding as a maintainer action", undated present tense --
but `.github/workflows/dispatch-tan-parity.yml` shipped it on 2026-07-28,
seven hours after the amendment it corrects was written, and fires on every
push to `dev`/`main` using a runtime-minted GitHub App token rather than the
long-lived PAT that amendment anticipated. The index row now points at a new
dated ADR-0020 amendment instead of restating the stale clause.
