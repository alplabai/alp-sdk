### Added — Linux GD32 bridge I2C3 proxy, polled irqchip, PWM provider, CAM_EN_LDO and CAN_STBY lines (#2727)

The kernel side of bridge protocol 0.17 for the V2N-family SoMs. `gpio-gd32-bridge` gains an I2C adapter for E1M-X I2C3 (GD32 `PC8`/`PC9`, `i2c3`), a polled interrupt controller (10 ms `GPIO_READ`, short edges are missed), `cam-en-ldo0..3` as gpio lines 24..27, a requestable `can-stby` (line 20), and a PWM provider for E1M `PWM0..7` over `PWM_SET`/`PWM_GET` (`#pwm-cells = <2>`, patch 0027). The PWM provider needs protocol >= 0.17 (enabling a channel on older firmware returns `-EOPNOTSUPP`; disable sends `PWM_SET` period 0 / duty 0, which older firmware misreads as a real period), and because `PWM0..3` share `TIMER0` and `PWM4..7` share `TIMER7`, enabling a channel with a period different from an enabled sibling returns `-EBUSY` instead of retuning it. Kernel consumers of the gpio lines and the PWM provider get `-EPROBE_DEFER` while the bridge is silent (userspace gets `-EAGAIN`), periods above 65536000 ns are rounded down to it, and I2C3 transfers wait up to 1 s for the bridge to answer. The I2C3 net has no pull-ups on the SoM, and on the X-EVK V2 carrier the J6 display I2C is not connected to it (carrier fix or bench bodge needed). See `docs/gd32-bridge-protocol.md`.
