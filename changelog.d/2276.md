### Fixed — AEN ISP-Pico camera backend ignored the caller's requested fps, always streaming at 10 fps (#2276)

`src/backends/camera/alif_isp_pico.c`'s `open()` hard-coded
`video_set_frmival(sensor_dev, {1, 10})` on the OV5647 shield path,
ignoring `alp_camera_config_t::fps` entirely — every `<alp/camera.h>`
stream through this backend ran at 10 fps no matter what an app asked
for. `examples/connectivity/camera-mjpeg-stream` set `ccfg.fps = 15` and
still measured 10 fps end to end (its own README's bench run 205).

Now requests `cfg->fps` (falling back to 10 fps, the same choice as
before, when `cfg->fps == 0` — this backend's own default, per
`<alp/camera.h>`'s `alp_camera_config_t::fps` doc, not the "must choose
or `alp_camera_open()` rejects" sentinel `width`/`height` use), reads the
effective rate back with `video_get_frmival()` (the OV5647 driver settles
on the closest of `ov5647_framerates[]` — 10/15/30/45/60/90/120 — it can
actually reach at the requested mode, VTS-clamped), and logs the
requested-vs-settled rate (`LOG_INF` when they differ, `LOG_DBG` when
they match), plus a `LOG_WRN` if `video_set_frmival()` itself fails or
the OV5647 device isn't ready.

`isp_pico.c`'s `isp_apply_ae()` derives its `int_time_max` ceiling from a
live `video_get_frmival()` read on every apply — confirmed on silicon
(bench run 220, E1M-AEN803 + OV5647, evk-02, bright daylight, 30 fps
request): boot readback `AE readback: int_time_max=32667 ...`, matching a
30 fps frame period, AE settling around intLine ~331-351, no `E:`/`W:`
log lines. But the OV5647 calibration AE block (`hal_alif` patches
`0006-isp-ov5647-ae-sensor-defaults.patch` /
`0011-isp-ov5647-ae-calib-envelope.patch`) still pins the exposure
ceiling to 3145 lines — the VTS for the sensor's 10 fps mode — and that
calibration block wins over `isp_apply_ae()`'s own push. In a dim scene
at 30 fps, AE may legitimately want to command an exposure longer than
one 30 fps frame period (VTS ~1049 lines), which the pinned 3145-line
ceiling doesn't reflect and which can lower the effective frame rate or
truncate the exposure AE wanted — unverified on silicon (run 220 was
bright daylight, so it never exercised this path); tracked in #2277, not
fixed here (fixing it means touching the `hal_alif` patches, out of this
PR's scope).

`examples/connectivity/camera-mjpeg-stream` now requests 30 fps
(`FRAME_FPS`, was a bare `ccfg.fps = 15` literal), confirmed end to end
at 30.03 fps by bench run 220 above; its README and file-header comment
are updated with that measurement.
`examples/aen/aen-isp-ov5647-viewfinder` pins `cfg.fps = 10` explicitly
instead of taking the new 30 fps default — its CPU YUV->RGB565
conversion headroom and dim-scene AE headroom are only bench-proven at
10 fps (runs 147/154), and it has not been re-benched at 30.

ignore `alp_camera_config_t::fps` entirely) and #2279 (`<alp/camera.h>`
has no way to report back the frame rate a backend actually settled on).
Refs #2277. Refs #2278. Refs #2279.

File-header comments in `alif_isp_pico.c` that called the hard-coded
10 fps a "floor" are corrected: 10 fps genuinely is the lowest entry in
`ov5647_framerates[]`, but calling it a floor implied the backend
couldn't run any other rate — it could always have requested a higher
one and simply never did. The comments now describe it as this
backend's own default choice (AE exposure headroom in a dim scene),
used only when the caller hasn't stated a preference.
