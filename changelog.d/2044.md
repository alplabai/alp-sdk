### Fixed — V2N/V2M CM33 supervisor now comes up by default (#2044)

`CONFIG_ALP_SDK_V2N_SUPERVISOR_SPI_BUS_ID` defaulted to `-1` and nothing set it, so on both in-tree RZ/V2N CM33 boards the GD32 supervisor singleton latched `ALP_ERR_NOT_READY` and every bridge-routed open (ADC/PWM/DAC/QENC/counter, ADC streams, TMU, TRNG poll, the Renesas power ext, `alp companion`) failed. It now defaults to `1` whenever the board enables the `alp-spi1` devicetree alias, which both `alp_e1m_v2n101_m33_sm` and `alp_e1m_v2m101_m33_sm` do (`alp-spi1 = &gd32_spi`). Boards without the alias keep `-1`.
