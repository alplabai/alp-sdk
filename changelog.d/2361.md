### Changed — the SoM serial grammar and serial-derived MAC layout live in one metadata file (#2361)

`metadata/identity/serial-mac.json` now holds:

- the serial pattern, year base, week range and Crockford index alphabet;
- the MAC's fixed first octet, the serial and eMMC-CID prefix nibbles, and the
  field widths;
- the published golden vectors.

`scripts/alp_eth_mac.py` loads its constants from that file instead of
restating them. `tests/scripts/test_alp_eth_mac.py` checks three things:

- the U-Boot patch 0010 C constants against it, now including the eMMC-CID
  prefix;
- that the fields fill the 40-bit payload;
- that every golden vector derives as published.
