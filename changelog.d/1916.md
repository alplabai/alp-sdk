### Documented — costed the in-tree APDU alternative for OPTIGA Trust M and recorded why it stays deferred (#1164)

`chips/optiga_trust_m/optiga_trust_m.c:139,149` still return `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT`
from `optiga_trust_m_send_apdu()` and `optiga_trust_m_read_product_info()` --
no behaviour changed here either. #1164 names two paths off the stub:
vendoring Infineon's host library, already costed in the driver's header
comment, or "an in-tree minimal APDU implementation of the handful of
commands actually needed." That second option had never been costed.

It now has. Upstream's own comms stack --
`src/comms/ifx_i2c/ifx_i2c_data_link_layer.c` (608 lines, an 11-state
retry/resend/ack/nack machine plus a CRC16 over every frame),
`ifx_i2c_presentation_layer.c` (1086 lines, the OpenApplication/
CloseApplication session handshake Trust M requires before any command APDU
runs), `ifx_i2c_transport_layer.c` (479 lines, cross-frame APDU chaining) --
sizes what "minimal" actually means: 3300+ lines of framed, stateful,
CRC-checked retry protocol, hand-derived from a wire spec rather than run
against upstream's tested implementation, for a security element with no
silicon in this dispatch to verify it against even once. That cost, and the
decision to defer both options rather than land either blind, is recorded as
this driver's own call at `chips/optiga_trust_m/optiga_trust_m.c:29-69` --
not attributed to #1164's comment thread, which in fact says the *transport*
is desk-work-feasible and leaves speculative-vs-wait as an open question,
not a ruling.

The two upstream facts the vendoring cost rests on -- no `zephyr/module.yml`
in Infineon's `optiga-trust-m` at `release-v5.8.1`, and 19,777 lines under
its `src/` + `include/` -- were re-verified independently against a fresh
clone of that tag rather than carried forward from the comment that first
stated them.

The callers/tests that pin the current `NOSUPPORT` contract are now recorded
next to it, so a future transport change moves all four in the same slice:
`tests/zephyr/chips/src/test_security.c:88,105`;
`examples/v2n/v2n-secure-element-sign/src/main.c:51,69`;
`examples/aen/aen-secure-element-sign/src/main.c:102,119`.
