### Removed — `coreMQTT-SN` is dropped: its upstream repository no longer exists (#1784)

`west.yml` pinned `coreMQTT-SN` to `v1.0.1` on the `freertos-org` remote, but
`github.com/FreeRTOS/coreMQTT-SN` has been deleted upstream. Anyone running
`west update --group-filter +extras-tier1` could not fetch it, so the whole
`extras-tier1` group was unfetchable rather than just this one library.
`examples/connectivity/mqtt-sn-publish/README.md` already recorded the
disappearance in prose while the manifest kept pointing at the dead remote.

A second break came with it: `metadata/libraries/coremqtt-sn.yaml` cited
`metadata/library-profiles/coremqtt_sn/`, a directory that does not exist.

Rather than repoint at an unofficial mirror of an abandoned library, the
library is removed: its west project and the now-unused `freertos-org` remote,
`metadata/libraries/coremqtt-sn.yaml`, the `coremqtt_sn` entry in
`metadata/library-aliases-v1.json`, the `ALP_MQTTSN_MBEDTLS` /
`ALP_MQTTSN_BEARSSL` / `ALP_MQTTSN_NO_TLS` Kconfig knobs, the
`examples/connectivity/mqtt-sn-publish` example, and its `ws6c-emit-parity`
fixture. `metadata/catalog.json` and `docs/portability-matrix.md` are
regenerated (the library × SKU cell counts move from 248/252 to 241/245 and
from 144/144 to 140/140; the four incompatible cells are unrelated and
unchanged).

MQTT itself is unaffected — `<alp/iot.h>`'s `alp_mqtt_*` surface and the
libmosquitto-backed Yocto implementation do not depend on this library. The
`VERSIONS.md` roadmap line offering MQTT-SN as a future secondary protocol on
that same surface also stands: it describes an Alp Lab API we have not built
yet, not this third-party package.
