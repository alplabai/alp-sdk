### Fixed — AEN pad map wrongly claimed Alif ownership of the SoM backlight LED pins (#2203)

`metadata/e1m_modules/aen/from-alif.tsv` and the generated
`metadata/pinmux/aen.yaml` carried a row for E1M pad A31 (`BL_LED_A`)
claiming it was Alif-owned, driven by silicon pad `P5_5` / peripheral
`UT2_T1_B`. Per the authoritative SoM netlist, A31 and its partner B31
(`BL_LED_K`, not in the map at all) are the LED output/feedback pair of the
E1M-AEN family's on-module backlight driver — a boost-converter output far
above logic level, reaching the panel through the carrier's display
connector directly from the driver, never through the SoC. `P5_5` is that
driver's enable input, a SoM-internal signal with no route to any E1M edge
pad. Anything consuming the pad map — pin-conflict checking, the generated
pin tables, an app or carrier design following the docs — was told A31 was
a free Alif GPIO/PWM pad, which risked a carrier wiring a logic-level signal
onto the backlight supply.

The A31 row is dropped from `from-alif.tsv`; A31/B31 now claim no Alif (or
other SoC) ownership anywhere in the generated pad map (AEN's pinmux table
drops from 96 to 95 pads, honestly reflecting that neither backlight pin is
silicon-backed). The underlying facts — the backlight driver's enable input
at `P5_5`, and A31/B31 as its LED output/feedback pair — are now recorded in
`metadata/e1m_modules/aen/on-module-links.yaml`, the family's existing home
for on-module wiring facts that don't belong in the edge-pad TSVs, alongside
a note explaining why this entry (unlike its neighbours) documents pads that
DO reach the E1M edge connector.

Found during `aen-dsi-display` (#2199) bring-up, where the backlight is lit
by driving `P5_5` directly rather than through any E1M-pad-derived route.
