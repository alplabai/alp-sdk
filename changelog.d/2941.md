### Fixed — `adc_alif` writes each `extra_samplings` round to its own buffer slot (#2941)

**An `adc_read()` with `extra_samplings` now fills the whole buffer.** `adc_context_update_buffer_pointer()` in `zephyr/drivers/adc/adc_alif.c` only handled the repeat case and never advanced the buffer between rounds, so every extra round overwrote the first `channels_count` words and the rest of the buffer was never written while `adc_read()` still returned 0. The pointer now advances by `channels_count` after each non-repeat round. Checked on E1M-AEN803 silicon with `extra_samplings = 3` on one channel: all four words are written and the guard word after the buffer is untouched.
