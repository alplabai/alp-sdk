### Documented — AEN803, 21/24 portability counts, and DRP-AI routing swept through the hand-written docs (#2244)

E1M-AEN803 shipped as the seventh AEN SKU (and the default bench SKU) and
the DRP-AI routing left the M-class (#2220), but the hand-written doc
enumerations still described the previous state. A sweep synced every
occurrence, verified against `metadata/` and the generated matrix blocks:

E1M-AEN803 now appears in the SoM-preset tables (`docs/board-config.md`,
`docs/tutorials/09-board-yaml-deep-dive.md`), the shipped board-tree lists
(`docs/getting-started.md`, `docs/vendor-partnerships.md`), the README
hardware table, `docs/os-support-matrix.md`, `docs/firmware-quickstart.md`,
and `docs/board-config-features.md`'s two AEN enumerations. The swap-test
counts are 21/24 E1M cells across 7 AEN SKUs (was 18/21 across 6) in
`README.md`, `docs/portability.md`, `docs/portability-matrix.md`'s
hand-maintained header, and `docs/tutorials/04-cross-family-portability.md`;
`docs/portability.md` no longer claims a byte-identical `alp.conf` (the
matrix makes a documented-expected-diffs claim, and AEN803 legitimately
emits `CONFIG_ALP_SDK_SOM_{DRAM,FLASH}_MBIT`). `docs/recommended-libraries.md`
stops calling DRP-AI a "Zephyr dispatch stub" and DEEPX a "Yocto dispatch
stub" (both A55/Yocto-only now), corrects its hw-backend coverage against
the manifests (22 of 35 declare the block, 15 carry gated entries, four
no-block rows removed from the table), and describes the ADR 0018 manifest
flow for adding a library. Example docs: `audio-noise-suppression` no longer
claims a ~3 ms DX-M1 invoke on its M33-only slice, `ai-camera-viewer` uses
the real `ALP_SDK_INFERENCE_*` knob names and its 240×320 ST7789 panel,
`examples/README.md`/`examples/aen/README.md` correct the aen directory
counts to 82/10 and add `aen-eeprom-provision` to the catalog, and the
`S-D.lib` misspelling of `§D.lib.loader` is fixed. `docs/glossary.md`'s
Ethos-U entry includes U85 on E4/E6/E8.
