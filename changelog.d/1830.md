### Fixed — DMA data buffers were never cache-maintained on the AEN E8, and the `<alp/peripheral.h>` SPI contract never said whose job it was (#1830)

The PL330 is programmed non-cacheable and non-snooping —
`dma_pl330_config_channel()` writes `CC_CCTRL_MODIFIABLE_VALUE = 0x2` into both
cache-control fields of `DMA_CCRn`, i.e. `AxCACHE = 0b0010`, Normal
non-cacheable non-bufferable (HWRM 12.2.4.3.15,
`zephyr/drivers/dma/dma_pl330_alif.c:219`). It therefore neither snoops nor
allocates, and software maintenance is mandatory for any buffer in cacheable
memory. Two of its consumers did none on caller data: `spi_dw_alif.c` held no
cache call at all, and `video_alif.c` had a single clean+invalidate at
*enqueue* — before the CPI writes the frame — and nothing after completion.
Neither direction produces an error; the wire carries pre-eviction bytes on TX
and the CPU reads pre-DMA bytes on RX.

Both are now maintained. `spi_dw_alif.c` cleans TX before `dma_start()`
(`:503`), clean+invalidates the RX destination before the transfer (`:431`),
and invalidates RX again once the transfer completes
(`zephyr/drivers/spi/spi_dw_alif.c:729`) — the second invalidate drops lines
speculative prefetch pulled in during the transfer window, which the
pre-transfer call cannot cover. `video_alif.c` invalidates the frame buffer on
the dequeue path (`zephyr/drivers/video/video_alif.c:842`). The policy this
settles is "buffers may be cacheable, the driver maintains them", not "callers
must allocate non-cacheable" — so no `BUILD_ASSERT` region check was added.

That policy is now written down where a caller can find it, on
`alp_spi_transceive` (`include/alp/peripheral.h:659` ("DMA and the data cache")), together with the one part that is **not** the driver's to fix:
maintenance operates on whole cache lines, so a partially-covered line is
invalidated in full. An `rx` buffer sharing a cache line with other live data
can lose a CPU write to that neighbour made during the transfer window. Give a
DMA-sized `rx` buffer its own cache line(s); `tx` is only ever cleaned, never
invalidated, so it is unaffected.
