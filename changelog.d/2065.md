### Fixed — the E1M-EVK rotary encoder part number named the wrong Bourns series (#2065)

`metadata/boards/e1m-evk.yaml:111` and `:258`, `metadata/templates/catalog-v1.json:87`
(and the generated `include/alp/boards/alp_e1m_evk_routes.h` they feed) named
the E1M-EVK's rotary encoder `PEC12R-4222F-S0024`. The 2626-R2 EVK schematic
netlist (`E1M-EVK-2626-R2_components.csv`, git-tracked in `alp-sdk-internal`
at `metadata/boards/e1m-evk/netlists/`, corroborated by a matching raw Altium
export) names designator `E1` as `PEC11R-4215K-S0024`, "Incremental encoder
24 detents 24 pulses", `Component Kind=Standard`. `PEC11R` and `PEC12R` are
different Bourns series; both option codes share the `S0024` (24 PPR) suffix
but differ in detent count, torque, shaft length/style, bushing, and switch
presence/debounce. The netlist export used here carries no DNP/fitted
column (every part reads `Standard`, `Standard (No BOM)`, or `Net Tie`), so
it establishes as-designed intent only, not as-assembled -- corroborated by
the separately git-tracked `E1M-EVK-2626-R2_components.csv` revision, which
does carry a DNP column and marks `E1` `DNP=0` (fitted) in the Alif variant.

`metadata/boards/e1m-x-evk.yaml` (`:60`, `:148`, `:247`) names `PEC12R-4222F`
for the **E1M-X EVK**, a different carrier board on a different netlist --
left unchanged; no netlist evidence was reviewed for that board in this
change.

`include/alp/boards/alp_e1m_evk_routes.h` regenerated via
`scripts/gen_board_header.py`; `tests/fixtures/emit-snapshots/proj-aen.
composed-route-table.snap` and `.../proj-aen.carrier-netlist.snap`
refreshed via `scripts/check_emit_snapshots.py --update` to match, byte-shape
unchanged apart from the corrected doc string.

Several other hand-authored mentions of the stale `PEC12R-4222F-S0024`
string for the E1M-EVK survive outside this change's scope (`docs/boards/
e1m-evk.md`, `docs/soms/aen.md`, `examples/aen/aen-qenc-readout/README.md`,
`examples/peripheral-io/gpio-button-led/board.yaml`, `examples/peripheral-io/
qenc-readout/{board.yaml,src/main.c}`, two board overlay comments, and
`scripts/alp_template.py`'s docstring) -- not touched here, left for a
follow-up sweep.
