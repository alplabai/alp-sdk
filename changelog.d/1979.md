### Documented — Alif's TF-A hardcodes the DevKit console UART in two places, and a carrier that routes a different UART needs both moved (#1979)

Recorded as a known gap in the vendor tree, not as a fix landed here.

`trusted-firmware-a_alif` @ `alif_lts-v2.10.8` does not take the BL32 console as
a build option. It is fixed to the DevKit-E7's UART2, and it is fixed **twice**:

- the register base, `PLAT_ALIF_BOOT_UART_BASE` / `PLAT_ALIF_RUN_UART_BASE` =
  `UL(0x4901A000)`, in
  `plat/alif/board/devkit_e7/common/include/platform_def.h`
- the pads, `pinconf_set(PORT_1, PIN_0, PINMUX_ALTERNATE_FUNCTION_1, ...)` and
  `pinconf_set(PORT_1, PIN_1, PINMUX_ALTERNATE_FUNCTION_1, ...)`, in
  `devkit_e7_pinmux_init()` in
  `plat/alif/board/devkit_e7/sp_min/devkit_e7_sp_min_setup.c`

A carrier that brings a different UART out to its console header has to move
**both**. Moving only one is the trap worth writing down: change the base and
leave the pads, and BL32 initialises a UART that works, runs, and drives pins
nobody wired to a connector; change the pads and leave the base, and the wired
pins are muxed to a peripheral nothing is writing to. Neither prints an error —
the boot is silent and looks like a dead A32 rather than a misrouted console,
which is a long way from the actual cause.

The A32 first light under #1972 was obtained with the two sites replaced by six
build-time knobs — `ALIF_CONSOLE_UART_BASE`, `ALIF_CONSOLE_RX_PORT`,
`ALIF_CONSOLE_RX_PIN`, `ALIF_CONSOLE_TX_PORT`, `ALIF_CONSOLE_TX_PIN`,
`ALIF_CONSOLE_PIN_FUNC` — so that the console could be pointed at
`0x4901D000` on `PORT_3` `PIN_4`/`PIN_5` with
`PINMUX_ALTERNATE_FUNCTION_2`. **Those knobs are a local patch to the vendor
tree.** They are not upstream, and they are not carried in this repo either, so
the BL32 build line quoted under #1972 is **not reproducible from a clean
checkout** — that is precisely what this issue exists to say. Anyone repeating
that build has to patch the two sites themselves, or the console goes to the
DevKit's UART2 regardless of what the carrier routes.
