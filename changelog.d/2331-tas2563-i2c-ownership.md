### Fixed

- **TAS2563 kernel/userspace I2C-address ownership documented and closed out (#2331 item 1).** The E1M-X-EVK carrier's `ti,tas2563` ALSA codec nodes bind i2c0@0x4d/0x4e; the SDK's own userspace `chips/tas2563` driver (over `alp_i2c_*`) must not also be wired up against the same bus without racing the kernel driver's registers. Resolved without a PACKAGECONFIG lock-out: `tas2563_init()` already propagates its bus call's status verbatim (#2077), so when a kernel driver holds the address, `ioctl(I2C_SLAVE)` fails `EBUSY` on the Yocto backend and `tas2563_init()` returns `ALP_ERR_BUSY` -- a clear, distinct refusal rather than a register race or a misleading `ALP_ERR_NOT_READY`. Documented on `tas2563_init()`'s `@retval ALP_ERR_BUSY`, in `metadata/chips/tas2563.yaml`, and in `e1m-x-evk.dtsi`'s audio comment. Zephyr/CM33 behaviour is unchanged -- no kernel client exists on those targets.
