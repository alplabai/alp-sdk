### Fixed — the Zephyr video and V2N N44 ISP camera backends now apply `alp_camera_config_t::fps` (#2278)

`zephyr_video.c` (the portable Zephyr video-class backend) and `v2n_n44_isp.c`
never read `cfg->fps`, so a caller's requested frame rate had no effect and
no diagnostic. Both now call `video_set_frmival()` with the request after
format negotiation, through a shared `camera_frmival.h` helper. `fps == 0`
keeps the driver's default. A driver with no settable rate, or one that
rejects the request, logs a warning and keeps streaming at its own rate, as
the AEN ISP-Pico backend already did.
