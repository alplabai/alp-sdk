### Added — RZ/V2N Cortex-M33 watchdog (WDT0) behind `<alp/wdt.h>`, bench-unverified (#2660)

The E1M-X V2N and V2M CM33 boards now declare `wdt0` (R_WDT0_BASE `0x41C00400`) and the alias `alp-wdt0`, `disabled` by default because an expiry resets the whole SoM; a product enables it with `&wdt0 { status = "okay"; }` and `alp_wdt_open()` reaches it through the existing Zephyr backend with no new public API. Upstream's `renesas,rz-wdt` driver cannot bind RZ/V2N (hal_renesas has no rzv `r_wdt`; its two builds are the RZ/A overflow timer and the RZ/T2M + RZ/N2L ICU path), so alp-sdk carries the rzt `r_wdt` adapted for WDT0 under `zephyr/drivers/watchdog/r_wdt/` and a thin glue driver for the new `renesas,rzv-wdt` compatible, the same way it carries the MHU-B port. Reset-only: interrupt-only expiry, `ALP_WDT_RESET_CPU`, a window and pause-on-debug answer `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT`, since hal_renesas names no WDT0 interrupt line for the CM33. `alp_sdk.wdt.rzv_cm33` builds both boards with the node enabled; nothing has run on silicon, and the WDT0 counting clock (24 MHz, inferred from the A55-side WDT1 period) and the CM33 clock hold (`renesas,cm33-owned-clocks`, on the unmerged ownership branch) are open. Ordered bench steps are in `docs/hil/rzv-wdt0-cm33.md`.
