### Added — pad-voltage tolerance gate, SoM-declared AUTO accelerator order, SoC die temperature

- **Pad voltage tolerance is SoC metadata, with a gate.** `metadata/socs/renesas/rzv2n/n44.json` gains `pad_tolerance` (RZ/V2N P90-P92, P2x and PBx are not 3.3 V tolerant, manual R01UH1071EJ0120 4.2.3.1.1 Note 1). Board presets may declare `pad_levels:` (`pad`, `signal_v`, `level_shifter`); the new `scripts/check_pad_voltage.py` (registry id `pad-voltage`, `pr-metadata-validate.yml`) fails a 3.3 V level on such a pad without a level shifter, and an `e1m_routes` bus enabling one with no declaration. No free-text tolerance notes existed to delete.
- **`inference.auto_order` in the SoM preset.** V2M101/102/103 declare `[deepx_dxm1, drpai, cpu]`, V2N101/102/103 `[drpai, cpu]`. The cmake-args emit passes `-DALP_SDK_INFERENCE_AUTO_ORDER=...`; `resolve_auto()` (Yocto) and the `.alpmodel` selector (new `alp_auto_order_parse()`, rank tiebreak) consume it, replacing the hard-coded order and the loader's `preferred_backend = AUTO` placeholder. `check_inference_backend_parity.py` validates the names, first == `preferred_backend`, `cpu` last. Public headers unchanged.
- **`alp_temperature_read_die_milli_c()`** (`[ABI-EXPERIMENTAL]`): SoC die temperature through the upstream Zephyr `die-temp0` alias and `SENSOR_CHAN_DIE_TEMP`; `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` where the SoC tree has no such node and on non-Zephyr builds.
