### Added — pad-voltage tolerance gate, SoM-declared AUTO accelerator order, SoC die temperature

- **Pad voltage tolerance is SoC metadata, with a gate.** `metadata/socs/renesas/rzv2n/n44.json` gains `pad_tolerance` (RZ/V2N P90-P92, P2x and PBx are not 3.3 V tolerant, manual R01UH1071EJ0120 4.2.3.1.1 Note 1, p.404). Board presets may declare `pad_levels:` (`pad`, `signal_v`, `level_shifter`); the new `scripts/check_pad_voltage.py` (registry id `pad-voltage`, `pr-metadata-validate.yml`) fails a 3.3 V level on such a pad without a level shifter, and an `e1m_routes` bus enabling one with no declaration. It scans board presets only, keyed by the SoC the preset hosts. No free-text tolerance notes existed to delete.
- **`inference.auto_order` in the SoM preset is the single source of the AUTO accelerator order.** V2M101/102/103 declare `[deepx_dxm1, drpai, cpu]`, V2N101/102/103 `[drpai, cpu]` and no longer carry `preferred_backend` (its first entry; the schema takes either, the parity gate rejects both). `--emit yocto-conf` writes `ALP_SDK_INFERENCE_AUTO_ORDER ?= "..."` into local.conf, the alp-sdk recipe forwards it to CMake, and `resolve_auto()` (Yocto) plus the `.alpmodel` selector (`alp_auto_order_parse()`, rank tiebreak) read it; absent keeps the built-in order. Zephyr and baremetal slices do not consume it, so the Zephyr `.alpmodel` loader tiebreak is unchanged. `check_inference_backend_parity.py` validates the names and requires `cpu` last. Public headers unchanged.
- **`alp_temperature_read_die_milli_c()`** (`[ABI-EXPERIMENTAL]`): SoC die temperature through the upstream Zephyr `die-temp0` alias and `SENSOR_CHAN_DIE_TEMP`; `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` where the build's devicetree has no such node (none of this repository's SoM trees declares it) and on non-Zephyr builds.
