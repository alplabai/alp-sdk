### Added — pad-voltage tolerance gate, SoM-declared AUTO accelerator order, SoC die temperature

- **Pad voltage tolerance is SoC metadata, with a gate.** `metadata/socs/renesas/rzv2n/n44.json` gains `pad_tolerance` (RZ/V2N P90-P92, P2x and PBx are not 3.3 V tolerant, manual R01UH1071EJ0120 4.2.3.1.1 Note 1, p.404). Board presets may declare `pad_levels:` keyed on the E1M route (`e1m`, `signal_v`, `level_shifter`), never on an SoC pad, so carriers stay SoM-agnostic. `scripts/check_pad_voltage.py` (registry id `pad-voltage`, `pr-metadata-validate.yml`) resolves every `e1m_routes` route of any class through the pinmux table and host SoC of each SoM family the board hosts (`som_families` is new in `metadata/pinmux/<family>.yaml`, generated by `gen_pinmux_capability.py`), and fails a route on a non-tolerant pad with no entry, or a level above the SoC `max_signal_v` without a `level_shifter`. No vendor literal in the gate. No free-text tolerance notes existed to delete.
- **`inference.auto_order` in the SoM preset is the single, required source of the AUTO accelerator order.** Every preset declares it (V2M `[deepx_dxm1, drpai, cpu]`, V2N `[drpai, cpu]`, AEN3xx-8xx and NX9101 `[ethos_u, cpu]`); `preferred_backend` is gone from the schema, presets and `check_inference_backend_parity.py` (the first `auto_order` entry is the preferred backend, and `ethos_u_variant` is required when it is `ethos_u`). One define, `ALP_SDK_INFERENCE_AUTO_ORDER`, reaches every OS build: `--emit yocto-conf` writes it into local.conf for the alp-sdk recipe, `--emit zephyr-conf` writes `CONFIG_ALP_SDK_INFERENCE_AUTO_ORDER` mapped by `zephyr/CMakeLists.txt`, and `src/baremetal/CMakeLists.txt` takes the same cache variable. Only the `.alpmodel` selector (`alp_auto_order_parse()`, rank tiebreak between fitting targets) consumes it; with no order the first fitting target wins. `check_inference_backend_parity.py` validates the names and requires `cpu` last. Public headers unchanged.
- **`alp_temperature_read_die_milli_c()` gains its Zephyr backend.** The function (one portable name for the SoC die temperature, `[ABI-EXPERIMENTAL]`, introduced with its Linux thermal-zone backend by the audit SYS-08 change) now also answers on Zephyr through the upstream `die-temp0` alias and `SENSOR_CHAN_DIE_TEMP`; `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` where the build's devicetree has no such node (none of this repository's SoM trees declares it) and on baremetal.
