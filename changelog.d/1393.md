### Fixed — ADR-0006 promised MCUboot swap-with-revert on AEN, but E1M-AEN801 ships single-slot; corrected in place with an amendment, not a rewrite (#1393)

`docs/adr/0006-secure-boot-secure-ota.md` told readers that the AEN OTA path
routes "through MCUboot's swap-with-revert dual-bank flow", that `apply()`
"writes to the secondary slot, swaps, reboots", and that the bootloader
"reverts on next reset". E1M-AEN801 has no secondary slot. Read from the board
device trees rather than from prose, both AEN801 targets declare five MRAM
partitions and neither a `slot1_partition` nor a `scratch_partition`:
`mcuboot` `0x80000000` 64 KiB, `image-0` (slot0) `0x80010000` on
`alp_e1m_aen801_m55_he` / `0x802b0000` on `alp_e1m_aen801_m55_hp` 2688 KiB,
`reserved` `0x80550000` 64 KiB, `storage` `0x80560000` 96 KiB, `atoc`
`0x80578000` 32 KiB — `64 + 2688 + 2688 + 64 + 96 + 32 = 5632 KiB`, the full
5.5 MiB App MRAM. Tree-wide, `slot1_partition` and `scratch_partition` survive
on exactly two board targets, `e1m_aen401_m55_hp` and `e1m_aen601_m55_hp`, and
on neither AEN801 target.

**The configuration was right and the ADR was what trailed it.** Commit
`1ad76193` ("fix(aen): disjoint per-core slot0 windows for E1M-AEN801 dual-M55
boot (#1069) (#1100)", 2026-08-03) removed both partitions deliberately, to fit
the disjoint dual-core slot0 budget: `0x802b0000` became HP's own slot0 (it was
slot1/OTA) and `0x80550000` became `reserved` (it was scratch), with OTA
deferred as part of the trade. `zephyr/sysbuild/aen/sysbuild.conf` already set
`SB_CONFIG_MCUBOOT_MODE_SINGLE_APP=y`, and
`examples/aen/aen-mcuboot-smoke/sysbuild.conf` already carried an explicit
`NO A/B YET ... the swap/upgrade path is UNTESTED`.

**Why this was worth correcting rather than leaving as tidy-up:**
revert-on-bad-image is a *security* property. A reader of the uncorrected
Decision section concludes a bad AEN image is reverted automatically on the
next reset; on a single-slot board there is no revert, because there is nothing
to revert *to*. Anyone designing an update strategy against this ADR was
designing against a guarantee the flash configuration does not provide. The one
bench attempt to confirm revert-on-bad-image (#1066) could not: its positive
control failed identically — a valid, byte-exact image B with a valid request
also did not swap — so that run supported no security claim either.

**The ADR is annotated, not rewritten.** An ADR is a record, so the original
Decision text stays and the correction is attached to it: inline `CORRECTED
2026-08-12 (item 7)` blockquotes on the two claims that mislead (the
"Zephyr backends" OTA bullet and the "Battery exhaustion" threat-model bullet,
where single-slot also removes the *stated mitigation* — a partially written
`image-0` fails verification and MCUboot stops at `E: Unable to find bootable
image`, recoverable over the debug port, not in the field), a supersession note
on Amendment item 2, and a new `## Amendment (2026-08-12)` item 7 carrying the
partition table, the `1ad76193` rationale, and the scope. Amendment items 5 and
6 are explicitly **not** retracted: the secure-*boot* half stays bench-proven —
single-slot removes the upgrade guarantee, not the verification one. `#1066`
is noted as re-scoped to blocking the re-enabling of OTA, since its mechanism
half (MCUboot's scratch magic never cleared on a no-erase MRAM device) cannot
be fixed or regression-tested while the board has one slot.

`docs/ota.md` gets the lighter touch its "v0.4 plan" framing earns: a note at
the head of the AEN-Zephyr section stating that the AEN801 flash map has no
secondary slot today, a matching clause in the doc's Status block, and step 3
of Option A ("Wires its image-write hook onto MCUboot's secondary slot") now
says what that step is blocked on. `examples/aen/aen-mcuboot-smoke/sysbuild.conf`
loses its one stale line — `slot1 @ 0x802b0000, scratch, storage` was
pre-#1100 language naming partitions the board no longer has, and
`0x802b0000` is now HP slot0; the rest of that file, including its `NO A/B YET`
note, was already accurate and is untouched.

**New gate, so prose cannot drift from the flash map again.**
`scripts/check_doc_drift.py` grows check (f), which reads
`zephyr/boards/alp/e1m_aen801_*/*.dts` and never hard-codes today's map.
(f1) fails any `<partition> @ 0x80......` claim in the AEN boot/OTA doc and
sysbuild surfaces, or a `` `<node>_partition` | `<label>` | `0x80......` ``
markdown table row (the ADR-0006 partition-map table shape itself is
checked, not just prose), that names a partition the DT does not
declare or puts an existing one at the wrong address — the
`slot1 @ 0x802b0000` line above is exactly what it catches. The
disjoint HE/HP slot0 pair is handled two ways: a freeform `slot0 @ 0x..`
claim is checked against the *set* of legal addresses across both
boards, but a table row whose header names a specific board per column
(the ADR-0006 table's own `` `alp_e1m_aen801_m55_he` ``/`` `_hp` ``
header cells) is checked POSITION-AWARE — each column against only the
board its header cell names — so transposing the HE and HP columns now
fails instead of passing on the (still-valid-somewhere) swapped
addresses. An UNBOUND row (no header, so no specific AEN801 board to
check against) naming `slot1_partition`/`scratch_partition`/`image-1` --
AEN801-illegal by design but real on `e1m_aen401_m55_hp` /
`e1m_aen601_m55_hp` -- is not reported unless the address matches no AEN
board's DT either, so a legitimate partition-map row for one of those
two targets in a broadly-scanned doc (e.g. `docs/boards/*.md`) is not
told "no such partition in the AEN801 board DT". (f2) fails a paragraph of
`docs/adr/0006-secure-boot-secure-ota.md` or `docs/ota.md` that promises
swap-with-revert / swap-using-scratch / a secondary slot without a
qualifier (`single-slot`, `no secondary slot`,
`CONFIG_SINGLE_APPLICATION_SLOT`, or a `#1069` / `#1100` / `#1393`
reference), and lifts itself the moment an AEN801 target regains a
`slot1`/`scratch` partition. `docs/secure-boot.md` is deliberately
outside (f2): it is the operator reference for the SWAP profile itself,
where the swap vocabulary is the subject rather than an AEN801 promise;
it names swap-using-scratch on seven lines, and three of its paragraphs
would be flagged by this scan if it were added here — two of those
three carry a qualifier nearby, the third (the "Omit the block to
inherit ... swap-using-scratch" stock-default line) does not — a live
gap this scan doesn't check, tracked separately at
github.com/alplabai/alp-sdk/issues/1413 (`secure.py`'s `swap_algorithm`
default of `scratch` builds against an AEN801 DT with no slot1/scratch
whenever a `boot:` block omits it). Both sub-checks stay inert only when
there is no AEN801 board tree to check at all (a synthetic test root); a real
checkout whose AEN801 board dirs go missing now fails loudly instead of
printing the affirmative "AEN801 MRAM map claims match the board DT"
line for a check that did not run.
Seventeen cases in `tests/scripts/test_check_doc_drift.py` cover both
sub-checks including the self-lifting behaviour, the no-board-tree
inert case, the table-row claim form (including the transposed-column
case position-awareness exists to catch and the sibling-AEN-board
carve-out), and the broken-board-tree loud failure; run against the
pre-fix `origin/dev` gate, the cases that assert a failure all fail
there.
