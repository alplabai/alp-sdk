### Changed — five more path-filtered workflows are safe to require: `generated-files · all`, `doxygen · all`, `tan-build · all`, `plain-cmake · all`, `abi-snapshot · all` (#2972)

`pr-generated-files.yml`, `pr-doxygen.yml`, `pr-tan-build.yml`, `pr-plain-cmake.yml` and `pr-abi-snapshot.yml` had `paths:` filters on their triggers, so requiring them would have blocked every PR touching no watched path (#1474) and, where `merge_group:` was missing, hung the merge queue (#1415). Each trigger is now unfiltered with the same path list in a `detect` job, has a `merge_group:` trigger, and reports through a static-named `if: always()` summary job that passes on success or a legitimate path-filter skip and fails otherwise. In the queue `abidiff` is skipped and `add_subdirectory()` consumer runs one leg to spare runners; the abi-snapshot diff stays PR-to-`main`/`release/**` only and its summary passes there with an explicit "not applicable" log line. `docs/ci/README.md` lists the contexts to require; the maintainer adds them to branch protection after this merges.

The `detect` jobs now check out the repo (full history) on `push` and `merge_group` and, on the queue, diff against `merge_group.base_sha`: without that, dorny/paths-filter failed with `not a git repository` on every push to `dev`/`main`, reddening `metadata-validate · all` and `python-smoke · all`, and the queue could not skip the heavy jobs for docs-only entries. A `merge_group` skip is a pass only when `detect` succeeded and found nothing relevant. `plain-cmake · all` is to be required on `main` only.
