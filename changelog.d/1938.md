### Fixed — `mqtt_drain_remaining()` treated `-EAGAIN` as terminal, re-wedging the connection #1918 was supposed to close (#1938)

`mqtt_drain_remaining()` in `src/backends/mqtt/zephyr_drv.c` stopped the first
time `mqtt_read_publish_payload()` returned a non-positive value, including
`-EAGAIN` — the ordinary answer when the next segment of an inbound PUBLISH
payload has not arrived yet, not a hard failure. Stopping there left Zephyr's
`mqtt_client::internal.remaining_payload` non-zero, so every subsequent
`client_read()` returned `-EBUSY` and the connection was wedged until torn
down — exactly the failure #1645 (landed as #1918) existed to close. It
reproduces on any payload larger than `CONFIG_ALP_SDK_MQTT_BUF_SIZE` that
arrives split across more than one TCP segment, which is the ordinary shape
of a large payload on a slow or congested link, not an edge case; a fast
loopback broker usually buffers the whole payload before the drain runs,
which is why it passed in testing.

Both call sites (the no-callback drain and the past-`rx_buf` overflow drain)
and the main `rx_buf`-filling read loop — which had the identical
first-`-EAGAIN`-stops shape and silently truncated a payload it could have
finished reading — now share one `mqtt_read_payload_deadline()` helper that
retries `-EAGAIN` by polling the socket up to a 5 s deadline instead of
giving up immediately. Hitting a genuine error or the deadline with bytes
still owed now calls `mqtt_abort()` rather than leaving the client wedged at
`-EBUSY` forever — a torn-down connection is recoverable, `-EBUSY` forever is
not.

Lifted the deadline-polling shape and the 5 s budget from PR #1658's
`drain_mqtt_payload()` (`fix/1645-unbounded-length-sweep`), written for the
same bug before #1918 landed a simpler drain that reopened it. Changed from
that PR: folded the drain-only and fill-`rx_buf` cases into one helper
(parameterized on a `NULL` destination for discard-only reads) instead of
`drain_mqtt_payload()` staying drain-only, so the main read loop gets the
same retry instead of relying solely on the follow-on drain to paper over an
early stop; and one shared 5 s deadline covers the whole `MQTT_EVT_PUBLISH`
handling of a message (fill + drain) rather than each call getting its own
fresh 5 s budget. #1658's MQTT hunk is superseded by this fix; its other
hunks (unrelated to the drain) are unaffected.

this repo turns on for `native_sim`, so no gate here compiles it (same
