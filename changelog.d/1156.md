### Investigated — `metadata/pinmux/v2n.yaml`'s TBD columns confirmed real, with two different causes (#1156)

- `e1m_pad: "TBD"` on all 207 rows is confirmed real, not a bug: the
  E1M-connector edge-ball assignment for RZ/V2N does not exist yet, in this
  repo or the internal metadata repository. Filling it in requires a SoM
  netlist export in the same shape as AEN's `from-alif.tsv`, which has not
  been produced. Left as-is pending that export.
- `e1m_function: "TBD"` on the 145 Renesas-owned rows has a different,
  narrower cause: the internal source TSV carries a third column the public
  one lacks, naming the E1M-standard signal for a subset of rows -- that
  column can populate `e1m_function` for those rows once ingested, but it
  cannot populate `e1m_pad` (it doesn't carry the edge ball either). Left as
  "TBD" pending that ingestion, not blocked on the netlist export above.
