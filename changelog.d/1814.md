### Fixed — the AEN calendar ran on the errata-bound LPRTC with no caveat, and the SoM's own `rv3028c7` workaround turned out to be unreachable (#1814)

`AERR0012` v2.0 leaves no Ensemble revision without an RTC accuracy quirk, and
`src/backends/rtc/lprtc_calendar_shim.c` — the AEN calendar path — carried
neither erratum. ER001 (Rev **A0**, the bench silicon, fixed in A1): a POR_N
assertion or a wake from STOP resets the RTC clock pre-scaler divider, and
re-programming it afterwards does not recover the lost accuracy. ER002 (Rev
**A1**, no fix planned): while POR_N is asserted the clock source falls from
the 32 kHz LFXO to the ~5%-accurate LFRC. Both are now recorded at the shim
(`src/backends/rtc/lprtc_calendar_shim.c:44` ("SILICON CAVEAT (#1814)")) and in
both AEN RTC example READMEs
(`examples/aen/aen-rtc-calendar/README.md:28` ("see alp-sdk#1814")).

Alif's own stated ER002 workaround is "use an external real-time clock
source", and the `E1M-AEN801` SoM already carries one — an `rv3028c7` at
7-bit `0x52` on `BRD_I2C`, driver already at `chips/rv3028c7/rv3028c7.c`
(`metadata/e1m_modules/E1M-AEN801.yaml:101` ("chip: rv3028c7")). It is
**not** switched to here. That gap is filed separately as #1848, which
corrects two beliefs at once: `BRD_I2C` is not the slave-only LPI2C0 (it is
SoC I2C0, function C, master-capable), but it is also not the "ordinary
plumbing" that correction first suggested -- the R2 netlist shows no
pull-up path to any rail on the bus, and the datasheet/HWRM disagree on
whether that is fatal without one
(`docs/bring-up-aen.md:224` ("blocks using the on-module")).

The LPRTC shim stays the only calendar source on AEN until #1848 lands a real
transport. Its counter keeps advancing across a reset; it is the *elapsed*
time across one that is untrustworthy on both silicon revisions, so a caller
that needs accuracy must re-set the time from an external source after any
POR (`src/backends/rtc/lprtc_calendar_shim.c:70` ("blocked on that transport")).
