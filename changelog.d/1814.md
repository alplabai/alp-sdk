### Fixed — the AEN calendar ran on the errata-bound LPRTC with no caveat, and the SoM's own `rv3028c7` workaround turned out to be unreachable (#1814)

`AERR0012` v2.0 leaves no Ensemble revision without an RTC accuracy quirk, and
`src/backends/rtc/lprtc_calendar_shim.c` — the AEN calendar path — carried
neither erratum. ER001 (Rev **A0**, the bench silicon, fixed in A1): a POR_N
assertion or a wake from STOP resets the RTC clock pre-scaler divider, and
re-programming it afterwards does not recover the lost accuracy. ER002 (Rev
**A1**, no fix planned): while POR_N is asserted the clock source falls from
the 32 kHz LFXO to the ~5%-accurate LFRC. Both are now recorded at the shim
(`src/backends/rtc/lprtc_calendar_shim.c:44` ("SILICON CAVEAT (#1814)")) and in
both AEN RTC example READMEs
(`examples/aen/aen-rtc-calendar/README.md:38` ("see alp-sdk#1814")).

Alif's own stated ER002 workaround is "use an external real-time clock
source", and the `E1M-AEN801` SoM already carries one — an `rv3028c7` at
7-bit `0x52` on `BRD_I2C`, driver already at `chips/rv3028c7/rv3028c7.c`
(`metadata/e1m_modules/E1M-AEN801.yaml:119` ("chip: rv3028c7")). It is
**not** switched to here, and the reason recorded in an earlier draft of this
fragment was wrong. That draft said the pads "carry no documented I2C function
at all". They do: `BRD_I2C_SCL` is `P7_1` and `BRD_I2C_SDA` is `P7_0`, and
`ADTS0013` v1.2 Table 3-16 gives those pads `I2C0_SCL_C` / `I2C0_SDA_C` --
I2C0, which HWRM 15.4.1 documents as master-or-slave capable. The slave-only
`0x40` constraint belongs to `LPI2C0` on `P7_4`/`P7_5`, which is not where this
bus is wired.

What that r1 run actually measured was two floating pins, and the conclusion
drawn from it does not carry. On an **r1** module the RTC and the TMP112 sit on
LPI2C0 (`P7_4`/`P7_5`) and **nothing is attached to `P7_0`/`P7_1`** -- so
nothing could have ACKed there whatever the pad biasing. The `-116`
(`-ETIMEDOUT`) with `E: User Abort on i2c@49010000` at High-Z, and the `-5`
(`-EIO`) with the pad's internal pull-up enabled, characterised an empty net
rather than a populated bus. The "no usable pull-up, the two 0 ohm jumpers are
not stuffed" reading and the open-drain-vs-push-pull inference both came from
that same floating net, and neither survives.

Bench-settled again on 2026-09-05 on **2626-R2** silicon -- the revision that
actually routes `BRD_I2C` to I2C0 -- the bus works on the SoC's **internal
pull-up alone**. The `rv3028c7` ACKs at `0x52` (ID register `0x28` = `0x44`,
seconds `0x01` -> `0x02`, so the oscillator runs), and every non-response is a
clean `rc=-5` (`-EIO`) NACK with zero `-ETIMEDOUT` across the whole run.
`R93`/`R94` stay DNP and are not needed. The withdrawal of the "not usable as
built" verdict, and the customer-facing writeup of the bus, are recorded in
this release's #1848 fragment.

So nothing electrical blocks `rv3028c7` from backing the AEN calendar any
more. **#1814 stays open as a deliberate backend-selection change, not a board
change** -- and it is not a free upgrade. `VDD_BAT`/`VBACKUP` has no supply
fitted on this batch (`R4`/`R68` both DNP), so the external RTC loses the time
on a cold boot exactly as the LPRTC does; switching backends buys accuracy
while running, not persistence across power.

The LPRTC shim therefore stays the AEN calendar source for now. Its counter
keeps advancing across a reset; it is the *elapsed* time across one that is
untrustworthy on both silicon revisions, so a caller that needs accuracy must
re-set the time from an external source after any POR
(`src/backends/rtc/lprtc_calendar_shim.c:100`
("selection deliberately (#1814)")).

A follow-up review round flagged 4 majors against a different framing bug:
every comment and doc line above described the LFXO-to-LFRC fallback as
happening only "while POR_N is asserted" -- accurate as errata text, but the
bench module's own SE boot header reports `[SES] No LF XTAL` on *every* boot,
meaning this board has no 32 kHz LFXO fitted at all and LFRC is its
permanent LPRTC clock source, POR_N or not. That is not a transient
startup state; it is the steady state, and it means the calendar drifts
continuously at LFRC's ~5% offset from LFXO -- roughly 72 minutes per day --
for as long as the shim runs, not only across a reset. The shim comment, both
example READMEs, and `docs/aen-se-services.md` are corrected to say so
(`src/backends/rtc/lprtc_calendar_shim.c:59` ("BENCH FACT (goes beyond the errata text above")).
