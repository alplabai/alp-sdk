### Fixed — the SN65DSI83 bridge recovers by itself after it resets to its defaults (panel black, backlight on) (#2815)

On the bench (E1M-AEN803 + Riverdi RVT121) the panel went black with the backlight on while the SoC side stayed healthy (CDC200 scanning, DSI PHY locked, EN `P13_4` high): the bridge had reset itself (CSR `0x0D` `0x00`, `0x0A` `0x0A`, `0xE5` `0x3D`; healthy reads `0x01`, `0x81`, `0x00`) and its init ran only once at boot. The driver now health-checks the bridge every `CONFIG_DISPLAY_SN65DSI83_RECOVERY_INTERVAL_MS` (default 1000, `CONFIG_DISPLAY_SN65DSI83_RECOVERY`, default y), and when PLL_EN is clear, the PLL is not locked or `HS_CLK_SRC` reads back clear it replays the same init (ID check, CSR table, PLL start, `0xE5` clear), rate-limited to one re-init per 5 s, counted and logged at WRN with the register values; `0xE5` error bits alone with the PLL fine are cleared and counted without a re-init. `sn65dsi83_recovery_count()` returns the counter. When the display core hands I2C1 to another core after init (the Trace Runner: the HE configures the bridge, the HP owns the controller for the camera), the display core never touches the bus again: the driver publishes its CSR table in shared SRAM (`recovery-recipe-address`) and an `alp,sn65dsi83-recovery` node on the bus-owning core replays it from that core's own I2C driver, so the check cannot race the camera's transfers. The Trace Runner's RVT121 fit overlay and the HP board overlay carry the two nodes; recipe at `0x0237FE00`.
