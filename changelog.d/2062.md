### Fixed — scope the OSPI0 NOR's identity, population, and chip-select claims to the SKU each is actually true for (#2062)

Five sites stated in the present tense that the E1M-AEN OSPI0 NOR is a
Macronix `MX25UM25645GXDI00` populated (`DNP = 0`) on "the R2 BOM." Measured
contradiction (issue #2041): on `E1M-AEN803` serial `2026W36-0002`, Flow C
ITCM RAM-run, the NOR answered a JEDEC ID read on OSPI0 **CS1** with
`9d 5b 19 10` at `BAUDR=20` (20 MHz), repeated on the alternate ISSI ReadID
opcode `0x9E`. `0x9d` is the ISSI manufacturer ID; `5b 19` is the
`IS25WX256` type/capacity pair -- matching `metadata/e1m_modules/
E1M-AEN803.yaml:59-72`'s `IS25WX256-JHLE`. Macronix's manufacturer ID is
`0xc2`.

Three separate errors were mixed into each site and are corrected
independently:

1. **Part identity.** Macronix `MX25UM25645GXDI00` -> measured ISSI
   `IS25WX256-JHLE`, cited to #2041, everywhere the claim named a SKU that
   actually populates OSPI0.
2. **Population.** `metadata/e1m_modules/E1M-AEN801.yaml:60-88` declares
   *both* external memories `assembled: false` (`memory.dram_mbit: 0`,
   `memory.flash_mbit: 0` at `:155-156`) -- E1M-AEN801 runs from the SoC's
   on-die MRAM and populates neither device on any revision as this repo
   models it. "The R2 BOM populates only OSPI0" is false for that SKU;
   every corrected site now says which SKU (E1M-AEN803) the claim is
   actually true for.
3. **Chip select.** `meta-alp-sdk/conf/machine/e1m-aen801-a32.conf:92`
   placed the NOR on CS0. The netlist puts `U10` (NOR) on `OSPI0_SS1` and
   `U9` (HyperRAM) on `OSPI0_SS0`; metadata was corrected to match in
   `44200cab` (#1944, #1990, PR #2007); #2041 confirmed it by distinguishing
   three `ser` values -- `0x00000002` (CS1) drains `9d 5b 19 10`,
   `0x00000001` (CS0) drains `00 00 00 00`, `0x00000000` drains 0/4 with
   `SR = 0x00000002`.
