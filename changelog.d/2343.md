### Fixed — E1M-X EVK metadata: U30 is an INA228 at 0x42, and the BMI323/ICM-42670 0x69 collision is documented (#2343)

The E1M-X EVK V2 netlist showed that the +5V input monitor U30 is an
INA228 (20-bit register map, A1 = GND, A0 = SDA → `0x42`), not an INA236
at `0x4A`. `XEVK_I2C_ADDR_INA236_5V`, `XEVK_INA236_SHUNT_5V_OHMS` and
`XEVK_INA236_MAX_5V_A` are removed. The new `XEVK_I2C_ADDR_INA228_5V`
(`0x42`) replaces them. `examples/v2n/v2n-power-monitor` now reads the
four INA236 rails only.

The carrier metadata keeps the intended addresses and records the V2
build's hardware defects on the macros:

- **U30:** SDA and SCL are netted swapped, so it cannot answer.
- **BMI323 (U13):** R47 is fitted and R48 is DNP, which puts it at
  `0x69` on top of the ICM-42670 (U12). Fitting R48 and removing R47
  moves it to `0x68`.

An i2c-0 sweep on E1M-V2M103 2026W38-0001 (EVK V2) confirms the as-built
state:

- Nothing answers at `0x42`, `0x4A` or `0x68`.
- `0x40`, `0x41` and `0x49` read INA236 MFG_ID `0x5449`.
- `0x48` is not an INA236 (U32 is removed on this batch).
