### Fixed — gpio-gd32-bridge no longer oopses on unbind then bind (#2508)

Re-binding `8-0070` after a successful sysfs unbind crashed in
`gpio_name_to_desc()` with a NULL dereference at `0xac`, leaking the device
lock and hanging every later unbind and `uevent_show`. A consumer that still
holds a line (`wl-reg-on`, `bt-reg-on`, the panel reset) keeps the old
`gpio_device` on gpiolib's global list with `gdev->chip == NULL`, and the
duplicate-name check that `gpio_chip.names` triggers reads `chip->ngpio`
(offset `0xac`) through it.

Changes:
- `0005-gpio-add-gd32-bridge-expander-driver.patch` no longer sets
  `gpio_chip.names`.
- `e1m-v2n-som.dtsi` supplies the same 20 names through `gpio-line-names`,
  which gpiolib assigns without walking other chips.
