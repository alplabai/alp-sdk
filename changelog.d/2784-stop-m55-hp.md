### Added — Alif SE STOP backend for the E8 M55-HP, bench-proven HP-only on the E1M-AEN803 (#2784)

`CONFIG_ALP_SDK_POWER_ALIF_SE` now also selects on `SOC_AE822FA0E5597LS0_RTSS_HP` (E1M-AEN801 and E1M-AEN803 HP targets), and `examples/aen/aen-power-stop` builds for both, each with its own overlay and a config fragment that turns the boot-time clock restore on. On the E1M-AEN803 HP (module 2026W36-0001 on an E1M-EVK, HP-only ATOC, 2026-10-10) three STOP cycles passed at 400 MHz with the clock restore on: LPTIMER 500 ms, RV-3028 countdown 3 s and RV-3028 alarm all woke (`mode=4`), `quiesced=restored=0x7f`, `SUMMARY cycles=3 pass=5 fail=0`. Not verified: an HP STOP while the HE runs, and the E1M-AEN801 HP.

The HP differs from the HE in the ways the Alif vendor sources show. It writes its own `AON.RTSS_HP_CTRL` / `RTSS_HP_RESET` (`0x1A604000` / `0x1A604004`), not the HE pair. It has `STOP` only (the vendor SOFT_OFF-class sleep), no TCM retention, and needs an MRAM-booted image. Its clock restore uses the 400 MHz RUN profile and the `PLL_CLK_SEL` `ES0` health bit. `STANDBY` is `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` from the dispatcher before the backend runs (the backend's own `hp_standby_unsupported` check is defence in depth). The other two refusals are named on the console: TCM or full retention is `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` with `reason=hp_tcm_not_retainable`, and a TCM-booted image is `ALP_ERR_NOSUPPORT` with `reason=hp_vtor_not_mram`. Wake events, EWIC groups and the BKRAM record are SoC-level and shared with the HE.

The backend cannot see the other core: with the HE running, an HP STOP powers down only the HP subsystem. `docs/aen-power-domains.md` has the HP table, the sources, and the open bench items. `tests/unit/power_alif_se` covers each new branch.

**HP STOP while the HE runs.** Measured on E1M-AEN803 2026W36-0001 (combined ATOC, HE idle, HP `aen-power-stop`): the HP is powered off and re-booted on the LPTIMER, but `STOP_MODE_STAT` stays clear because the DC-DC never turns off, so the wake record was discarded and cycle 1 repeated. This core's own `RTSS_HE_CTRL` / `RTSS_HP_CTRL` WIC [9:8] = `0b01` is now a second STOP witness, consumed on every boot; a STOP record is trusted when either witness holds, and `RESETSYNDROME` bit2 with the WIC bits as the only witness marks an aborted entry. `ALP_POWER_MODE_STOP` in `alp/power.h` now says the realised mode is the calling core's. Implemented and unit-tested; bench re-run pending.
