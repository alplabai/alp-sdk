### Fixed — `validate · soc-spec-v1` no longer times out after every test passes (#2369)

The `tests/scripts/` pytest step in `pr-metadata-validate.yml` now has a
35-minute cap instead of 20. The suite takes about 1200 s on the hosted
runner, so `dev` and PR runs were being killed at the 20-minute cap right
after pytest reported every test passing.
