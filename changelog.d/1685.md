### Fixed — the V2N `ipc:` carve-out deferred reconciliation to a closed issue (#1685)

`zephyr/boards/alp/e1m_v2n101_m33_sm/alp_e1m_v2n101_m33_sm_r9a09g056n48gbg_cm33.dts`'s
OpenAMP carve-out comment said the paper `ipc:` carve-out in `board.yaml`
("still `ocram_low` pending a follow-up that reconciles the two") would be
reconciled by alp-sdk #683. #683 is closed, and it settled a different
question — bench-validating the real `/dev/rpmsg` transport for the GHSA-xhm8
close-protocol redesign — not this address reconciliation. The deferral
pointed at an issue that never covered it, so the open question had nothing
tracking it.

The comment is now self-contained: it explains directly that
`resolve_carve_outs()` (`scripts/alp_orchestrate/carveout.py`) allocates the
`ipc:` entry from `ocram_low` only because that is the sole non-cacheable
region reachable from both `a55_cluster` and `m33_sm`
(`metadata/socs/renesas/rzv2n/n44.json`), that nothing consumes the address
it resolves to, and that the real transport instead uses a hand-authored DDR
window sized from the OpenAMP resource table's vring math. It also spells
out the resulting exhaustion instead of leaving it implicit: `carve_out_kb:
512` in both `examples/multicore/rpmsg-v2n/board.yaml` and
`examples/multicore/heterogeneous-offload/board.yaml` is the *entire*
512 KiB of `ocram_low`, so a second `ipc:` entry on this SoM has no headroom
left there once the paper allocation becomes load-bearing.

No carve-out was resized. Whether the fix is teaching the resolver about the
real DDR window or shrinking the examples' paper `carve_out_kb` values is a
sizing decision on real SoM memory, left for a follow-up that actually picks
one.
