### Added — `examples/aen/aen-trace-runner`: the Trace Runner exhibition game (#2446)

Trace Runner is the endless-runner game Alp Lab shows at exhibitions on an
E1M-AEN803 with the E1M-EVK and the RK055HDMIPI4MA0 MIPI-DSI panel. It now
ships as an example, and exercises four of the module's engines at once:
- the M55-HE runs the game logic, HUD and frame pacing;
- the M55-HP runs the OV9281 camera and MoveNet pose estimation on the
  Ethos-U55, and the player steers with their body;
- a bare-metal Cortex-A32, booted by TF-A, renders the 3D scene.

`README.md` has the ordered build: A32 images, HE, HP, the MoveNet cut + Vela
step, and the ATOC packaging script. `a32/release/FLASH-RECIPE.md` covers
flashing. `tests/host/runner.sh` runs the host unit tests. `testcase.yaml`
adds three build-only M55-HE scenarios: A32 render, A32 render with NPU pose
input, and HE-only render.

The optional `sound/` HP image only builds with `-DTR_SND_REWORKED_U46=ON`.
On a stock 2626-R2 carrier, U46 contends with the SoC on P9_3/P9_4/P9_5 as
soon as I2S3 is muxed.
