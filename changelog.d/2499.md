### Fixed — ISP-Pico capture no longer stalls after a stream restart (#2499)

On the E8 ISP-Pico path the CPI takes one frame per start, and the ISP re-arms
it from its own frame-end handling. That re-arm could reach the CPI driver
before the CPI's STOP interrupt for the same frame had been serviced; the
driver then treated it as a duplicate and dropped it, and the STOP handler's
`CAM_CTRL` write left the CPI idle with nothing to start it again. Every
later capture timed out. Per-frame 3A logging slowed the ISP path enough to
hide the race.

The re-arm is now latched when a snapshot is still armed, and the STOP
interrupt starts the next snapshot
(`zephyr/drivers/video/video_alif.c:529` ("data->rearm_pending = true;")), so
an in-flight snapshot is still never soft-reset.

Verified on E1M-AEN803 serial 2026W36-0001 with an INNO-MAKER CAM-IMX335-5MP:
- ISP path, 1280x960 YUV420, `CONFIG_LOG=n`: stop/start restarts captured
  10 of 10 in two runs (1 of 10 before).
- Writing `CAM_CTRL=0x00001011` over SWD at a stalled restart completed the
  stuck capture before the change, which is what isolated the lost re-arm.
- 60 consecutive ISP frames captured; the CPI-only path's 20-restart test
  still passes 20 of 20.
