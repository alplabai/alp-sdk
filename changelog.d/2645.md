### Added — E1M-X EVK I2C inventory: INA228 chip manifest and a board I2C table (#2645)

Every I2C bus and fixed device on the E1M-X EVK with an E1M-V2N / E1M-V2M SoM was checked against the hardware design data and against recorded `i2cdetect` sweeps on E1M-V2M103 units. The SDK already described all but one of them.

- **`chips/ina228` + `<alp/chips/ina228.h>` (new), `metadata/chips/ina228.yaml` (new).** The board metadata names `part: ina228` for the +5V input monitor at `0x42`, but the part had no chip manifest and no driver. The portable driver (integer math, micro-units, 64-bit for the 40-bit ENERGY / CHARGE registers) probes the manufacturer and die ID, programs `SHUNT_CAL` from a shunt in micro-ohms and a max current in micro-amps, and reads shunt / bus voltage, die temperature, current, power, energy and charge, resets the accumulators and reads `DIAG_ALRT`. `ina228_init()` returns `INA228_ERR_NOT_PRESENT` when nothing acknowledges, distinct from `ALP_ERR_NOT_READY` (something answered that is not an INA228). It exists, like `chips/ina236`, because the EVK's INA228 is on E1M-X I2C0, which Linux owns on the A55 (`i2c-0`), and the 6.1.141-cip43 kernel has no INA228 driver (`ina238` matches `ti,ina238` only). Upstream Zephyr v4.4.1 ships a `ti,ina228` driver (`drivers/sensor/ti/ina2xx`, `CONFIG_INA228`) and remains the path for a bus a Zephyr core masters. `driver_status: partial` (alert limit registers and `SHUNT_TEMPCO` are not exposed), registered as `ALP_SDK_CHIP_INA228` in CMake, Zephyr Kconfig and the Yocto `alp-chips` recipe, with a fake i2c-emul test in `tests/zephyr/chips`. The shunt scale is selectable end to end: `ina228_init()` and `ina228_set_shunt_range()` take the range (`CONFIG.ADCRANGE`: +/-163.84 mV or +/-40.96 mV, `SHUNT_CAL` times 4 on the narrow one), the switch clears the ENERGY and CHARGE accumulators (the datasheet does not say they survive it), `ina228_pick_adcrange()` picks the range for a shunt and expected current, `ina228_check_over_range()` reports a clipped reading, and a max current above the range's full scale is rejected (unlike `chips/ina236`, which clamps). The board calibration entry gained optional `adc_range` / `adc_range_macro` (emitted as `XEVK_INA228_ADCRANGE_5V`, default the wide range) and `max_current_note`, and `scripts/gen_board_header.py` rejects a `max_current_a` above the range's full scale for the shunt. The driver has not been run on hardware. On the current EVK revision the device's bus pins are documented as swapped and corrected by a hand rework, so it answers at `0x42` only on carriers with that rework; no answer means the part is absent, not a fault. The only bench fact is that `0x42` acknowledged on 2026-10-02, with no ID register read.
- **`docs/boards/e1m-x-evk.md`.** New "I²C buses and devices" section: each bus with its controller and Linux adapter number, each device with its address, strap, interrupt / reset lines and SDK driver, and a column saying whether the address was seen on a real unit.
- **`metadata/boards/e1m-x-evk.yaml`.** `XEVK_I2C_ADDR_EEPROM` (`0x50`) is now described as what it is: the SoM identity EEPROM, which shares E1M-X I2C0 with the carrier sensors. The carrier has no EEPROM of its own. The macro and its value are unchanged; only the comment in `include/alp/boards/alp_e1m_x_evk_routes.h` moves. The block's board notes also record the 2026-10-02 sweeps, in which `0x42` and `0x68` answered.
- **Removed: the `0x48` camera-rail monitor (`XEVK_I2C_ADDR_INA236_VCAM2`, `XEVK_INA236_SHUNT_VCAM2_OHMS`, `XEVK_INA236_MAX_VCAM2_A`).** The INA236B strapped to `0x48` on E1M-X I2C0 is not fitted; `0x48` there is the shared address of the two TAS2563 amplifiers (recorded in `metadata/chips/tas2563.yaml`, which `scripts/check_i2c_address_uniqueness.py` reads, so nothing else can be assigned it). The `0x48` allowlist entry in that gate is gone, the three macros are recorded in `docs/abi/removed-symbols.json`, and `examples/v2n/v2n-power-monitor` and its HIL spec now read the 3V3, 1V8 and VCAM3 INA236 monitors, plus a `5V` row from the INA228 (shunt 100 mOhm, `XEVK_INA228_SHUNT_5V_OHMS` / `XEVK_INA228_MAX_5V_A` in the board header; the max is the ADCRANGE = 0 shunt full scale, not a rail limit). `--ina228-range 163mv|40mv|auto` selects the INA228 scale (`auto` switches with hysteresis and reports over-range). When the INA228 is not present the example prints one line and a `--` row and keeps running; the HIL spec asserts nothing about it. The board doc, the I2C scan tutorial and the board metadata no longer describe an open BMI323 / ICM-42670 address clash: the as-built addresses are `0x68` (BMI323) and `0x69` (ICM-42670).

No other address changed and no Linux device tree node was added. Parts with an SDK `chips/` driver stay on that driver over i2c-dev (#2339, #2335).

Still open on #2645, all needing a powered board: an ID-register read at `0x42`, `0x68` and `0x69` on `i2c-0`; which controller the display touch bus and the mikroBUS I2C reach; the enable polarity of the PCIe / M.2 I2C switch.
