### Fixed — BL2 now boots the V2N/V2M Cortex-M33 (#2354)

No bitbake-built BL2 started the on-module CM33: the TF-A enablement (`PLAT_M33_BOOT_SUPPORT := 1` plus a memory-mapped BL22 read) lived only as `docs/rzv2n-tfa-m33-boot.patch`, which no recipe applied, so every `firmware-pack` BL2 went straight to BL31 and no Zephyr CM33 app could run on a production module. The patch now lives in `meta-alp-sdk/recipes-bsp/trusted-firmware-a/trusted-firmware-a/` and the `trusted-firmware-a` bbappend applies it on every `rzv2n-family` MACHINE. The flash layout is unchanged: BL2 at `0x0`, FIP at `0x60000`, CM33 image at xSPI `0x200000` (`mtd1` offset `0x1a0000`).
