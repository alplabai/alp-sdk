### Fixed — microSD/eMMC writes no longer go out 4 KiB at a time on V2N/V2M (#2357)

The RZ/V2N SDHI internal DMAC takes one contiguous buffer per request, so the
kernel advertised `max_segs = 1`; with no IOMMU every page-cache write reached
the card as a single 4 KiB command. On an E1M-V2M103 with the E1M-X EVK the
microSD wrote at ~2.7 MB/s in HS/SDR50, and SDR104 writes hung on
`mmc1: Card stuck being busy!`, while reads ran at ~85 MB/s. Kernel patch
`0010-mmc-renesas_sdhi-bounce-multi-segment-requests-in-internal-dmac.patch`
copies multi-segment requests through one 256 KiB coherent buffer per host,
so requests of up to 256 KiB reach the card; single-segment requests keep the
zero-copy path.
