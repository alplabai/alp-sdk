### Fixed — OV5647 driver now turns its own auto-white-balance off, leaving the E8 ISP as the sole white-balance actor (#2255)

Bench-proven on E1M-AEN803 (evk-02): with `0x5001` never driven by the
driver, the sensor's own AWB stayed at its power-on-enabled value (`0x01`)
and could fight the ISP's own white-balance stage. `ov5647.c` now writes
`0x5001 = 0x00` (new `OV5647_ISP_CTRL01`/`OV5647_ISP_CTRL01_AWB_EN`) as
part of the common init block, matching mainline
`drivers/media/i2c/ov5647.c`'s `OV5647_REG_AWB` default of off. This is
distinct from — and must not be confused with — Alif's `0x5001 = 0x01`
addition that run 58 traced issue #2248's CSI "incorrect frame sequence"
fatals to: bench run 217 held `0x5001 = 0x00` mid-stream with zero CSI
errors and every dequeue `ALP_OK`, and `0x5002`/`0x4050`/`0x4051` remain
never written.
