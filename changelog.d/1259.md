### Added — ONNX Runtime CPU floor enabled for E1M-V2M101 / V2M102 / V2M103 / V2N102 images (#1259)

`alp-sdk` gains an `ort` PACKAGECONFIG (`-DALP_SDK_USE_ORT_CPU=ON
-DALP_SDK_ORT_REQUIRED=ON`, depends on the layer's own `onnxruntime` recipe)
that the four MACHINE confs switch on with `ALP_ENABLE_ORT_CPU ?= "1"`.
`resolve_auto()` still orders CPU last, so V2M SKUs keep DEEPX and DRP-AI
ahead of it. Silicon passes per SKU are still owed.
