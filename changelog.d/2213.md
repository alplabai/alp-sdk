### Added — OV5647 + OV9281 Zephyr sensor drivers for Raspberry-Pi-style CSI-2 camera shields (#2213)

Two board-agnostic Zephyr shields land under `zephyr/boards/shields/`:
`raspberry_pi_camera_module_1` (OmniVision OV5647, 5 Mpx raw Bayer, 25 MHz
oscillator, CCI 0x36) and `innomaker_cam_ov9281` (OmniVision OV9281, 1 Mpx
global-shutter mono, 24 MHz oscillator, CCI 0x60), both 2 MIPI data lanes.
Neither shield carries any Alif/EVK specifics — each just wires its sensor's
`fixed-clock` and endpoint onto the upstream RPi camera shield label
contract (`chosen zephyr,camera`, `&csi_interface`, `&csi_ep_in`,
`&csi_i2c`); build with `SHIELD="<carrier-connector-shield> <camera-shield>"`.

`zephyr/drivers/video/ov5647.c` is a byte-for-byte, ADR 0017 Tier-1
upstream-PENDING backport (INTERIM) of the still-open upstream PR
zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr#119301 @ `d81b1f630ee0f5078de6e3a47e7bc1b3613424c2`
— not yet in the pinned Zephyr v4.4.1 base. Its own binding treats
`pwdn-gpios` as optional, and the `raspberry_pi_camera_module_1` shield
omits it: the E1M-EVK's 15-pin RPi camera connector carries no PWDN GPIO
line to wire it to. Delete the driver, its Kconfig, and its binding — see
the file header and the new ADR 0017 amendment — the moment the Zephyr pin
advances past a revision containing that PR.

`zephyr/drivers/video/ov9281.c` is an ADR 0017 Tier-1.5 (third-party
permissive port) of the Apache-2.0 Espressif `esp-video-components`
`esp_cam_sensor/sensors/ov9281` driver (@
`f1e4bff773ce36204434a47bdefb5bc943dc6647`) onto the upstream Zephyr v4.4
video API, keeping Espressif's register addresses/values/mode-setting
tables verbatim, plus one Alp-derived 1280x800 (full-array) table (see the
driver's file header). Three modes are supported — 1280x720 and 640x400
(Espressif's), plus 1280x800 (Alp-derived) — all RAW8 mono
(`VIDEO_PIX_FMT_GREY`), 2 data lanes, 24 MHz XVCLK; no RAW10 mode is
invented. **BENCH-VERIFIED 2026-09-21** on an E1M-AEN803 on the E1M-EVK:
live GREY8 frames captured and CRC-verified via an SWD dump in all three
modes, each at its configured frame rate (measured 60-frame bursts:
640x400 ~100 fps, 1280x720 ~50 fps, 1280x800 ~100 fps); the sensor test
pattern is also verified in all three modes.

`tests/zephyr/video_sensors` runs a real ztest for OV9281
(`ov9281_test.c`) against an I2C emulator on `native_sim`, checking
`get_caps`/`set_format`/register programming/exposure range check instead
of only compiling the driver; OV5647 stays compile-coverage in that same
runtime suite (no emulator). See `docs/camera-shields.md` for the full
driver/shield/control reference.
