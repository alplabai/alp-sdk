### Added — OV5647 Zephyr sensor driver for a Raspberry-Pi-style CSI-2 camera shield (#2213)

A board-agnostic Zephyr shield lands under `zephyr/boards/shields/`:
`raspberry_pi_camera_module_1` (OmniVision OV5647, 5 Mpx raw Bayer, 25 MHz
oscillator, CCI 0x36, 2 MIPI data lanes). It carries no Alif/EVK specifics —
it just wires the sensor's `fixed-clock` and endpoint onto the upstream RPi
camera shield label contract (`chosen zephyr,camera`, `&csi_interface`,
`&csi_ep_in`, `&csi_i2c`); build with
`SHIELD="<carrier-connector-shield> raspberry_pi_camera_module_1"`.

`zephyr/drivers/video/ov5647.c` is a byte-for-byte, ADR 0017 Tier-1
upstream-PENDING backport (INTERIM) of the still-open upstream PR
zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr#119301 @ `d81b1f630ee0f5078de6e3a47e7bc1b3613424c2`
— not yet in the pinned Zephyr v4.4.1 base. Its own binding treats
`pwdn-gpios` as optional, and the `raspberry_pi_camera_module_1` shield
omits it: the E1M-EVK's 15-pin RPi camera connector carries no PWDN GPIO
line to wire it to. Delete the driver, its Kconfig, and its binding — see
the file header and the ADR 0017 amendment — the moment the Zephyr pin
advances past a revision containing that PR.

`tests/zephyr/video_sensors` stays compile-coverage for OV5647 (no
emulator) in the same runtime suite that ztests OV9281. See
`docs/camera-shields.md` for the full driver/shield/control reference.
