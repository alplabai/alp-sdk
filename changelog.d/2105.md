### Fixed — cc3501e READY gate proved a level, not an edge, so a stuck-high pad clocked every reply phase near-instantly (#2105)

`cc3501e_reply_gate()` (`chips/cc3501e/cc3501e_core.c`) used to latch
`ctx->ready_pin` as "proven" off a single level read: the first HIGH sample
set `g_ready_line_proven` and returned without paying its inter-phase
settle, and every later gate skipped straight to the same level check. A
level cannot tell "the slave just drove this HIGH" apart from "this pad is
driven HIGH by something else entirely and has never moved" — and the
driver's own history already documents that a too-short inter-phase gap
desyncs this link outright.

Bench evidence (run8, e1m-aen-evk-01): several AEN example bridges open
Alif P2_6 as `CC3501E_BRIDGE_PIN_READY`. On e1m-aen-evk-01, P2_6 is E1M pad
AH7 / I2S1_SCLK, which the EVK carrier repurposes as the Arduino header's
CK_RST net (`metadata/boards/e1m-evk.yaml`) — not the CC3501E's real
GPIO17 READY signal, which lands on E1M pad G3 / IO16 instead
(`metadata/e1m_modules/aen/from-cc3501e.tsv`, `from-alif.tsv`). Reading
CK_RST as READY returns a constant 1 (its reset line idles high), which
the old level-only gate proved on the very first call. With the READY
gate trusting that pad, station connect never associated in 4 of 4 boots
(`get_version` returned -5 afterward); with `ready_pin` left `NULL`, 5 of
7 boots associated on the same firmware.

`cc3501e_reply_gate()` now proves the line only on a real edge — a LOW
then a HIGH, or the HIGH-to-LOW busy transition the bridge drives around a
radio op — and pays its full fixed settle on every gate until an edge is
observed. A line that was proven but then misses its expected busy LOW
for `CC3501E_READY_STUCK_STREAK` (3) consecutive gates is un-proven back
to the fixed settle rather than trusted forever on stale evidence; the
degrade latches once via `cc3501e_ready_line_was_stuck()` for a caller to
report.

The AEN example bridges (`examples/aen/*/src/cc3501e_bridge.c`) no longer
open a READY pin at all by default on the E1M EVK — the driver's fix makes
a mis-wired line harmless regardless, but this also stops the examples
claiming a pad as READY that this board does not actually wire that way.
`examples/peripheral-io/alp-console/src/cc3501e_bridge.c`'s dead
`cc3501e_bus_ready()` / `bodge_ready_raw()` — a "strong override" of a weak
symbol that does not exist anywhere in `chips/cc3501e` — is removed.

New `tests/zephyr/cc3501e_host_driver` coverage drives a fake READY GPIO
through stuck-high, stuck-low, and correctly-toggling fixtures and asserts
the fallback-delay accounting in each case; restoring the old level-proves-it
behaviour turns the stuck-high case red.
