### Fixed — cc3501e READY gate proved a level, not an edge, so a stuck-high pad clocked every reply phase near-instantly (#2105)

`cc3501e_reply_gate()` (`chips/cc3501e/cc3501e_core.c`) used to latch
`ctx->ready_pin` as "proven" off a single level read: the first HIGH sample
set `g_ready_line_proven` and returned without paying its inter-phase
settle, and every later gate skipped straight to the same level check. A
level cannot tell "the slave just drove this HIGH" apart from "this pad is
driven HIGH by something else entirely and has never moved" — and the
driver's own history already documents that a too-short inter-phase gap
desyncs this link outright.

Bench evidence (run8, e1m-aen-evk-01): several AEN example bridges open
Alif P2_6 as `CC3501E_BRIDGE_PIN_READY`. On e1m-aen-evk-01, P2_6 is E1M pad
AH7 / I2S1_SCLK, which the EVK carrier repurposes as the Arduino header's
CK_RST net (`metadata/boards/e1m-evk.yaml`) — not the CC3501E's real
GPIO17 READY signal, which lands on E1M pad G3 / IO16 instead
(`metadata/e1m_modules/aen/from-cc3501e.tsv`, `from-alif.tsv`). Reading
CK_RST as READY returns a constant 1 (its reset line idles high), which
the old level-only gate proved on the very first call. With the READY
gate trusting that pad, station connect never associated in 4 of 4 boots
(`get_version` returned -5 afterward); with `ready_pin` left `NULL`, 5 of
7 boots associated on the same firmware.

`cc3501e_reply_gate()` now requires `CC3501E_READY_EDGE_AGREE_MIN` (3)
CONFIRMED transitions — each one debounced (`CC3501E_READY_CONFIRM_READS`,
8 agreeing reads in a row; a pad that flips every read, real noise or this
fix's own regression fixture, can never confirm even one sample) — before
trusting the line, and only counts a sample taken in a `busy_expected` gate
call: the three call sites that run immediately after a transceive within
the same 4-phase exchange, where the slave re-arming really is expected to
dip READY. The REQUEST-HEADER gate is excluded from both proving and the
stuck-streak below: in ordinary spaced traffic it always sees READY already
idling HIGH, so counting its misses degraded even a healthy line. Every
gate pays its full fixed settle until proven, including the confirming
call itself. A line that was proven but then misses its expected busy LOW
for `CC3501E_READY_STUCK_STREAK` (3) consecutive busy-expected gates is
un-proven back to the fixed settle — clearing the proving state too, so a
stale pre-degrade sample cannot instantly re-prove it off one leftover
comparison — rather than trusted forever on stale evidence; the degrade
latches once via `cc3501e_ready_line_was_stuck()` (`CONFIG_ZTEST`-only
reset helper) for a caller to report.

The AEN example bridges (`examples/aen/*/src/cc3501e_bridge.c` and their
board overlays) no longer open, mux, or input-enable a READY pin by
default on the R2 module e1m-aen-evk-01 holds — the driver's fix makes a
mis-wired line harmless regardless, but this also stops the examples and
overlays claiming a pad as READY that this module does not actually wire
that way. In-tree evidence (`changelog.d/1799.md`, `cc3501e_sockets.c`)
shows READY genuinely worked on a separately hand-reworked r1 unit; a
board or unit that really does wire CC35 GPIO17 to an Alif pad can opt
back in by setting `fw->ready_pin` — documented in one place per app,
next to `CC3501E_BRIDGE_PIN_READY`'s declaration.
`examples/peripheral-io/alp-console/src/cc3501e_bridge.c`'s dead
`cc3501e_bus_ready()` / `bodge_ready_raw()` — a "strong override" of a weak
symbol that does not exist anywhere in `chips/cc3501e` — is removed.

`tests/zephyr/cc3501e_host_driver` coverage drives a programmable fake
READY GPIO through stuck-high, stuck-low, noisy (flips every read), a
single glitch, a healthy line under spaced traffic, a miss-hit-miss-miss
streak sequence, the request-header exclusion's timing, and the
un-prove stale-sample reset, asserting the fallback-delay accounting (and
`cc3501e_ready_line_was_stuck()`) in each case. Each of the edge-proving,
streak-reset-on-hit, request-header-exclusion, and un-prove-reset fixes was
mutation-verified: reverting it individually turns exactly its own
regression(s) red and nothing else.
