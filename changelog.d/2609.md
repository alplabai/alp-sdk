### Fixed — Yocto `DISTRO_VERSION` is derived from `include/alp/version.h`, not hardcoded to 0.7.0 (#2609)

The E1M-V2M103 `som-0.2.0` image's login banner and `/etc/os-release` said
"Alp SDK 0.7.0" while the SDK was at 0.16.0: `meta-alp-sdk/conf/distro/alp.conf`
carried a literal `DISTRO_VERSION` ("bump on release") that no release bumped.

Changes:
- `DISTRO_VERSION` in `meta-alp-sdk/conf/distro/alp.conf:40` ("DISTRO_VERSION := ") now reads
  `ALP_VERSION_STRING` from `${ALP_SDK_LAYERDIR}/../include/alp/version.h` at parse
  time; `ALP_SDK_LAYERDIR` is exported by `meta-alp-sdk/conf/layer.conf:10` ("ALP_SDK_LAYERDIR :=").
  `ALP_SDK_VERSION`, os-release `VERSION` and `MENDER_ARTIFACT_NAME` follow it.
- A layer copied outside an alp-sdk checkout fails the parse with a `bb.fatal`
  naming the missing file, instead of falling back to a stale string.
- `scripts/check_version_doc_sync.py` now rejects any literal `DISTRO_VERSION`
  assignment under `meta-alp-sdk/`.
- `docs/build-yocto-v2n.md` no longer claims the banner says `Alp SDK 6.30`.
