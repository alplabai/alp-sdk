### Fixed — E1M-V2M101's `dram_mbit` and `flash_mbit` didn't match the SKU spec (#1230)

`metadata/e1m_modules/E1M-V2M101.yaml` carried `dram_mbit: 32768` (4 GiB) and
`flash_mbit: 32768` (4 GB) — stale values left over from before the V2M101/V2M102
split. Bench readings on a real V2M101 module (`/proc/device-tree/memory@…/reg` ->
8 GiB DRAM; `/sys/block/mmcblk0/size` -> ~256 GB eMMC) surfaced the mismatch, but
neither reading settled it on its own: the DRAM figure matched V2M102's preset
exactly, while the eMMC figure matched neither SKU (off by roughly 64x from
either), which is the signature of a lab unit fitted with a larger-than-production
eMMC part rather than a wrong SoM identity.

Maintainer BOM confirmation on the issue resolved both fields against the
shipping spec, not the bench module: **8 GB DRAM / 16 GB eMMC**.
`dram_mbit` is now `65536` (64 Gbit LPDDR4X, matching the bench measurement) and
`flash_mbit` is now `131072` (128 Gbit eMMC 5.1 — the production part, larger than
the ~256 GB lab-only chip that was actually on the bench).

`docs/soms/v2n-m1.md`'s SKU table and the generated `docs/portability-matrix.md`
Notes column are updated to match. `alp.lock`'s `digests.metadata` is regenerated
(`metadata/catalog.json` is unaffected — it does not carry these fields).
