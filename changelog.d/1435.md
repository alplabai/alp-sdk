### Fixed — a failed `cc3501e_wifi_connect()` left the STA role up, wedging the very next connect (#1435)

`cc3501e_wifi_connect()` (`chips/cc3501e/cc3501e_wifi.c`) submits `WIFI_CONNECT_STA`
once and then polls the firmware's independent `WIFI_STATUS` latch for the
outcome. Both of that poll's error exits — the terminal
`ALP_CC3501E_WIFI_CONN_FAILED` branch and the poll-loop's budget-exhaustion
`remaining == 0u` return — reported the failure and returned, without ever
issuing the `WIFI_DISCONNECT` (0x13) that tears the STA role back down. The
firmware's role stayed up, so the *next* `WIFI_CONNECT_STA` failed at the
role-up kick (`ALP_CC3501E_WIFI_FAIL_KICK`, `chips/cc3501e/cc3501e_wifi.c:226`)
even for a correct SSID and passphrase that had connected seconds earlier.

Root-caused on real silicon, E1M-AEN801 r1, one boot, single variable,
reproduced 2/2: connect to a real AP succeeds (`rssi=-48 dBm`,
`ip: 192.168.1.14`); `wifi disconnect`; the SAME connect succeeds again
(`rssi=-47 dBm`); one connect to a non-existent SSID fails `-5`; the SAME
connect that just worked now ALSO fails `-5`, nothing else changed;
`wifi disconnect`; the same connect succeeds again (`rssi=-49 dBm`). A clean
disconnect/reconnect never leaked — only the failure path did.

Both exits now route through a small `connect_failed()` helper
(`chips/cc3501e/cc3501e_wifi.c:236`) that calls `cc3501e_wifi_disconnect()`
before returning and discards the teardown's own result on purpose — the
caller needs to know why the CONNECT failed, not why the cleanup did, and a
discard here can never mask a real CONNECT failure behind a cleanup one.

New regression coverage in `tests/zephyr/cc3501e_host_driver/src/test_host_driver.c`:
`test_wifi_connect_failure_tears_down_sta_role_1435` and
`test_wifi_connect_failure_timeout_reason_tears_down_sta_role_1435` drive the
terminal `CONN_FAILED` branch (both `fail_reason` legs) and assert a
`WIFI_DISCONNECT` was issued and that the CONNECT's own error survives, not
the teardown's `ALP_OK`; `test_wifi_connect_poll_exhaustion_tears_down_sta_role_1435`
drives the same assertion through the independent poll-exhaustion exit.
