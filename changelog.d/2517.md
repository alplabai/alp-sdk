### Fixed — OPTIGA Trust M reset-and-retry when a session open finds the part idled out (alplabai/alp-sdk#2517)

`optiga_trust_m_init_with_reset()` recovered a Trust M that went silent before
the init probe, but `read_product_info()` and `send_apdu()` on a context that
idled after init still failed with the part NACKing.

Changes:
- `optiga_trust_m_init_with_reset()` now keeps the reset hook and its `reset_user`
  in the context. When opening a session fails with `ALP_ERR_IO`, the
  driver pulses RESET once through the hook (same timings as the init path)
  and opens again. Without a hook nothing changes.
- A timed-out library operation (open, close, read, transceive) is recorded
  and drained at the start of the next call; while it still runs the call
  returns `ALP_ERR_BUSY` and never pulses RESET. The read and length buffers
  the library writes now live in the context, not on the stack.
- A session that is already open is not retried: a reset would drop the
  caller's APDU state, which it must rebuild itself (`send_apdu` callers own
  `OpenApplication`).
- ABI: `optiga_trust_m_t` gains `reset`/`reset_user` (trailing) and
  `op_pending`, `xfer_len`, `uid` (before `op_status`); callers must rebuild. `docs/abi/v0.16-snapshot.json` is
  regenerated. The hook and `reset_user` must outlive the context.
- `tests/unit/optiga_trust_m_idle_reset` gains the mid-app cases (one pulse and
  a reopen with a hook, none without).

Status: verified with a host mock only; the on-silicon check on E1M-V2M103
(read_product_info after a >10 s idle succeeds with one `se-rst` pulse) is pending.
