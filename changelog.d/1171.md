### Added — V2N/V2M Linux DT: SSIU/I2S wiring reaches the TAS2563 codec pair (#1171)

`e1m-x-evk.dtsi` now carries a `rcar_sound` audio-graph playback path for
the carrier's two TAS2563 smart amps (U27 left, U28 right): two
`ti,tas2563` codec nodes on `&i2c0` (0x4D/0x4E), two independent
audio-graph ports (SSI1 -> left amp, SSI2 -> right amp, the R-Car "SSI
parallel mode" pairing that shares BCK/WS), and a `sound_card` joining
them. `CONFIG_SND_SOC_RCAR` and `CONFIG_SND_SOC_TAS2562` are both already
`y` in the vendored linux-renesas 6.1.141-cip43 build (confirmed against a
prior real bitbake `.config` for `e1m-v2m101-a55`, not just the mainline
defconfig), and `tas2563-audio.cfg`'s `CONFIG_SND_SOC_TAS2562=y` fragment
now has a DT consumer.

dtc-clean (zero new warnings vs. baseline) for both `e1m-v2n101-x-evk` and
`e1m-v2m101-x-evk` against the vendored linux-renesas source tree, with
every phandle in the new nodes resolved and hand-checked against the
decompiled `.dtb`. Not bench-verified -- no audible output claimed.

Two things are flagged, not silently fixed, and block a real board flash:

- The new `ssi1_pins`/`ssi2_pins` pinmux (port 4, pins 4-7) collides with
  the existing `i2c6_pins`/`i2c7_pins`, which already claim the same four
  physical pins and are both `status = "okay"`. Neither i2c6 nor i2c7 is
  in `metadata/boards/e1m-x-evk.yaml`'s `carrier: buses:` list or in
  `renesas-peripheral-map.tsv`, while SSIU1/SSIU2 on those same pins are
  -- needs a schematic-backed call before this ships.
- Amp `shutdown-gpios` / `interrupts` (shared `\SD_N` / `IRQ_N`) and
  IV-sense capture are not wired -- playback-path DT only.

The CM33/Zephyr half of #1171 is out of scope: no SSI/SSIU driver exists
in this tree, `hal_renesas`, or upstream.
