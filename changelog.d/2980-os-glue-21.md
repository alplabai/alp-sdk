### Fixed — zephyr: ADC stream rollback/gap, supervisor ATTN IRQ vs SWD, safe EEPROM banner bitrate (#2980)

- adc: a BEGIN2 reply with full_scale 0 rolled back with an unchecked
  STREAM_END and freed the slot even when the GD32 stream stayed live; it
  now goes through bridge_stream_end_and_free. spectrum read_bins drops the
  partial FFT block when the stream read reports a discontinuity (BUSY).
- v2n-supervisor: try_init_locked re-checks swd_session after attn_setup()
  and disarms the P71 IRQ, and disarms it on a failed handshake, so a
  racing SWD session cannot leave the rising-edge IRQ armed.
- hw-info: the boot banner EEPROM read defaults to 100 kHz (was 400 kHz)
  because alp_i2c_open retunes the shared controller and never restores it.
