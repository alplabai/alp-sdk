### Fixed — `alp_gpu2d_format_t` no longer collides numerically with `alp_pixfmt_t` (#1647)

`alp_gpu2d_format_t` (`include/alp/gpu2d.h`) and `alp_pixfmt_t`
(`include/alp/peripheral.h`) are two public pixel-format enums that share three
member names -- `RGB565`, `RGB888`, `ARGB8888` -- but disagreed on two of their
numeric values: `ARGB8888` was 0 in one and 3 in the other, `RGB888` was 3 in one
and 2 in the other. A caller wiring `alp_display_get_caps()`'s advertised
`alp_pixfmt_t` straight into an `alp_gpu2d_surface_t.format` got a silently
relabelled format -- garbled rendering, not a crash, since every wrong value was
still in range.

`alp_gpu2d_format_t`'s three shared members are renumbered to agree with
`alp_pixfmt_t`. `A8` and `RGBA8888` have no `alp_pixfmt_t` counterpart, so they
take the remaining values in the (still contiguous 0..4) enum.

`gpu2d.h` is `[ABI-EXPERIMENTAL]` and has no active customers, so this is a plain
value renumber, not a symbol removal -- no `docs/abi/removed-symbols.json` entry
applies. `scripts/abi_snapshot.py --diff` reports it as `CHANGED`, which is a
pass-with-warning on an experimental header.
