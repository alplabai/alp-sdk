### Fixed — `cross-platform-setup.md` and `firmware-quickstart.md` still claimed tan-cli's deleted `crates/` tree "remains" the frozen v0.4.1 oracle (#1510)

tan-cli deleted `crates/`, `Cargo.toml`, `Cargo.lock`, and the oracle-parity suite on 2026-08-10
(`2883cdf4`, shipped in `v0.6.0-rc1`) — five days before the alp-sdk `v0.16.0-rc1` tag. Both
setup guides' "install `tan` from source" sections ended with a present-tense sentence claiming
the tree "remains" available, sending a reader who clones `tan-cli`'s `dev` branch straight into
a directory that no longer exists. Deleted both stale sentences; left the "(current release:
v0.5.1)" parentheticals and the dated ADR amendment blocks untouched, since those are correct.
