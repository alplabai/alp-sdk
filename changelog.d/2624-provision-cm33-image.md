### Added — provisioning writes and checks a CM33 image from the bundle (#2624)

- **`cm33` bundle role and `write_cm33` step.** The optional `cm33` component (`flash_target` `xspi:mtd1`) is the stored, padded image: `0x3000` zero bytes plus `zephyr.bin`, at most `0x30000` bytes. `write_cm33` runs after `write_xspi`, writes it at `mtd1` offset `0x1A0000` and md5-reads it back; skipped when the bundle has none. `preflight` and `check_som_bundle.py` refuse a non-zero pad, an initial SP outside SRAM0 (`0x08xxxxxx`), a reset vector without the Thumb bit or outside `0x08003000..0x08033000`, or an oversize image. `mtd_write_verify` takes an `offset`. With a `cm33` in the bundle, `cm33_firmware` blocks on an md5 mismatch. Not yet benched; which image ships is undecided, so `cm33` is not required yet.
