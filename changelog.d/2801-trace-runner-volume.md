### Added — the trace-runner's game sound has an adjustable volume: EVK rotary encoder, push switch, and an SWD request word (#2801)

With `TR_HP_SOUND=ON` the volume is one tagged word in the shared SRAM0 page (`TR_MEM_VOL`, `0x0237FD80`, `src/ipc/tr_vol.h`; a cold-SRAM value reads as the default, 100 %), 0..100 % of the build's `TR_SND_VOLUME` ceiling. The HP scales every 16 ms block of mixed samples by it (a linear ramp across the block, so a step is never a click) and never touches I2C2 or SD_N: 0 is silence with the amps running, and the I2C2 lease rules are unchanged. The HE owns the word and sets it from the EVK's rotary encoder through `alp_qenc_*` (`EVK_ENC_ROTARY`, 5 % a detent), its push switch through `alp_gpio_*` (`EVK_PIN_ENCODER_SW`, mute and back), and a request word a bench writes over SWD (`0x0237FD84`, `0x564F0000 | percent`, validated, refused values counted); `a32/release/FLASH-RECIPE.md` has the recipe. The HUD shows `VOL n%` (or `MUTE`) for 1.5 s over its bottom row on every screen. The encoder pads are GPIO3 / GPIO4, clear of the HP's GPIO5 and lpgpio lines. Host-tested (`tests/host/test_vol.c`, `test_hud.c`); the encoder and switch themselves are bench-unverified.
