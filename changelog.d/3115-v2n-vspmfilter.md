### Added — ISU scaler/converter in the V2N multimedia package group

**`packagegroup-alp-multimedia` now also installs `gstreamer1.0-plugin-vspmfilter` and its VSPM/MMNGR kernel and user modules on V2N machines with `meta-rz-codecs`.** `docs/v2n-multimedia.md` documents the camera-to-encoder pipelines and what is verified; the ISU does not demosaic Bayer.
