### Fixed — OV5647 auto-exposure never held: the ISP's compiled-in ceiling was 3x looser than a 30 fps frame, and the sensor's own on-chip AEC/AGC overrode the ISP (#2277)

**Two independent causes, both fixed.** The ISP's compiled-in AE ceiling
stayed pinned at the sensor's 10 fps VTS (3145 lines) regardless of the
app's requested rate — nearly 3x looser than a 1049-line 30 fps frame —
while the sensor's own on-chip AEC/AGC (`0x3503`) went inactive
somewhere between the ISP's manual-mode gate and streaming, so its
auto-exposure silently overrode every write the ISP made.

**Fixed by moving the ceiling into the sensor driver, where the VTS
facts already live, and re-asserting manual AEC/AGC as the last write
before streaming:** `ov5647_set_ctrl_exposure()` (`ov5647.c`) now clamps
every `VIDEO_CID_EXPOSURE` write to the active mode's VTS - 4 lines, and
`ov5647_set_stream(true)` re-asserts both AEC/AGC-manual bits (`0x3503`)
immediately before `MODE_SELECT_STREAMING`.

Bench run 251 (E1M-AEN803 2026W36-0001, OV5647, 640x480 requesting
30 fps): `0x3503` reads `0x07` (AEC/AGC/VTS manual) at stream start and
on every ~1 s poll during streaming; sensor exposure holds at 1045
lines (VTS 1049 - 4), ISP frame rate 30.00 fps.
